## TOOL 7: The Prompt Iteration Log

**Purpose:** Track every change you make to your prompts over time. This becomes your institutional knowledge—the record of what works, what doesn't, and why. After six months, this log is more valuable than any generic "prompt engineering" article.

**Time needed:** 1 minute per iteration to log.
