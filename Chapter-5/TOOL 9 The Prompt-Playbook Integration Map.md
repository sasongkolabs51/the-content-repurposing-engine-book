## TOOL 9: The Prompt-Playbook Integration Map

**Purpose:** Embed your prompts directly into your Platform-Format Playbook so that format rules and AI commands live side-by-side. One document to open when you're ready to create.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes to set up.

---

### The Integration Structure

In your Platform-Format Playbook (Chapter 3, Tool 4), each platform section should now have TWO parts:

**Part A: The Grammar (what you already have)**
- Hook patterns
- Structural template
- Cadence rules
- Visual rules
- Tone notes
- AI Instruction Block (the short version)

**Part B: The Full Prompt (new — paste from Tool 2)**
- The complete Voice-Preserving Prompt template
- With all five components filled in
- Ready to copy-paste into your AI tool

---

---

### The Workflow (After Integration)

When you're ready to create a LinkedIn carousel:

1. Open your Platform-Format Playbook → LinkedIn section.
2. Scroll to Part B: The Full Prompt.
3. Copy the prompt.
4. Open your Hub for the current pillar piece.
5. Paste the Hub content into the Constraint section of the prompt.
6. Tweak the Creative Intent (one sentence).
7. Paste the full prompt into your AI tool.
8. Generate. Score. Edit. Publish.

**Total prompt setup time:** Under 2 minutes.
