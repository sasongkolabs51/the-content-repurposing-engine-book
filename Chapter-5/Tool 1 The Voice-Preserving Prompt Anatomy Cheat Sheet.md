## Tool 1: The Voice-Preserving Prompt Anatomy Cheat Sheet

**Purpose:** A one-page reference for the five essential components of a prompt that actually sounds like you. Pin it above your workspace or keep it inside your prompt library document until the structure becomes automatic.

### The Five Components

| Component | What It Does | Example Snippet |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **1. Role** | Orients the AI’s persona and expertise level. | “You are a sharp, informal content strategist who writes in short, punchy bursts and never uses corporate jargon.” |
| **2. Tone & Voice Constraints** | Encodes your Voice Fingerprint as specific, teachable rules. | “Open with a bold, declarative, counterintuitive statement under 140 characters. Use contractions. End each tweet with a pattern interrupt. Never use ‘leverage’ or ‘utilize’.” |
| **3. Structural Rules** | The platform-native grammar from your Playbook (hook patterns, line breaks, length, visual cues). | “7–9 tweets. First tweet is a standalone hook. Each subsequent tweet is 1–2 sentences. Insert line breaks between every tweet. Final tweet is a reflective question.” |
| **4. Reference Example** | A high-performing post of yours pasted in. Gives the AI a concrete stylistic target. | “Model the rhythm and hook style of this tweet: [paste example]” |
| **5. Constraint** | The specific Hub blocks to draw from, and rules about what *not* to do. | “Use only the Core Thesis, the second Key Story, and the Quotable Lines from the Hub below. Do not invent data. Do not add external research.” |

**Golden Rule:** If any of these five components is missing or vague, the AI fills the gap with its statistical average—which is why you get “In today’s fast-paced digital landscape…”
