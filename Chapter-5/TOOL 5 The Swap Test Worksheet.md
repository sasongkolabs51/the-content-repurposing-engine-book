## TOOL 5: The Swap Test Worksheet

**Purpose:** Verify that your prompts are robust and content-agnostic. A prompt that only works for one specific Hub topic is too tightly coupled. The Swap Test proves your constraints are general enough to handle any pillar content.

**Time needed:** 15 minutes

---

### If Scores Dropped

**Diagnosis:** Your constraints were probably too topic-specific.

**Common culprits:**

☐ You wrote "Open with a statement about decision fatigue" instead of "Open with a bold, counterintuitive statement drawn from the Core Thesis"
☐ You referenced a specific story by name in the Structural Rules instead of in the Constraint
☐ Your Creative Intent was topic-locked ("Make the reader feel seen in their decision fatigue") instead of general ("Make the reader feel seen in the specific struggle named in the Core Thesis")

**The fix:** Go through each prompt and replace any topic-specific language with general structural language.

| Topic-Specific (BAD) | General (GOOD) |
|---|---|
| "Open with a statement about decision fatigue" | "Open with a bold, counterintuitive statement drawn from the Core Thesis" |
| "Use the judge parole story" | "Use the story specified in the Constraint block" |
| "Make the reader feel less alone in their productivity struggle" | "Make the reader feel seen in the specific struggle named in the Creative Intent" |
| "Close with a question about morning routines" | "Close with a reflective question that echoes the Core Thesis" |
