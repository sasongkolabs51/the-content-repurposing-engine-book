**Instructions:** Grab your Voice Fingerprint document. Use the prompts below to compress it. Aim for a block of 5–8 sentences that captures your hook style, rhythm, vocabulary, argument structure, and emotional register.

Structure arguments as [your pattern, e.g., “problem → insight → action”]. Emotional register: [e.g., “70% authoritative, 30% vulnerable self-disclosure”].
