## TOOL 1: The Voice-Preserving Prompt Anatomy Worksheet

**Purpose:** Deconstruct the five-component prompt architecture and build your understanding by filling in each layer for your own content. This is the "learn the scales before you play the song" exercise.

**Time needed:** 25–30 minutes

**What you need:** Your Voice Fingerprint (Chapter 2), your Platform Playbook (Chapter 3), one populated Hub (Chapter 4), and one high-performing post of yours to use as a Reference Example.

---

### The Five Components (Quick Reference)

| # | Component | What It Does | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Role** | Orients the AI's persona, expertise, and default tone | You write it |
| 2 | **Tone & Voice Constraints** | Encodes your Voice Fingerprint as mechanical rules | Chapter 2 Fingerprint |
| 3 | **Structural Rules** | Specifies the platform's native grammar and format | Chapter 3 Playbook |
| 4 | **Reference Example** | Gives the AI a concrete stylistic target to emulate | Your best past post |
| 5 | **Constraint** | Defines what material to use (Hub blocks) and what NOT to do | Chapter 4 Hub |

---

### Component 1: Role

**The prompt to yourself:** *If I were hiring a human assistant to write this specific platform asset, what three adjectives would describe the person I'd want? What's their expertise? What's their default energy?*

**My Role statement for Twitter:**

"You are _______________________________________________________________
_______________________________________________________________"

*(Example: "You are a sharp, slightly combative content strategist who writes in short, punchy bursts. You sound like a friend who's had two coffees and needs to tell you something important. You never use corporate jargon.")*

**My Role statement for LinkedIn:**

"You are _______________________________________________________________
_______________________________________________________________"

*(Example: "You are a warm, professionally candid editorial assistant who writes LinkedIn carousels that feel like a colleague sharing a hard-won lesson over coffee. You blend insight with one moment of vulnerability per post.")*

**My Role statement for Newsletter:**

"You are _______________________________________________________________
_______________________________________________________________"

*(Example: "You are my newsletter cowriter. You write in my exact voice—a letter to one smart friend at 11pm. You can be uncertain, tangential, and honest. You never sound like a broadcast.")*

### Component 3: Structural Rules

**The prompt to yourself:** *What are the platform-specific format rules from my Playbook? State them as explicit instructions the AI can follow mechanically.*

**For Twitter:**

```
STRUCTURAL RULES — TWITTER THREAD:
- Length: [X] tweets
- Tweet 1: [hook pattern]
- Tweets 2–[n-1]: [tension arc rules]
- Final tweet: [close pattern]
- Line breaks: [rule]
- Sentence count per tweet: [rule]
- No hashtags. No "Thread 🧵". No links mid-thread.
- [Any other platform-specific rules from your Playbook]
```

**For LinkedIn:**

```
STRUCTURAL RULES — LINKEDIN CAROUSEL:
- Slides: [X]
- Slide 1: [hook pattern]
- Slides 2–[n-1]: [narrative arc]
- Final slide: [close pattern]
- Per slide: bold headline (6–10 words) + max 2 sentences
- Include [VISUAL NOTE] per slide
- Tone: [platform-specific adjustment]
- [Any other rules]
```

**For Newsletter:**

```
STRUCTURAL RULES — NEWSLETTER:
- Length: [X] words
- Subject line: [pattern]
- Opening: [scene-setting rule]
- Body: [expansion rules]
- Close: [pattern]
- Tone: [most conversational platform]
- [Any other rules]
```

### Component 5: Constraint

**The prompt to yourself:** *Which specific Hub blocks should the AI pull from for this asset? What should it NOT do?*

**My Constraint Block (template):**

```
CONSTRAINT:
- Use ONLY these Hub blocks: [list specific blocks]
- Core Thesis: [paste]
- Story to feature: [paste specific story]
- Data point to include: [paste]
- Objection to address: [paste]
- Quotable line to weave in: [paste]
- Do NOT invent any ideas, data, or stories not in the Hub.
- Do NOT summarize. REARCHITECT.
- If a needed element is missing, leave a [PLACEHOLDER] note.
```

---

### Assembly Check

Now look at all five components together. Do you have:

☐ A Role that orients tone and expertise
☐ Voice Constraints with at least 6 mechanical rules
☐ Structural Rules specific to the target platform
☐ A Reference Example from your own best work
☐ A Constraint that specifies exact Hub blocks and prohibitions

**If any component is missing or vague, the output will drift.** Fill it in before you generate.
