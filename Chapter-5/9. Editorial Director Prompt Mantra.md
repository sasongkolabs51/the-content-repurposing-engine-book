## 9. Editorial Director Prompt Mantra

Use this before any prompt-building or editing session. It locks in the shift from “I hope this works” to “I know how to instruct my team.”

> I don’t write prompts—I write creative briefs.  
> Every brief contains a role, voice constraints, format rules, a reference, and a clear constraint.  
> When output fails, I don’t blame the tool. I find the missing constraint and add it.  
> My Voice Fingerprint is a precision spec, not a vague vibe.  
> My prompts get better every time I use them because I test, score, and refine.  
> I am an editorial director. I instruct; my AI executes. And together, we ship work that sounds unmistakably like me.
