## 3. Fill-in Prompt Templates for Your Core Platforms

These are starter templates built on the five-component anatomy. Fill in the bracketed sections with your own Voice Constraints, Platform Playbook rules, and a link to your Hub. Once filled, save each as a template you can reuse by swapping only the Hub content.

### 3.1 Twitter/X Thread Prompt Template

```
You are [Role: e.g., “an assistant who writes in my exact voice, specializing in high-tension Twitter threads.”]

[Paste your condensed Voice Constraints block here]

STRUCTURAL RULES FOR THIS THREAD:
- Length: [e.g., 7–9 tweets]
- Hook: first tweet must be [e.g., “a bold, declarative, counterintuitive statement under 140 characters”]
- Each subsequent tweet: [e.g., “1–2 sentences, ends with a pattern interrupt or implied question”]
- Line breaks: insert empty lines between every tweet
- Final tweet: [e.g., “a reflective question; never a link”]
- No hashtags. No emojis beyond an occasional period or dash.

REFERENCE EXAMPLE: Below is a high-performing thread of mine. Match its rhythm, hook intensity, and voice.
[Paste your best thread]

CONSTRAINT: Use ONLY the following blocks from my Hub:
- Core Thesis
- Quotable Lines
- Audience Objections
Do not invent any data, stories, or external references. If the Hub lacks a needed element, leave a placeholder.

Hub:
[Paste your Hub content for those blocks]
```

### 3.3 Newsletter Expansion Prompt Template

```
You are [Role: e.g., “my newsletter co-writer, tasked with expanding a pillar idea into a 500–700 word issue in my exact voice.”]

[Paste your condensed Voice Constraints block here]

- Subject line: [e.g., “a personal curiosity gap, under 60 characters”]
- Opening: start with a personal anecdote or recent realization that leads into the topic
- Body: expand the Core Thesis with fresh commentary, one data point, and one counterargument addressed
- Tone: conversational, like a letter to a single friend
- Closing: a reflective question and a gentle call to action (reply, forward, etc.)
- Word count: [e.g., 500–700 words]

[Paste a favorite issue you’ve written]
```
