## TOOL 3: The Voice Fingerprint Condensation Template

**Purpose:** Create the dense, paste-ready "system prompt" block that goes into every single AI interaction. This is your Voice Fingerprint compressed into a format the AI can follow mechanically.

**Time needed:** 15 minutes (first time). 5 minutes (monthly updates).

---

### The Condensation Formula

Take your full Voice Fingerprint (Chapter 2, Tool 2) and compress it into a single paragraph using this structure:

```
VOICE CONSTRAINTS — [Your Name / Brand]:
Write in the voice of [2–3 adjectives that describe your overall
energy]. Open with [your hook pattern as a rule]. Use [sentence
rhythm rule]. Vocabulary: always use [top 3 phrases]; never use
[top 3 anti-vocabulary words]. Structure arguments as [your
argument skeleton]. Emotional register: [X]% [tone A], [X]%
[tone B]. Close with [your closing pattern]. Contractions:
[rule]. Address the reader as [rule]. Additional rule: [the
single most distinctive micro-pattern you discovered, e.g.,
"Use two-word fragments followed by a period for emphasis:
'Exactly this.' 'Almost. Almost.'"].
```

### The "Can an AI Follow This?" Test

Paste your condensed block into an AI tool and add:

```
Based on the voice constraints above, write a 3-sentence paragraph
about [any random topic, e.g., "why I switched from coffee to tea"].
Follow every rule mechanically.
```

**Read the output. Check:**

☐ Does the opening match my hook pattern?
☐ Is the sentence rhythm right?
☐ Are my "use" words present?
☐ Are my "avoid" words absent?
☐ Does the emotional register feel right?
☐ Would I recognize this as "mine" in a blind test?

**If any check fails:** The corresponding rule in your condensed block is too vague. Make it more specific. Add a "Do this / Don't do this" example.
