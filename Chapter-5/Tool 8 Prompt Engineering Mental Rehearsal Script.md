## Tool 8: Prompt Engineering Mental Rehearsal Script

**Purpose:** To replace the old feeling of dread when you open a chat window with the calm confidence of a director handing a precise creative brief to a skilled executor. Read aloud or record.

---

*I am not guessing. I am not hoping the AI “gets” me. I am handing it a precise creative brief, just as I would to a human collaborator.*

*My prompt has five components: Role, Voice Constraints, Structural Rules, Reference Example, and Constraint. Together, they leave no room for the bland statistical average. The AI is boxed into my voice, my platform grammar, and my content. It cannot drift because I haven’t given it space to drift.*

*When an output disappoints me, I don’t blame the tool or rewrite it manually out of frustration. I become a detective: which constraint was missing? Was my hook rule too vague? Did I forget to ban corporate phrases? I add the missing rule, regenerate, and the output tightens. Every failure is just feedback I can use to improve my prompts.*

*My prompt library is a living asset. It gets sharper with every iteration. Today, I will test, score, and refine until my templates produce drafts that I can edit in minutes, not hours. Because I am not a writer struggling with AI. I am an editorial director who has finally learned to conduct the machine.*

*Let’s build my command prompts.*
