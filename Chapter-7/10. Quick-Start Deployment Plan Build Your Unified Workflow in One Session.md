## 10. Quick-Start Deployment Plan: Build Your Unified Workflow in One Session

Set aside 90 minutes. You’ll go from fragmented to unified.

- [ ] **Minute 0–10:** Complete the Tool Stack Diagnostic (Section 1). Circle your top three handoff hotspots.  
- [ ] **Minute 10–15:** Open your Hub template and add the four Spoke sections from the Hub-as-Dashboard Extension (Section 2). Save as the new master.  
- [ ] **Minute 15–30:** Copy your three core prompt templates (Twitter, LinkedIn, Newsletter). Paste the appropriate Scheduler-Ready Output Format extension (Section 3) at the end of each. Customize with your specific scheduling tool’s requirements (use the Cheat Sheet in Section 4).  
- [ ] **Minute 30–50:** Run a test sprint with a real Hub using these updated prompts. Triage and paste the finals into the Hub’s Spoke sections.  
- [ ] **Minute 50–65:** Follow the End-to-End Workflow Test Checklist (Section 5). Paste each Spoke into your scheduler and check formatting. Adjust prompt output instructions immediately if something is off.  
- [ ] **Minute 65–75:** Create your visual Workflow Diagram (Section 7). Make it clean enough to reference during sprints.  
- [ ] **Minute 75–80:** Schedule your first Monthly Workflow Audit (Section 6) in your calendar—set it to repeat.  
- [ ] **Minute 80–90:** Read the Unified Workflow Identity Anchor (Section 9) aloud. Breathe. You now have a single pipeline, not a tool graveyard.
