## TOOL 10: The 30-Day Implementation Roadmap (Chapter 7)

**Purpose:** Build your unified workflow over four weeks. From messy tool stack to clean pipeline.

---

### Week 1: Diagnose and Design

- [ ] Complete the **Tool Stack Diagnostic** (Tool 1). Map your current workflow. Identify top 3 friction points.
- [ ] Sketch your **Ideal Flow** (Tool 1, Step 5). Define your target architecture.
- [ ] Add the **Hub-as-Dashboard Spoke sections** (Tool 2) to your Hub template.
- [ ] Choose your scheduling tool(s). Confirm they support your platforms.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~45 minutes

### Week 2: Build the Scheduler-Ready Prompts

- [ ] Add **Output Format blocks** (Tool 3) to each of your prompt templates. Match your specific scheduler.
- [ ] **Test each Output Format** against your scheduler. Paste. Check. Adjust. Re-test.
- [ ] Run the **End-to-End Test Protocol** (Tool 5) with a real Hub. Time the full pipeline.
- [ ] Fix any friction points found during the test. Update prompts.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes

### Week 3: Draw and Lock In

- [ ] Create your **Visual Workflow Diagram** (Tool 4). Fill in your specific tools and times.
- [ ] Post it near your workspace. (Print it. Tape it. Make it visible.)
- [ ] Run a **full sprint using the unified pipeline.** Hub → Prompts → Triage in Hub → Paste to Hub Spokes → Copy to Scheduler. No extra tools.
- [ ] Fill in the **Handoff Elimination Tracker** (Tool 8). Log what you've eliminated.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~90 minutes (including the sprint)

### Week 4: Maintain and Optimize

- [ ] Run your **second full unified sprint.** Should be smoother than Week 3.
- [ ] Do the first **Monthly Workflow Audit** (Tool 6). Even though it's only been a month. Establish the habit.
- [ ] Apply the **Tool Creep Prevention Rules** (Tool 7) to any new tools that tempted you this month.
- [ ] Update the **Platform-Specific Formatting Quick Reference** (Tool 9) with any quirks you discovered.
- [ ] Review your **Handoff Elimination Tracker.** Calculate total time saved.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes
