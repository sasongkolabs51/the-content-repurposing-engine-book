## 4. Scheduler-Ready Output Format Cheat Sheet

A quick reference for the most common scheduling tools. Fill in your chosen format and keep it taped near your sprint workspace.

| Tool | Twitter/X Thread | LinkedIn Carousel / Post | TikTok / Reels | Newsletter |
|------|------------------|--------------------------|----------------|------------|
| **Buffer** | Plain text, one tweet per line, blank line between tweets. Or .txt file with line breaks only. | Plain text caption for carousel; slides uploaded separately. Use [Slide X] notation in notes. | N/A (post natively) | N/A |
| **Later** | Thread as separate posts with numbering in caption; or bulk upload. | Caption as plain text; carousel slides uploaded manually. Use [Slide X] notation for reference. | N/A | N/A |
| **Hootsuite** | CSV with Date, Time, Message columns for bulk import. | Single post caption with carousel. For slides, upload PDF and write caption. | N/A | N/A |
| **Native Platform** | Paste and schedule within platform's composer. | N/A (carousel creation native, but prompt gives you slide text to copy into each slide). | Script used separately; video uploaded natively. | Paste into your email platform's editor. |

When adding a new tool, update this cheat sheet and adjust your prompt's Output Format accordingly.
