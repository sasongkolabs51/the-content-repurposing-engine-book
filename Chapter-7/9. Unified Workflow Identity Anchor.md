## 9. Unified Workflow Identity Anchor

Use this before you open any tool after a sprint. It re-centers you on the system, not the scattered apps.

> My Hub is the command center—not a folder of drafts, not six open tabs.  
> Every spoke lives in my Hub. Every final version lives there.  
> My prompts don’t just write for platforms; they write for my scheduler.  
> When I paste into Buffer or Later, it works the first time.  
> If it doesn’t, I fix the prompt once and it’s fixed forever.  
> I am an editorial director who designed the pipeline. I don’t fight the pipeline.  
> Today, I move from sprint to published with the ease of a system that works *for* me.
