**Purpose:** A printable challenge card for when you need to prove the translation principle to yourself (or to a collaborator who's skeptical). Also useful as a team exercise if you have a content assistant.

### The Test

Read all four versions aloud, one after another. Do they sound like four different *performances* of the same idea? Or four photocopies at different sizes?

☐ Four distinct performances. The translation worked.
☐ They're similar. I'm still summarizing. Need more structural divergence.
☐ Honestly, they're basically the same text with different line breaks. Back to the drawing board.
