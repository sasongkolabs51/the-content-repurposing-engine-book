## 7. Platform-Specific Post Anatomy Cheat Sheet

Keep this one-pager visible until the grammars become second nature. It’s a condensed reference you can glance at before writing or prompting.

| Element | Twitter/X Thread | LinkedIn Carousel | TikTok/Reels | Newsletter |
|---------|------------------|-------------------|--------------|------------|
| **Hook** | Bold claim or open loop, under 140 chars | Provocative question or professional struggle on title slide | On-screen text + spoken hook in 1.5s, often contradictory | Personal moment or curiosity gap in subject line + first sentence |
| **Body rhythm** | Short tweets, each ending on a tension point; line breaks for breathing room | 1 idea per slide; headline + one-sentence support; logical progression | Visual change every 5-7 seconds; short spoken lines; text overlays | Narrative paragraphs with fresh commentary and anecdotes |
| **Personal element** | Sprinkled through as voice, rarely deep stories | A vulnerability beat (usually one slide) | High energy, direct-to-camera intimacy | Central; often the opening and a story in the body |
| **Closing** | Reflective question or loop back to hook | Engagement question (“What’s your take?”) | Loopable twist, challenge, or CTA to profile | Reply invitation or gentle nudge to next step |
| **Biggest don’t** | Don’t write a blog post sliced into tweets | Don’t create slides with walls of text | Don’t explain without visual changes | Don’t sound like a marketer; it’s a letter, not a pitch |
