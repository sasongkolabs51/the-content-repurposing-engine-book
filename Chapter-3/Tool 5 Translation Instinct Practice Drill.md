## Tool 5: Translation Instinct Practice Drill

**Purpose:** To install the rearchitecture reflex so deeply that when you look at a pillar insight, your brain automatically starts generating platform-native hooks—no AI required. This is the difference between someone who “repurposes” and a true editorial director.

**Instructions:** Take a single insight from your current content queue (or use the same one from Tool 1). Set a timer for 15 minutes. Without referencing any templates or notes, manually draft *three different openings* for three different platforms—no full posts, just the hook and the first structural beat.

### Insight: _______________

**Platform 1: Twitter/X**  
*Hook (first tweet):* _______________  
*Tweet 2 (the immediate tension builder):* _______________

**Platform 2: LinkedIn Carousel**  
*Slide 1 title and supporting line:* _______________  
*Slide 2 headline (the problem or story moment):* _______________

**Platform 3: TikTok / Short-Form Video**  
*Text overlay for the first 1.5 seconds:* _______________  
*First spoken line (after the visual hook):* _______________

**After 15 minutes, reflect:**
- Which platform came most naturally? Which felt like a struggle?
- Did you catch yourself defaulting to summary mode? If so, at what exact moment?
- What one rule from the grammar cards would have made the hardest one easier?

**Make it a habit:** Do this drill once a week with a fresh insight. It’s the creative equivalent of practicing scales—boring at first, transformative over time.
