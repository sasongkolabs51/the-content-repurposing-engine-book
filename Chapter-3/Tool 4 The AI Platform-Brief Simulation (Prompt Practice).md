## Tool 4: The AI Platform-Brief Simulation (Prompt Practice)

**Purpose:** To close the gap between “I now know the platform grammar” and “I can instruct an AI to use it.” This exercise builds the muscle of writing precise, constraint-rich prompts that make your Cocreator Pact operational.

**Instructions:** Using your Platform-Format Playbook (Tool 3) and your Voice Fingerprint (Chapter 2), write a full prompt as if you were about to generate a piece of content for a specific platform. You won’t actually run the AI yet—just practice constructing the instruction set.

### Step 1: Select your target platform and content.
- **Platform:** _______________
- **Core Insight or Hub snippet you’ll transform:** _______________

### Step 2: Draft the prompt using the template below.

---

**VOICE CONSTRAINTS**  
*[Paste your Voice Fingerprint summary from Chapter 2 here, or reference it as “see attached fingerprint.”]*

**PLATFORM-FORMAT RULES FOR [Platform Name]**  
*[Copy the relevant section from your Platform-Format Playbook. Be as specific as possible: hook style, structure length, line-break cadence, visual cues, tone notes.]*

**SOURCE MATERIAL**  
*[Paste the relevant excerpt from your Hub or a clear, structured summary of the insight, story, and data you want to transform.]*

**TASK**  
Transform the source material into a [platform name] post according to the voice and platform-format rules above. Specifically:
- [List 2–3 micro-instructions: e.g., “Open with a hook that challenges the productivity-hacker narrative,” “Use at least two line breaks per tweet,” “End with a reflective question that doesn’t mention my product.”]

---

### Step 3: Review your prompt and answer:
- Did I include a specific opening instruction? (Yes/No)
- Did I give a clear length or structure constraint? (Yes/No)
- Did I specify tone or banned phrases for this platform? (Yes/No)
- Would a person who’s never heard my voice be able to follow these rules and produce something that sounds like me? (Yes/No)
