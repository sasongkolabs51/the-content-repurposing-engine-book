## Tool 1: The Side-by-Side Translation Map

**Purpose:** To physically experience the difference between summarizing an idea and rearchitecting it for two different platforms. This map will become a reference point you can return to whenever the old distribution habit tries to creep back.

**Instructions:**  
Choose one sharp insight from a recent pillar piece of content—something you could state in one or two sentences. (Example: “Decision fatigue destroys more productivity than distraction ever does.”)  
Open a blank document and create two columns. On the left, manually draft a Twitter thread (5–7 tweets). On the right, draft a LinkedIn carousel script (5–7 slides). Work from the exact same insight; don’t look at any templates yet. As you draft, annotate your structural choices in the margins or below, noting *why* you made each decision.
