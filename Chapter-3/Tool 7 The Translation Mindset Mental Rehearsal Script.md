## Tool 7: The Translation Mindset Mental Rehearsal Script

**Purpose:** To anchor the shift from “I summarize for platforms” to “I translate ideas into native experiences.” Read before a repurposing session or record in your own voice.

---

*I am not chopping a video into smaller pieces. I am translating a core idea into distinct languages—each platform has its own grammar, its own expectations, its own audience rhythm.*

*Twitter wants a fast tension arc that rewards curiosity. LinkedIn wants a professional framework wrapped in a personal story. TikTok demands a visual pattern interrupt before the second mark. My newsletter is a living-room conversation with people who opted in for depth.*

*I know these grammars now. They’re not mysteries—they’re systems I’ve documented in my Playbook. I don’t have to guess. I don’t have to reinvent the wheel each time. I have rules, and those rules make me faster and more creative, not less.*

*When I sit down with my Hub, I’m not facing a wall of text to be reduced. I’m holding a rich idea to be orchestrated. I’ll pull the hook that fits Twitter. I’ll build the slide sequence that teaches on LinkedIn. I’ll script the visual-punch rhythm for short video. None of these are afterthoughts; they are editorial compositions.*

*Today, I will think like a translator, not a photocopier. The idea remains constant; the experience around it changes. That’s the craft. That’s the code. Let’s build.*
