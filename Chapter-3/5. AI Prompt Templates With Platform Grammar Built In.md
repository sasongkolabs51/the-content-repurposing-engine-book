## 5. AI Prompt Templates With Platform Grammar Built In

These prompts assume you already have your Voice Fingerprint (Chapter 2) and your Hub (Chapter 4). They incorporate your Platform-Format Playbook rules so that AI output lands not just in your voice, but in the native language of each channel.

```

[Paste your full voice fingerprint from Chapter 2]

- Thread length: [e.g., 7 tweets]
- Cadence: [e.g., single sentences, line break after each]
- Each tweet ends with: [e.g., a curiosity gap or a surprising stat]
- Final tweet: [e.g., reflective question, no link]

Hub components for this pillar:
- Core thesis: [paste]
- Quotable line: [paste]
- Story: [paste]
- Data point: [paste]

Write a Twitter thread from these inputs. Use only the provided Hub material. Follow both the voice fingerprint and the platform rules exactly.
```
