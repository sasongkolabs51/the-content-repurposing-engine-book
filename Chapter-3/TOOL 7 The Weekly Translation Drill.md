## TOOL 7: The Weekly Translation Drill

**Purpose:** A 15-minute weekly exercise to keep the translation muscle sharp. Like scales for a musician. You do it so that when the real performance comes (your actual content), the instinct is already warm.

**Time needed:** 15 minutes, once a week

**Schedule:** Sunday evening or Monday morning, before your actual content work.

---

### Week of: _______________

**The drill:** Take ONE sentence—a single insight, observation, or claim—and write it natively for three platforms. No Hub. No AI. Just you and the grammar.

*(Examples: "Most people confuse being busy with being effective." / "The best feedback I ever got made me want to quit." / "Your morning routine is a lie you tell yourself.")*
