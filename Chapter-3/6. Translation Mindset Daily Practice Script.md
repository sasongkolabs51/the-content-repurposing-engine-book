## 6. Translation Mindset Daily Practice Script

Use these before you begin any repurposing session to lock in the rearchitecture reflex. Say them aloud, or write the main line at the top of your blank document.

**Full version:**
> I’m not summarizing today; I’m translating.  
> Twitter is a tension engine. LinkedIn is a teaching library. TikTok is a visual carnival. My newsletter is a living room conversation.  
> I don’t chop up one piece into smaller pieces. I take one idea and stage it differently for each audience.  
> My Platform-Format Playbook is my grammar guide. My voice fingerprint is my style guide. My AI co-creator follows both.  
> I am an editorial director, and today I compose distinct experiences.

**Quick anchor (write at the top of your draft):**  
*“What would this idea look like if it were born on [platform]?”*
