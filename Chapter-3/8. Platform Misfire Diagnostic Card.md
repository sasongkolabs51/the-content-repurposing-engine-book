## 8. Platform Misfire Diagnostic Card

When a post underperforms, instead of blaming the algorithm, diagnose whether you violated a platform grammar rule.

| Symptom | Likely broken rule |
|---------|-------------------|
| Twitter thread has low read-through | Tweets don’t end with a pattern interrupt; reads like a chopped essay |
| LinkedIn carousel gets no comments | Final slide doesn’t ask a specific, reflective question; feels like a broadcast |
| TikTok video has high swipe-away in first 2 seconds | Hook doesn’t combine bold text with a spoken statement; or it’s too slow |
| Newsletter open rate drops | Subject line is generic or summarizes the topic instead of creating curiosity |
| Engagement is flat across platforms | You’re using the same post format everywhere; there’s no platform-native rearchitecture |

Tape this card to your monitor. Before publishing, ask: “Did I follow my Playbook for this platform?”
