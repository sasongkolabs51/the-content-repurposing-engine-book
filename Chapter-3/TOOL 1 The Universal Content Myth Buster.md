## TOOL 1: The "Universal Content" Myth Buster

**Purpose:** Expose, with your own data, the places where you've been treating platforms as identical buckets. Make the cost of the "just summarize it everywhere" approach viscerally clear.

**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**What you need:** Three recent pieces of content you posted on multiple platforms. Pull up the analytics if you can.

☐ Same structure, different formatting (add line breaks, add emojis)

☐ Actually changed the narrative structure for at least one platform
