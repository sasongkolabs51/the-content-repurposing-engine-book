## Tool 6: Platform Translation Checklist (Before You Hit Publish)

**Purpose:** A final-sweep checklist to use right before you publish or finalize any repurposed asset. It ensures you didn’t accidentally slip back into distribution mode.

**For Every Platform Asset, Ask Yourself:**

- [ ] **Hook:** Does this opening meet the specific hook standard for this platform? (If it’s Twitter, is it a self-contained curiosity bomb? If TikTok, would it stop someone mid-scroll?)
- [ ] **Structure:** Does the body follow the platform’s native narrative arc? (Tension thread? Slide-based teaching? Visual punch rhythm?)
- [ ] **Voice Integrity:** If you mute your name, does this still sound unmistakably like you? (Check against your Voice Fingerprint—specifically lexical phrases and emotional register.)
- [ ] **Platform Tonality:** Is the register appropriate for this space? (LinkedIn’s professional warmth, Twitter’s conversational speed, TikTok’s visual-first energy.)
- [ ] **Visual/Format Adherence:** Have you respected line-break cadences, text overlay placements, or image rules? Did you accidentally leave a generic link that kills reach?
- [ ] **Call to Action:** Does the close fit the platform’s unspoken rules? (Reflective question on LinkedIn, no link in a Twitter thread’s final tweet unless it’s organic, etc.)
- [ ] **No “Universal” Residue:** Read it out loud. If it could have been posted on any other platform with zero changes, flag it and rework.

**If any box remains unchecked, take 2 minutes to adjust before publishing.**
