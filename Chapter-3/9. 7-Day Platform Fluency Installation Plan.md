## 9. 7-Day Platform Fluency Installation Plan

**Day 1 – Audit Your Universal Habit**  
- Complete The Universal Content Illusion Audit (Section 1).  
- Identify the one platform where you default to summary most.  
- Write this platform at the top of your Playbook document as your priority.

**Day 2 – Deconstruct Your Own Best Posts**  
- Use the Platform Grammar Deconstruction Worksheet (Section 2) for two of your top-performing posts on different platforms.  
- Extract the distilled rules and add them to your Platform-Format Playbook (Section 4).

**Day 3 – Deconstruct an Inspiration Post**  
- Find one high-performing post from another creator on a platform you struggle with.  
- Fill out the Deconstruction Worksheet for it. Notice patterns you hadn’t seen before.  
- Add any new insights to your Playbook.

**Day 4 – Manual Translation**  
- Complete the Side-by-Side Translation Map (Section 3) with a fresh insight from your content.  
- Don’t rush. The goal is to feel the difference between building tension and building a teaching arc.

**Day 5 – Build Your Full Playbook**  
- Fill out the Platform-Format Playbook Builder (Section 4) for every platform you’re active on.  
- If you’re unsure about a rule, make your best guess—you’ll refine with data.

**Day 6 – Prompt Practice**  
- Choose one platform and use the corresponding AI Prompt Template (Section 5) with your Playbook rules and voice fingerprint.  
- Generate a draft. Compare it to your manual translation from Day 4. Where did the AI succeed? Where did it miss?  
- Tighten your Playbook rules based on what you observe.

**Day 7 – Full Orchestra Run**  
- Take one Hub (or a rough set of components from a pillar piece).  
- Use all four platform-specific prompts to generate drafts.  
- Spend 10 minutes editing each, applying the orchestrator approach from Chapter 2.  
- Reflect: What felt different about repurposing now that you have a grammar guide for each channel?

You now have the complete toolkit to make platform-native storytelling a reflex. With your Voice Fingerprint and this Platform-Format Playbook, you’ve turned the art of translation into a system you can hand to an AI—or a human collaborator—and get back content that feels like it was born on each channel. The final piece of the Reset is building the physical Hub that will feed all these prompts. That’s next.
