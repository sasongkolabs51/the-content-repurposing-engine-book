## TOOL 4: The Platform-Format Playbook (Full Template)

**Purpose:** Your living reference document. One section per platform. This is what you paste into AI prompts. This is what you hand a ghostwriter. This is the spec sheet that turns "write something for LinkedIn" into a precise creative brief.

**Time needed:** 60–90 minutes for the first draft (all platforms). 15 minutes per platform for monthly updates.

**Instructions:** Fill in each section based on (a) the Grammar Cards in Tool 2, (b) your own best-performing content, and (c) your Voice Fingerprint from Chapter 2. Where your natural voice and the platform grammar overlap, that's your sweet spot. Where they conflict, the platform grammar wins for structure, your voice wins for language.
