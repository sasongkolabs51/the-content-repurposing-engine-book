## TOOL 9: The 30-Day Implementation Roadmap (Chapter 3)

**Purpose:** Turn platform grammar knowledge into operational fluency over four weeks.

---

### Week 1: See the Gaps

- [ ] Complete the **"Universal Content" Myth Buster** (Tool 1). Audit 3 cross-platform posts.
- [ ] Print or screenshot the **Platform Grammar Cards** (Tool 2). Put them where you'll see them daily.
- [ ] Do the **Side-by-Side Translation Map** (Tool 3) for one insight. Twitter + LinkedIn.
- [ ] Annotate every structural decision. Make the invisible visible.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes across the week

### Week 2: Build the Playbook

- [ ] Draft your full **Platform-Format Playbook** (Tool 4). All platforms you actively manage.
- [ ] For each platform, pull 2–3 examples from your best past content to ground the rules.
- [ ] Write the **AI Instruction Block** for each platform (Section F of each platform in Tool 4).
- [ ] Start your **Platform Hook Library** (Tool 8). Log your top 5 hooks per platform from past content.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~90 minutes across the week

### Week 3: Test with AI

- [ ] Use the **AI Prompt Templates** (Tool 6) to generate one asset per platform from a single Hub.
- [ ] Run the **Translation Quality Checklist** (Tool 5) on each output.
- [ ] Use the **"Translation or Summary?" Audit Prompt** (Tool 6, Prompt E) on the weakest output.
- [ ] Revise the prompt based on the audit. Regenerate. Compare.
- [ ] Do the **Weekly Translation Drill** (Tool 7) on Sunday.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~2 hours across the week

### Week 4: Lock In the Instinct

- [ ] Run a full content cycle: Pillar → Hub → 3+ platform spokes, all using the Playbook + AI prompts.
- [ ] Do the **Weekly Translation Drill** (Tool 7) twice this week (different sentences).
- [ ] Update the **Hook Library** (Tool 8) with any new hooks you tested.
- [ ] Review the Playbook. Revise any rules that didn't produce the results you expected.
- [ ] Re-do the **"Universal Content" Myth Buster** (Tool 1) on this week's content. Compare to Week 1.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~2 hours across the week (but replacing 4+ hours of old-pattern work)

---

### The 30-Day Proof

At the end of Day 30:

1. Can I look at a Hub document and immediately see 3–4 distinct platform experiences radiating from it (without feeling like I'm "chopping it up")?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Getting there ☐ Not yet

   ☐ Yes ☐ Mostly ☐ Not yet

3. When I read my own cross-platform content, can I tell which pieces are translations and which are still summaries?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Sometimes ☐ Not yet

4. Has my "skip rate" for the hardest platform decreased?

   ☐ Yes ☐ A little ☐ No change

5. Can I write a 3-tweet Twitter version and a 7-slide LinkedIn version of the same idea in under 20 minutes (manually, without AI)?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Close ☐ Not yet

> *If you hit 4+ "yes" answers, the platform grammar is installed. You're no longer guessing. You're directing. Chapter 4 is where you build the physical Hub that feeds this entire machine.*
