## Tool 7: Complex Scenario Challenge Prompt Builder

**Purpose:** To practice adapting your advanced prompt architecture to emotionally charged or sensitive topics—where the wrong tone can alienate your audience instantly. Use this builder to craft a safe, authentic, and magnetic prompt.

**Scenario:** Imagine your next pillar piece is on burnout and mental health. The core message is about slowing down and redefining productivity, not about “optimizing rest.” Fill in the prompt template below.

### Complex Scenario Prompt Template

**ROLE:** You are a compassionate, emotionally intelligent editor writing in my voice. This topic requires warmth, vulnerability, and restraint—never prescriptive advice, never hustle-culture language.

**VOICE CONSTRAINTS:** [Paste your core Voice Fingerprint from Tool 2, Chapter 5, but add these scenario-specific modifications:]
- Avoid all productivity jargon: “optimize,” “hack,” “maximize,” “grind,” “scale.”
- Do not use framework-style numbering or slides that imply a “system” for mental health.
- Increase emotional register markers: short, reflective sentences; personal admissions; use of “I felt,” “I realized.”
- If presenting any takeaway, frame it as a personal experiment or a question, not a universal solution. E.g., “What I tried—and what you might explore if it resonates.”

**CONTENT TYPE:** Personal (forced). Do not switch to Analytical.

**HOOK ARCHITECTURE:** Use the Vulnerability Ledger (LinkedIn) or Specificity Bomb (Twitter) pattern. The hook must invite the reader into a shared emotional space, not promise a solution.

**NARRATIVE ARC:** Struggle → Dark moment or realization → Small turning point → Ongoing practice (not final victory) → Gentle invitation for the reader to reflect.

**ADDITIONAL CONSTRAINTS:**
- If the AI generates any phrase that sounds like “you should” or “here’s how to fix this,” replace it with an open question.
- End the post with a reflective question that doesn’t demand an answer, just offers company.
- No statistics about burnout percentages unless they are framed as validating, not alarming.

**REFERENCE EXAMPLE:** [Paste a sensitive, vulnerable post of yours or a creator you admire, with a note: “Model the emotional pacing and honesty, not the topic.”]

**HUB CONTENT:** [Insert a brief mock Hub: Core Thesis about hitting a wall, a personal story of exhaustion, a turning point that wasn’t dramatic, and a gentle reflective takeaway.]
