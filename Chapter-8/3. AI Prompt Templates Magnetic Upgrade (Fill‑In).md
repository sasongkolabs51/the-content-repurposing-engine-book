These are the fully upgraded templates. They include the conditional logic block (Section 4) and hook architecture references. Paste these over your old prompt templates, or create a new “Magnetic” version alongside them.

### 3.1 Magnetic Twitter Thread Prompt

```
You are my editorial assistant. Write in my voice and use the magnetic hook architecture specified below.

[PASTE YOUR CONDENSED VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK HERE]

=== CONTENT TYPE DETECTION (conditional) ===
Read the Core Thesis and Key Stories from the Hub. Classify the content as:
- ANALYTICAL (teaching a framework or system)
- PERSONAL (story of struggle, transformation, or failure)
- CONTRARIAN (challenging a widely held belief)

Then apply these rules:

ANALYTICAL → Use the Specificity Bomb or Insight‑First hook (never Confession Opener). Build a tension arc that leads from specific example to universal insight. Maintain confident, instructive tone.
PERSONAL → Use the Confession Opener or Specificity Bomb. Structure as narrative: struggle → insight → transformation → lesson. Add one moment of self‑doubt. Avoid numbering frameworks.
CONTRARIAN → Use the Bold Reframe or Contrarian Cliffhanger. Build a tension arc: challenged belief → evidence → reframed perspective. Tone should be provocative but inviting debate.

=== HOOK ARCHITECTURES (pick one based on content type) ===
- Bold Reframe: “Most [common belief]. The opposite is true.”
- Specificity Bomb: “I [did something oddly specific]. The result: [unexpected outcome].”
- Confession Opener: “I [admit a failure or misconception]. Here’s what I learned.”
- Contrarian Cliffhanger: “[Controversial statement]. At least, that’s the version we’ve heard.”

Do NOT open with a rhetorical question or “We’ve all…”

=== STRUCTURAL RULES ===
- 7–9 tweets
- First tweet: hook, under 140 chars
- Each subsequent tweet: 1–2 sentences, ends with a pattern interrupt or implied question
- Final tweet: reflective question, never a link
- Single blank line between tweets

### 3.3 Magnetic Newsletter Prompt

```
You are my editorial assistant. Write in my voice and use the magnetic hook architecture specified below.

[PASTE VOICE CONSTRAINTS]

=== CONTENT TYPE DETECTION ===
ANALYTICAL → Subject: Curiosity Gap or Counterintuitive Promise. Open with a personal moment that led to the framework.
PERSONAL → Subject: Personal Time‑Stamp. Open with the struggle moment. Arc: struggle → insight → universal takeaway → reader invitation.
CONTRARIAN → Subject: Controversial Promise. Open with the challenged belief and a promise to dismantle it.

=== SUBJECT LINE ARCHITECTURES ===
- Curiosity Gap: “The moment I stopped [X] (and got better results)”
- Personal Time‑Stamp: “Last [day], I [unexpected situation] and realized…”
- Controversial Promise: “Everything you’ve read about [topic] is making you [worse outcome]”

=== STRUCTURAL RULES ===
- Subject line under 60 chars
- Open with a personal anecdote or specific observation within 2 sentences
- Body expands the Core Thesis with fresh commentary, one data point, and one counterargument addressed
- Close with a reflective question and a gentle CTA
- Word count: 500–700
