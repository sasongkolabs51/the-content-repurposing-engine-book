## Tool 8: Magnetism Summary Card & Mental Rehearsal Script

### Summary Card: Magnetic Prompts in Five Lines

- **Replace vague “strong hook” with explicit architectures.** Bold Reframe, Specificity Bomb, Vulnerability Ledger—AI executes patterns, not prayers.
- **Teach AI to read the room.** Conditional logic (Analytical/Personal/Contrarian) matches hook, arc, and tone to content’s emotional core.
- **Let your best posts train your prompts.** Monthly exemplar loop feeds audience data back into the system; AI models what already worked.
- **Diagnose failure, don’t tolerate it.** Hook-context mismatch, fatigue, emotional flatline—each has a specific prompt fix.
- **Magnetism is a practice, not a setting.** A/B test hooks, rotate patterns, and treat every flop as a lesson your prompt can learn from.

### Mental Rehearsal Script

*I’m not just giving my AI voice and format rules. I’m giving it the psychology of attention—the specific hook architectures that make human brains unable to scroll past, the narrative arcs that hold them until the last word, the emotional intelligence to match tone to topic.*

*My prompts now have conditional logic. They know when my content is analytical, personal, or contrarian, and they choose the right hook and arc automatically. They’re not robots following one script; they’re context-aware editors adapting to the material.*

*My audience is my best prompt engineer. Every month, I’ll look at what resonated most and feed that intelligence back into the system. The AI isn’t guessing what works—it’s learning from my actual, live data. Over time, the gap between my best manual writing and my AI’s output becomes invisible.*

*When a post flops, I won’t despair. I’ll diagnose: was it a hook-context mismatch? Pattern fatigue? An emotional flatline? And I’ll add a precise fix to my prompt. Every failure makes the system sharper.*

*Today, I’m not just a creator using AI. I’m a student of attention, an engineer of magnetism, an editorial director who can command the scroll. Let’s build something unignorable.*
