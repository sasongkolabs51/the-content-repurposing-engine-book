## Tool 2: Conditional Prompting Integration Template

**Purpose:** To teach your AI to choose the right hook, narrative arc, and tone based on the emotional register and complexity of your Hub content. This eliminates mismatches between content tone and hook style.

**Instructions:** Copy the block below into each of your platform prompt templates. Fill in the branch details (hook, arc, tone) using your own voice and playbook. Then test with a Personal and an Analytical Hub to confirm differentiation.

### Content Type Detection Block (insert before platform-specific structural rules)

**CONTENT TYPE DETECTION:** Read the Core Thesis and Key Stories in the Hub. Categorize the content into one of three types:

1. **Analytical** – if the primary focus is teaching a framework, system, or process.
2. **Personal** – if the primary focus is a personal story of struggle, failure, or transformation.
3. **Contrarian** – if the primary focus is challenging a widely held belief or practice.

Then apply the corresponding creative direction:

**If Analytical:**
- Hook: Use the **Insight-First Headline** (LinkedIn) / **Bold Reframe** or **Specificity Bomb** (Twitter).
- Narrative Arc: Problem → Framework → Application → Reflective Takeaway.
- Tone: Confident, instructive, warm but authoritative. Use one personal anecdote as supporting evidence, not the emotional center.
- Voice adjustment: Slightly more formal transitions allowed; minimize self-disclosure to 10% of the post.

**If Personal:**
- Hook: Use the **Vulnerability Ledger** (LinkedIn) / **Specificity Bomb** (Twitter).
- Narrative Arc: Struggle → Insight → Transformation → Lesson for the reader.
- Tone: Warm, self-disclosing, but not self-indulgent. Prioritize emotional truth over polish.
- Voice adjustment: Increase emotional register markers; avoid framework-style numbering; use fragments that mimic internal thought.

**If Contrarian:**
- Hook: Use the **Contrarian Cliffhanger** (Twitter) / a provocative **Insight-First Headline** that challenges a norm (LinkedIn).
- Narrative Arc: State the challenged belief → Present evidence or story that undermines it → Offer the reframed perspective → Invite debate.
- Tone: Provocative but not aggressive; confident but open to dialogue. Never insult the reader.
- Voice adjustment: Shorter, punchier sentences; fewer qualifiers; use rhetorical questions sparingly if they serve the provocation.
