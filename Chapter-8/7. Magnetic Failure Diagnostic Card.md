## 7. Magnetic Failure Diagnostic Card

When a post underperforms despite your upgraded prompts, use this to pinpoint the issue.

| Symptom | Possible Cause | Fix |
|---------|----------------|-----|
| High click‑through, low engagement | Hook‑Context Mismatch: hook promised something the post didn’t deliver | Re‑assign content type; ensure hook matches the arc. If Personal content got a Contrarian hook, add a rule: “Personal content never uses Contrarian Cliffhanger.” |
| Gradual performance drop on a once‑strong hook | Fatigue Pattern: audience has seen this hook too many times | Rotate the hook. In the prompt, add: “Avoid [pattern name] for the next 4 posts. Use [alternative] instead.” Cycle your exemplars to include fresher references. |
| Decent likes, low shares/retweets | Emotional Flatline: post is useful but not evocative | Add an emotional peak instruction: “Insert a moment of self‑doubt or a surprising personal confession in slide 3 / tweet 4.” Adjust emotional register in Voice Constraints. |
| Post feels generic despite all the rules | Voice Constraints block over‑optimized for safety; not enough risk | Add: “Take one creative risk in this post—use a fragment, break a grammar rule, or say something that might mildly alienate a small subset of readers.” |
| AI still uses banned words | Lexical ban too soft | Move the ban to the very top of the prompt and make it absolute: “You are FORBIDDEN from using these words: [list]. If you use any, stop and regenerate.” |
