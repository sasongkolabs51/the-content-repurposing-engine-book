## Tool 3: The Exemplar Loop Monthly Ritual Kit

**Purpose:** To turn your own top-performing posts into a self-improving training set for your prompts, so your AI output gets sharper every month based on real audience data.

**Instructions:** Schedule a recurring 30-minute calendar block once a month. Use the templates below to review, diagnose, and update.
