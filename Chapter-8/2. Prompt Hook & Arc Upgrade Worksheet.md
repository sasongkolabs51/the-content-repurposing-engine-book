## 2. Prompt Hook & Arc Upgrade Worksheet

**Purpose:** Upgrade each core prompt template with explicit hook architectures, arc instructions, and “Do/Don’t” guardrails.

**Instructions:** Work platform by platform. For each, write in your chosen hook patterns, then define how the rest of the post should support that hook.

**Negative examples I forbid:**  
- Do not open with: “We’ve all…” “In today’s…” “It’s important to…”  
- Do not open with a rhetorical question.  
- Do not open with a statistic unless it’s genuinely shocking and immediately explained.

**Arc that follows the hook:**  
(e.g., “The thread must build tension tweet by tweet. Each tweet ends with a curiosity gap, a provocative question, or a pattern break. The final tweet is a reflective question—never a link.”)

**Emotional register check:**  
Is this content Analytical, Personal, or Contrarian? If I use a Confession Opener for Analytical content, it might mislead. I’ll use the conditional logic from Section 4 to assign hooks automatically.

### Platform: LinkedIn Carousel

**Old hook instruction:**  
____________________________________________________________

**New hook architectures:**  
1. __________________________ – Do example: __________________________  
2. __________________________ – Do example: __________________________

**Negative examples:**  
- Do not open the carousel with a generic “How to” as the only hook.  
- Do not use a hook that would fit better on Twitter; this is a professional teaching environment.

**Arc:**  
Slide 1: hook. Slides 2–5: one insight each, each with a personal story beat or a statistic. Slide 6: key takeaway. Slide 7: reflective question.

**Vulnerability beat:**  
Designate one slide where I will insert a personal failure, moment of doubt, or surprising admission.
