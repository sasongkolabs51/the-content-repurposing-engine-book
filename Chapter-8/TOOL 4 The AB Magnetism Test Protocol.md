## TOOL 4: The A/B Magnetism Test Protocol

**Purpose:** Test two hook architectures against live audience data. The winner becomes your primary pattern for that content type. This is how you move from "I think this hook is good" to "my audience proved this hook is good."

**Time needed:** 10 minutes to set up. 3–7 days to collect data. 5 minutes to analyze.
