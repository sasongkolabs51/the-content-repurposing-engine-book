## TOOL 7: The Resilience Protocol Card

**Purpose:** The pre-planned fallback for when life hits. Print it. Tape it next to your launchpad. When you miss a sprint, you don't spiral. You look at the card. You follow the protocol. The system survives.

**Time needed:** 5 minutes to create. 0 minutes to use (it's already decided).

---

### My Personal Resilience Triggers

| Situation | My Protocol |
|---|---|
| Client emergency eats my sprint day | Microsprint that evening or next morning. 15 min. Twitter only. |
| Family / health crisis | Intentional Pause. One post. Rest. No guilt. |
| Creative burnout / nothing feels right | Archive Restore. Resurface 2–3 evergreen posts. Take the week off from creation. |
| Travel / no reliable internet | Pre-schedule a week of content before leaving. Or: Archive Restore from phone. |
| "I just don't feel like it" (no external reason) | Microsprint. 15 min. Start the timer. Usually the resistance breaks at minute 3. |
| Missed 3+ weeks and feeling ashamed | Re-read this card. Forgive myself. Run ONE microsprint. That's the re-entry. |

---

### The Re-Entry Protocol (After a Pause)

When you come back after 2+ weeks away:

1. ☐ Open the launchpad. Don't open anything else.
2. ☐ Pick your highest-scoring Hub. (Don't overthink it.)
3. ☐ Run a **microsprint**. One platform. 15 minutes.
4. ☐ Schedule the output.
5. ☐ Feel the relief. The system still works. You still work.
6. ☐ Next week: run a full sprint. You're back.

**You don't need to "catch up." You don't need to produce a week's worth of content in one day. You just need to restart the engine. One microsprint. That's the whole re-entry.**
