## 4. Daily 5‑Minute Tune‑Up Checklist

**Purpose:** A tiny end‑of‑day ritual that catches issues early and keeps the system improving daily. Do it at the same time each day—e.g., right before you close your laptop.

- [ ] **Check today’s posts.** Open each platform where a repurposed post went live today. Scan for:
    - Formatting glitches (line breaks, broken links, missing images)
    - Any error in the content itself (wrong stat, off‑brand phrase)
- [ ] **Spot unusual responses.** Glance at comments/shares. Note any:
    - Spikes in engagement (positive or negative)
    - A comment that challenges, questions, or deepens the idea (this is gold for future Objections blocks)

**Total time:** 5 minutes. No more. If a big issue surfaces, park it for the Weekly Data Whisper or a dedicated prompt‑fix session. Don’t solve it now—just catch it.
