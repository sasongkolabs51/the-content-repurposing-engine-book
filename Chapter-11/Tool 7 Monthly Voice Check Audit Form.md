## Tool 7: Monthly Voice Check Audit Form

**Purpose:** To catch the slow creep of voice drift across a month of output, and apply a single reinforcing constraint to your Voice Fingerprint.

**Instructions:** Once a month, gather all repurposed posts from a single platform (e.g., all Twitter threads from the last 30 days). Read them in sequence, from oldest to newest. Fill in the audit form. It should take about 15 minutes.

### Voice Check Audit

**Month:** __________  
**Platform audited:** __________  
**Number of posts reviewed:** __________

**1. Hook Architecture Check:**
- Did all openings follow my specified hook pattern(s)? (Yes/No)
- If No, which post(s) drifted, and how? (e.g., “Week 3 thread started with a generic question instead of a bold statement.”)

**2. Lexical Consistency Check:**
- Are my signature phrases present throughout? (Yes/No)
- Are banned words (corporate jargon, formal phrasing) appearing more frequently? (List any violations.)
- Has sentence length or formality increased? (e.g., “Tweets averaged 22 words; my target is under 15.”)

**3. Emotional Register Check:**
- Do the posts from early in the month feel tonally consistent with those from late in the month? (Yes/No)
- Is there a subtle shift toward safer, more generic emotional expression? (Describe if yes.)

**4. Overall Voice Fidelity Score (1–5):**
*(1 = significant drift, sounds like a different person; 5 = indistinguishable from my manual writing)*

**Score:** _____

**If drift is detected (score below 4):**  
Identify the single most impactful reinforcing constraint to add to your Voice Fingerprint or prompt templates.  
Example: “Add to Twitter prompt: ‘Tweets must be under 160 characters. Reject any tweet that starts with “It’s important to” or “We all”.’”

**Constraint to add:** _______________

**After adding, test with one quick prompt generation** and verify the drift is corrected. Log the change in your Prompt Update Log (Tool 4 or Chapter 5’s log).
