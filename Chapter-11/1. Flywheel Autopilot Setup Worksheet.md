## 1. Flywheel Autopilot Setup Worksheet

**Purpose:** Design the fixed rhythms and environmental cues that make your Content Transformer the path of least resistance.

**Instructions:** Fill in each section with concrete times, locations, and triggers. Then transfer the commitments to your calendar immediately.

### 1.1 Fixed Sprint Window

- **Day & Time:** ____________________ (e.g., “Tuesdays, 10:00–11:00 a.m.”)
- **Why this time?** (tie to existing habit, e.g., “Right after my video goes live,” “Before my first meeting block.”)
- **Calendar entry title:** _________________________________
- **Protection level:** I will decline or reschedule other commitments during this block unless __________________ (extreme exception).
- **Pre‑sprint ritual:** (e.g., “At 9:55, I close email, make tea, and open the Sprint Launchpad.”)

### 1.2 Hub‑Building Block (Repurpose‑First)

- **When:** ____________________ (e.g., “Immediately after finishing the pillar recording/writing, or Monday 4:00 p.m. if it’s a batch.”)
- **Time needed:** ______ minutes.
- **Commitment:** “I will build the Hub for every pillar piece within 24 hours of creation, no exceptions.”

### 1.3 Environmental Triggers

- **Launchpad link/home:** ____________________ (e.g., a Notion page, a desktop folder, a pinned browser tab.)
- **Reminder:** I will set a recurring alarm/notification: “Sprint in 10” at _____ a.m./p.m.
- **Physical cue:** (e.g., “My sprint timer lives on my desk,” “I put a ‘Deep Creation – Do Not Disturb’ sign on my door.”)

### 1.4 Accountability (Optional)

- **I will share my sprint commitment with:** ____________________ (a peer, a Transformer Circle, a public commitment).
- **Check‑in method:** (e.g., “I send a ✅ in a group chat after each sprint.”)

**After filling this out, block the times in your calendar as recurring events. Color‑code them as non‑negotiable.**
