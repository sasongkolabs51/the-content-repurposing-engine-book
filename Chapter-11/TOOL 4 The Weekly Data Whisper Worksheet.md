## TOOL 4: The Weekly Data Whisper Worksheet

**Purpose:** The ten-minute weekly review that keeps your Decision Matrix calibrated against reality. Prevents the matrix from hardening around stale assumptions. Turns engagement data into strategic intelligence.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes. End of publishing week.

---

### WEEKLY DATA WHISPER — Week of [Date]

---

#### Step 1: Update Matrix Scores (5 minutes)

| Pillar Piece | Predicted E/R/S | Actual Performance This Week | Score Adjustment? |
|---|---|---|---|
| | ___/___/___ | *(e.g., "Twitter thread: 2.4K impressions, 89 shares, 12 replies. LinkedIn carousel: 340 views, 28 comments, 3 DMs.")* | ☐ No change ☐ Adjust: ___ |
| | ___/___/___ | | ☐ No change ☐ Adjust: ___ |

**Did any piece overperform or underperform its predicted score?**

☐ Yes → Which: _______________
  - Overperformed: Why? (Hook? Topic? Timing? Platform algorithm?)
  - Underperformed: Why? (Fatigue? Wrong platform? Bad timing? Voice drift?)

---

#### Step 2: Note Anomalies (2 minutes)

**Anything unusual this week?**

☐ A post got 3×+ normal engagement → Flag for Exemplar Loop.
☐ A post got 50% below normal → Flag for investigation.
☐ A new follower spike from a specific piece → Note which.
☐ A DM / reply / lead from a specific piece → Note which. This is the REAL metric.
☐ Nothing unusual. Steady week.

---
