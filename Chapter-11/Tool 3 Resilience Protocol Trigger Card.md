## Tool 3: Resilience Protocol Trigger Card

**Purpose:** A prewritten fallback plan that eliminates the guilt spiral when you miss a sprint. Print it, keep it on your desk or phone wallpaper, and follow it without hesitation.

### The Protocol

**Level 1 – Microsprint (15 min)**
*When I can’t spare a full hour but can give 15 focused minutes.*
- Pick the top‑scoring pillar from my Decision Matrix.
- Run the Twitter prompt only (or my primary platform).
- Triage (approve/lightly edit/flag) in 10 minutes.
- Schedule immediately.
- Goal: Maintain the sprint → output → relief pathway and keep my primary platform alive.

**Level 2 – Archive Restore (5 min)**
*When even 15 minutes is impossible.*
- Open my Hub‑as‑Dashboard (Chapter 7, Tool 2) and go to a past Spoke section.
- Pick a high‑performing post (Twitter thread, LinkedIn carousel, etc.) that is still evergreen.
- Re‑queue it via my scheduler (no edits needed—it’s already voice‑true).
- Goal: Keep a presence without creating anything new.

**Level 3 – Intentional Pause (2 min)**
*When life requires a full break from content.*
- Write and schedule a single post across platforms: “I’m taking a brief pause to handle some life things. Back soon.”
- Turn off notifications guilt‑free.
- When ready to return, open my Sprint Launchpad; the prompts, Hub templates, and matrix are waiting.

**My Commitment:** I will follow this protocol without self‑judgment. A missed sprint is not a failure; it’s a signal to downshift, not abandon.
