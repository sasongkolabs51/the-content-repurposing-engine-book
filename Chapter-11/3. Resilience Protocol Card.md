## 3. Resilience Protocol Card

**Purpose:** Prevents a missed sprint from turning into a total system collapse. Keep this card somewhere you’ll see it—taped to your monitor, in your Launchpad, or on your phone’s lock screen.

### If I Can’t Do a Full Sprint This Week…

**Level 1: The Microsprint (15 minutes)**
- **When to use:** I have a fraction of my usual time, but I’m not in crisis.
- **Action:** Run ONE prompt for my highest‑scoring pillar, for ONE platform (usually Twitter or LinkedIn). Generate, triage lightly, schedule. Done.
- **Goal:** Keep the sprint→output→relief pathway alive. One post is infinitely better than zero.

**Level 2: The Archive Restore (5 minutes)**
- **When to use:** I can barely open my laptop, but I don’t want dead air.
- **Action:** Go to my Hub’s Spoke sections or my scheduler’s archive. Find a high‑performing evergreen post from 3+ months ago. Re‑queue it natively or with a minor update.
- **Mindset:** “My audience grew since this posted. Most of them never saw it. This is service, not laziness.”

**Level 3: The Intentional Pause (2 minutes)**
- **When to use:** Life has truly hit—health, family, emergency. I need to step away entirely.
- **Action:** Schedule one brief post across platforms: “I’m taking a short break to handle some life things. I’ll be back [approximate date]. Thanks for your patience.”
- **Bonus:** Pin it to your profile. When you return, unpin and your first post back will feel like a reunion, not a recovery.

**My personal resilience thresholds (customize):**
- Miss 1 sprint → I will run a microsprint within 48 hours.
- Miss 2 sprints → I will restore an archive post and re‑evaluate my schedule.
- Miss 3 sprints → I will post an intentional pause and give myself grace. The Launchpad will be waiting.
