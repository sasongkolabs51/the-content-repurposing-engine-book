## 10. AI Prompt Templates for Flywheel Maintenance

These prompts use your AI co‑creator to help you run the feedback loops, not just generate content.

### 10.1 Weekly Data Review Assistant

After collecting your Weekly Data Whisper notes, use this to get a summary and suggestions before you finalize your matrix updates.

```
You are my editorial data analyst. Below are notes from my content performance this week, including engagement observations and any anomalies. Based on these, please:

1. Summarize the top 2–3 patterns you see (e.g., "Hooks that started with a specific number outperformed others," "Posts about [topic] generated deeper comments").
2. Suggest any adjustments to my Decision Matrix scores for the pillar pieces mentioned.
3. Recommend ONE tweak to my prompt library that might address any recurring issues.

My notes:
[PASTE YOUR WEEKLY DATA WHISPER NOTES – PART A AND B]

Keep your response concise. I’ll make the final call.
```

### 10.2 Monthly Voice Drift Detector

Before you do the manual Monthly Voice Check, feed your last month’s posts to this prompt for an automated first pass.

```
You are my voice consistency auditor. Below are my last [X] posts from [platform], in chronological order. My voice fingerprint is:

[PASTE YOUR CONDENSED VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK]

Please scan these posts and flag any of the following:

- Instances where the hook does not match my specified architecture.
- Words or phrases that appear on my banned list or that feel more formal than my typical voice.
- Posts where the emotional register seems inconsistent with my fingerprint (e.g., suddenly distant, overly enthusiastic, or lacking my usual warmth).
- Any other voice anomalies you detect.

List each flag with the post excerpt and a brief explanation. I will then review manually.

Posts:
[PASTE THE LAST MONTH'S POSTS, EACH SEPARATED BY "---"]
```

After getting the AI’s report, use it to guide your manual check. Don’t treat it as a final verdict—your human ear is still the ultimate judge.

### 10.3 Quarterly Reflection Prompt

Use this during your Quarterly External Audit prep, or as a personal debrief.

```
I am conducting my quarterly content review. Here is a summary of my system’s performance over the last 3 months, including progress tracker data and any external feedback I received.

Progress data:
[PASTE YOUR PROGRESS EVOLUTION TRACKER ROWS FOR THE QUARTER]

External feedback summary:
[PASTE KEY POINTS FROM YOUR QUARTERLY EXTERNAL AUDIT]

Based on this, help me brainstorm:
1. What is the single biggest opportunity to improve my Content Transformer in the next quarter?
2. Is there any part of my system that seems to have plateaued or atrophied?
3. What’s one bold experiment I could run next quarter to push my voice or reach further?

Provide a short, direct response.
```
