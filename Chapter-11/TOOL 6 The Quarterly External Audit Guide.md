## TOOL 6: The Quarterly External Audit Guide

**Purpose:** Get outside eyes on your content. Bypass your own blind spots. The coldest, most honest mirror. One question. One sentence of feedback. One improvement focus for the next quarter.

**Time needed:** 30 minutes (15 to prepare, 15 to process feedback). Once per quarter.

---

#### Step 2: Prepare the Sample (10 minutes)

Select 4–5 pieces from the last quarter. Include variety:

☐ 1 Twitter thread (or equivalent short-form)
☐ 1 LinkedIn carousel or text post
☐ 1 newsletter section
☐ 1 TikTok script (or video link)
☐ 1 piece you're NOT sure about (the one you're worried might be drifting)

**Send them with this message:**

> *"Hey [name], I'm doing a quarterly check on my content and I'd love your honest eye. I'm sending you 4–5 pieces from the last few months. I need you to answer ONE question: If you didn't know me, would this content make you want to? Would you follow? Would you trust me? Would you buy from me?*
>
> *You don't need to write an essay. One or two sentences per piece is plenty. And please be honest—I'd rather hear 'this one felt generic' than 'it's all great!' The honesty is the gift.*
>
> *Thank you. I owe you a coffee / a favor / a reciprocal review."*

#### Step 5: Say Thank You

☐ Sent a thank-you message / coffee / reciprocal review.

> *The quarterly audit is the single highest-leverage feedback mechanism in the system. Not because the feedback is complex. But because it bypasses the one blind spot you can never see yourself: whether your content still makes a stranger want to lean in. You can measure engagement. You can track voice drift. But you can't feel what it's like to encounter your content for the first time. Only another person can give you that. And one sentence from them—"this one felt like you were performing instead of talking"—is worth more than a hundred hours of self-analysis.*
