## Tool 6: One Percent Rule Weekly Improvement Log

**Purpose:** To make the flywheel compound by capturing exactly one small improvement each week. This prevents perfectionism (you’re not fixing everything) and ensures steady progress.

**Instructions:** At the start or end of each week, identify one measurable improvement to make. It could be a prompt tweak, a workflow simplification, a new hook test, or a tiny automation. Log it, implement it, and note the result.

**Examples:**
- “Add a negative example to Twitter prompt: ‘Never open with a rhetorical question.’”
- “Reduce LinkedIn carousel from 8 slides to 7 based on engagement drop‑off data.”
- “Test Specificity Bomb hook vs. Bold Reframe hook on this week’s thread (A/B test).”
- “Add a line to TikTok prompt: ‘End with a visual joke, not a text overlay.’”
- “Move my Daily Tune‑Up from 5 p.m. to 4 p.m. because I’m too drained at 5.”

**Quarterly Reflection:** How many improvements have I made? What cumulative effect do I notice? (e.g., “My prompt library is far more robust; AI output requires 50% less editing than three months ago.”)
