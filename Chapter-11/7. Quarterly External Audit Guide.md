## 7. Quarterly External Audit Guide

**Purpose:** Get an outside pair of eyes on your content to catch blind spots you can’t see. Once a quarter, ask a trusted peer, a member of your Transformer Circle, or even a small segment of your audience.

> 3. What’s one thing you wish I’d write more about, or go deeper on?
