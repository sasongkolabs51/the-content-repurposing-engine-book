## TOOL 5: The Monthly Voice Check Protocol

**Purpose:** The immune system against slow voice drift. Read last month's posts in sequence. Catch the subtle creep of formality, the vanishing contractions, the generic closings. One reinforcing constraint. That's all it takes.

**Time needed:** 20 minutes. First Monday of each month.

---

### MONTHLY VOICE CHECK — [Month / Year]

---

#### Step 1: The Sequential Read (10 minutes)

Open your Hub Spoke sections (or your scheduling tool's archive) for ONE platform. Read the last 4–5 posts in sequence. Not for content. For *voice*.

**Platform I'm checking this month:** _______________
*(Rotate platforms each month: Jan = Twitter, Feb = LinkedIn, Mar = Newsletter, etc.)*

**Read them aloud.** (Yes, aloud. Your ear catches what your eyes skip.)

---

#### Step 2: The Three-Marker Check (5 minutes)

| Marker | What to Listen For | Drift Detected? |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook Architecture** | Do all openings follow my documented patterns? Or have some drifted to generic questions / "In today's world…" / safe statements? | ☐ No drift ☐ Drift in post(s): ___ |
| **Lexical Consistency** | Are my signature phrases present? Contractions? Informal asides? Or has the language gotten more formal / corporate / uniform? | ☐ No drift ☐ Drift in post(s): ___ |
| **Emotional Register** | Does the warmth / humor / vulnerability level match my Fingerprint? Or has it flattened into "helpful but neutral"? | ☐ No drift ☐ Drift in post(s): ___ |

**Additional checks:**

☐ Sentence length: Has it crept up? (Average should be ~___ words. This month's average feels: ☐ Right ☐ Too long ☐ Too short)
☐ Close pattern: Are all closings matching my documented pattern? Or have some become generic CTAs?
☐ Direct address: Am I still saying "you" and talking TO the reader? Or has it shifted to impersonal "one might" / "it is important"?

---

#### Step 3: The One Reinforcing Constraint (3 minutes)

If drift is detected, write ONE specific rule to add to your Voice Fingerprint / prompt templates:

**The drift:**

_______________________________________________________________
*(e.g., "My tweets have grown 20% longer over the last month. Average went from 14 words to 18. The punch is gone.")*

**The reinforcing constraint:**

```
[Paste into your Voice Fingerprint / prompt templates]

VOICE REINFORCEMENT — [Month]:
[e.g., "Cap tweets at 180 characters. If a tweet exceeds 15 words,
split it or cut. The punch is in the brevity. When in doubt,
shorter."]
```

**Added to prompt template?** ☐ Yes ☐ Not yet → Do it before next sprint.

---

#### Step 4: The "Blind Test" (2 minutes)

Pick one post from this month and one from six months ago. Read them back-to-back.

**Do they sound like the same person?**

☐ Yes. Voice is consistent.
☐ Mostly. Slight shift in: _______________
☐ No. The recent one sounds more: ☐ Formal ☐ Generic ☐ Flat ☐ Corporate ☐ Other: ___

**If "no":** The drift is significant. Schedule a Voice Fingerprint refresh (Chapter 2, Tool 9). Re-extract your patterns from your 3 best recent posts. Update the condensed block.
