## Tool 8: Quarterly External Audit Request Template

**Purpose:** To get a cold, honest outside perspective on your content once per quarter, bypassing your own blind spots.

**Instructions:** Copy and send this message (adapted for your relationship) to a trusted peer, a Transformer Circle member, or even a small paid consultant. Give them 3–5 recent posts from different platforms.

### Request Template

*Hi [Name],*

*I’m doing a quarterly review of my content and I’d love your honest, outside perspective. I’ve attached [or linked] a few recent posts of mine from [platforms]. I have one main question, but please share any other thoughts you have.*

*Main question: If you didn’t know me at all, would this content make you want to follow me? Why or why not?*

*Specifically, I’m curious about:*
- *Does the voice feel consistent and human across the pieces?*
- *Are the hooks compelling, or did any make you want to scroll past?*
- *Does anything feel overly “AI‑like,” generic, or emotionally flat?*
- *Any other gut reactions—good or bad—are incredibly helpful.*

*No need to be gentle; honest feedback is exactly what I need to improve. Thank you so much!*

**After receiving feedback:**  
Distill the feedback into one improvement focus for the next quarter. Example: “My hooks are sharp, but my closings feel rushed. I’ll add a prompt rule: ‘End every post with a reflective question that’s personal, not a generic CTA.’”
