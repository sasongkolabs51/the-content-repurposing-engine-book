## Tool 11: Flywheel Mental Rehearsal Script & Summary Card

### Mental Rehearsal Script (read aloud weekly, especially after a missed sprint)

*I’ve built a system that doesn’t depend on my willpower. It runs on triggers and defaults—my sprint launches from a calendar alert, my Hub builds itself right after recording, and my daily tune‑up catches issues before they grow.*

*When life interrupts, I don’t panic. I have a prewritten resilience protocol: microsprint, archive restore, intentional pause. I can downshift without abandoning. The flywheel doesn’t need to spin at full speed every single week; it just needs to keep turning.*

*I’m not chasing perfection. I’m making one small improvement each week—the One Percent Rule. Over a year, that’s over fifty refinements. My system will be unrecognizably smoother, sharper, and more my own.*

*I have nested feedback loops: daily tune‑up, weekly data whisper, monthly voice check, quarterly external audit. Together they catch fires, refine predictions, preserve voice, and validate direction. I don’t need a massive overhaul; I just follow the rhythm.*

*My Content Transformer now works on my best days and also when I’m tired, distracted, or simply living. I am no longer the engine. I am its strategic overseer. The flywheel spins. My job is to guide it—and to enjoy the quiet mastery of the everyday.*

### Summary Card: The Flywheel in Five Lines

- **Autopilot trumps motivation.** Fixed sprint windows, a launchpad, and environmental triggers remove the need to decide.
- **Resilience is preplanned.** Microsprint → Archive Restore → Intentional Pause. Downshift, don’t abandon.
- **A five‑minute tune‑up beats a quarterly overhaul.** Catch formatting glitches, engagement spikes, and drift daily.
- **Feedback loops compound.** Weekly whispers refine the matrix; monthly checks preserve voice; quarterly audits validate direction.
- **The One Percent Rule.** One measurable improvement each week. Fifty‑two tweaks a year. Effortless mastery.
