## Tool 2: Weekly Flywheel Rhythms Calendar Builder

**Purpose:** To lock in the four non‑negotiable beats that keep the flywheel spinning with minimal effort. This template helps you define the exact days/times and then block them on your calendar.

**Instructions:** Fill in your ideal times below, then immediately open your calendar and create recurring events for each. Total weekly commitment: under two hours.

### My Flywheel Rhythms

| Rhythm | What It Is | Duration | My Day & Time | Calendar Event Name |
|--------|------------|----------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Sprint Window** | Full repurposing sprint (or microsprint if time‑crunched) | 60 min (or 15 min for micro) | | “60‑Min Multi‑Platform Sprint” |
| **Hub‑Building Block** | Fill Hub immediately after recording/writing pillar content | 30 min | | “Build Hub for [Pillar]” |
| **Daily Tune‑Up** | Quick review of that day’s posts + one micro‑adjustment | 5 min (end of workday) | | “Daily Tune‑Up” |
| **Weekly Data Whisper** | Update Decision Matrix scores from the week’s repurposed posts | 10 min (end of publishing week) | | “Weekly Data Whisper” |

**Add to calendar now.** Choose a start date for this week and make the Sprint Window and Hub‑Building Block recurring weekly. (Daily Tune‑Up can be a recurring reminder, not a full event.)

**First‑Month Calendar View:** On a separate page or whiteboard, map the first 30 days with these rhythms visible. For the first month, also schedule:
- Week 2: First Weekly Data Whisper
- Week 4: First Monthly Voice Check (see Tool 7)
