## TOOL 9: The One Percent Rule Log

**Purpose:** Track your weekly micro-improvement. One thing. Every week. Not a revolution. A refinement. Over a year, 50+ small changes transform the system into something you couldn't have designed on day one.

**Time needed:** 1 minute per week to log.

---

### THE ONE PERCENT RULE

**The rule:** Each week, make exactly ONE measurable improvement to your system. Not five. Not zero. One.

### The Compound Math

- 1 improvement/week × 52 weeks = **52 improvements/year**
- Each improvement makes the next one slightly easier (the system gets more refined, so the remaining improvements are finer-grained)
- After 2 years: **100+ refinements.** Your system is unrecognizable from the one you built in Chapter 4. Sharper. Faster. More magnetic. More *you*.

> *The One Percent Rule is the antidote to two opposing failure modes: perfectionism ("I need to fix everything at once") and stagnation ("it's good enough, I'll leave it alone"). One thing. Every week. That's all. The compound interest does the rest.*
