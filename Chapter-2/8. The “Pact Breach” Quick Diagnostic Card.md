## 8. The “Pact Breach” Quick Diagnostic Card

When AI output goes wrong mid-workflow, don’t spiral. Run this 30-second diagnostic to locate the missing constraint.

**Symptom → Likely missing input**  
- Output feels generic, uses corporate phrases → **Voice Fingerprint not included or too vague** (add lexical bans, tone descriptions)  
- Opens with “In today’s…” or a bland question → **Hook architecture constraint missing**  
- Sentences all same length, no punch → **Rhythm rules missing**  
- Structure feels meandering → **Argument structure not specified**  
- Ideas feel shallow or not mine → **You didn’t provide the Hub components; the AI was forced to invent**  
- Platform-agnostic, same post on every channel → **Platform format rules not given** (add Chapter 3 grammar)  

Tape this card to your monitor. The next time you get a bad draft, you’ll spend ten seconds diagnosing instead of ten minutes despairing.
