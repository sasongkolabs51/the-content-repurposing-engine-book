## 5. AI Prompt Templates With Voice Fingerprint Integration

These prompts are built to accept your Voice Fingerprint as a primary constraint. Fill in the bracketed sections with the details from your workbook before using.

### 5.1 Voice-Infused Social Post Prompt (Any Platform)

```
You are writing for a creator with the following voice fingerprint:

- Hook: [e.g., short, blunt, counterintuitive statement under 10 words]
- Rhythm: [e.g., single-line paragraphs, mix of punchy and slightly longer sentences]
- Lexical rules: USE [list 3-5 go-to phrases, contractions]; NEVER use [list 3-5 banned words]
- Argument structure: [e.g., problem → surprising insight → reflective question]
- Tone: [e.g., irreverent, warm, self-deprecating, directly addressing "you"]

Now, take the following hub components and turn them into a [platform] post that fits the native grammar of that platform:
- Core thesis: [insert]
- One supporting story (1-2 sentences): [insert]
- Quotable line: [insert]

Write the post. Do not add any ideas that aren't in the brief. Stay strictly within the voice constraints.
```

### 5.2 “Voice Fingerprint Debugger” Prompt

Use this when the AI’s output feels flat, and you want it to analyze why.

```
Here is my voice fingerprint:
[Paste your draft voice fingerprint from Section 2.6]

Here is a piece of content I wrote that I consider highly voice-authentic:
[Paste one of your best posts]

Here is an AI-generated draft that I asked to use my voice fingerprint, but it still feels off:
[Paste the disappointing AI draft]

Please analyze the AI draft against my fingerprint. List 3–5 specific ways it violates my voice rules. Then, rewrite the draft to comply.
```

### 5.3 Orchestrator’s Multi-Platform Brief Prompt (Advanced Preview)

This prompt assumes you’ll feed it your Hub and ask for multiple platform adaptations at once—an approach you’ll fully install in Chapter 6. But you can start experimenting now.

```
You are my structural adaptation engine operating under our Cocreator Pact.  
My voice fingerprint: [paste full fingerprint]  

Hub components for this pillar:  
- Thesis: [insert]  
- Story 1: [insert]  
- Story 2: [insert]  
- Data point: [insert]  
- Quotable line: [insert]  
- Audience objection: [insert]  

Generate the following adaptations, each strictly using my voice fingerprint and the native grammar of the platform:

1. Twitter/X thread (7–10 tweets): each tweet ends on a pattern interrupt; hook must be under 140 characters.  
2. LinkedIn carousel (7 slides): start with a diagnostic question, build a narrative arc, end with a reflective prompt.  
3. TikTok/Reels script (60 seconds): open with a bold visual metaphor; include my quotable line as on-screen text; end with a loopable challenge.  

For each, output a separate section with the platform name as header. Do not add any external information. Work only from the Hub components provided.
```
