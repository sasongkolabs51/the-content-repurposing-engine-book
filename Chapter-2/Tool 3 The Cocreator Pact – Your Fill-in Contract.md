## Tool 3: The Cocreator Pact – Your Fill-in Contract

**Purpose:** To externalize the new rules of collaboration so that neither you nor the AI oversteps. When a draft comes back generic, you’ll know exactly which input was missing—rather than blaming the tool.

**Instructions:** Fill in the two sections below. Print it, save it as your desktop wallpaper, or keep it at the top of your prompt library. This is your operating charter.

### What I Provide (My Creative Responsibilities)

*Check and complete each item. This is what I commit to handing the AI before asking for a draft.*

- [ ] **The Hub Document (or a clear content brief):** I will give the AI the modular intellectual components—core thesis, stories, data, quotes—not a raw, undigested transcript. *(If I don’t have a Hub yet, I will at minimum provide a structured summary.)*

- [ ] **The Target Platform’s Format Rules:** I will specify what the platform demands (e.g., “Twitter thread: 7–10 tweets, each ending with a curiosity hook; no thread unroll links; use line breaks for rhythm.”) *(Chapter 3 will build this fully, but for now I’ll note what I know.)*

- [ ] **My Voice Fingerprint:** I will paste the summary from Tool 2 into the prompt, giving the AI explicit constraints on hook style, rhythm, vocabulary, structure, and emotional register.

- [ ] **Examples of My Writing (optional but powerful):** I may include 1–2 short examples of my best posts as additional style references.

- [ ] **Final Editorial Authority:** I understand that the AI is generating a *first draft*. I will review every output, make light edits, add personal anecdotes or timely references, and approve before publishing. The buck stops with me.

### What the AI Executes (Its Bounded Role)

*I will hold the AI accountable for these actions—and only these.*

- [ ] **Structural Adaptation:** It rearranges and restructures the material I provided to fit the platform’s narrative grammar. It does not invent core ideas or change my opinion.

- [ ] **Voice Compliance:** It adheres to the constraints in my Voice Fingerprint to the best of its ability. If it violates them, I will flag the specific violation and ask for a rewrite.

- [ ] **First-Draft Generation:** It produces a complete draft, not bullet points or outlines, in the format I specified. I accept that this draft is raw material for my editorial direction.

- [ ] **No Rogue Decisions:** It does not decide that a different hook would be “better,” nor does it insert generic motivational platitudes or corporate speak. If it does, I will tighten the constraints in my next prompt.

**Pact Statement** (read aloud before your next AI session):  
*I am the editorial director. The AI is my structural adaptation engine. I supply the vision, voice, and final judgment. It supplies the labor of restructuring. Together, we produce platform-true, voice-consistent work at a pace neither of us could achieve alone.*
