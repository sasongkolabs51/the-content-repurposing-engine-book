## TOOL 4: The Authenticity Reframe Practice

**Purpose:** Prove to yourself, with a stopwatch, that editing an AI draft using your Voice Fingerprint is faster (and often better) than writing from scratch. This is the exercise that converts the intellectual reframe into a felt, bodily knowing.

**Time needed:** 25–30 minutes

**What you need:** The last AI-generated draft you rejected as "not sounding like me." If you deleted it, regenerate something similar. If you've never used AI, generate a quick draft now using a generic prompt (e.g., "Write a Twitter thread about why most creators burn out") so you have something to work with.
