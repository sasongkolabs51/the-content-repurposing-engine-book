## Tool 5: Orchestrator Identity Installation Checklist

**Purpose:** To embed the identity shift from “writer who tolerates AI” to “orchestrator who directs AI” into your daily workflow.

**Instructions:** Print or pin this checklist. Read it before you open any AI tool. Check each statement you’ve internalized.

### My New Operating Assumptions (Orchestrator Edition)

- [ ] Authenticity is about the audience’s experience, not the origin of the keystrokes. A voice-consistent, platform-true post is authentic, period.
- [ ] My Voice Fingerprint is a living document. It’s not a static artifact but something I refine with every piece of content.
- [ ] I don’t ask AI for “a good post.” I give it a creative brief, voice constraints, and platform rules. That’s the pact.
- [ ] When AI output feels generic, I don’t blame the tool. I ask: “Which constraint was missing or too vague?” Then I tighten it.
- [ ] My highest-leverage work is editorial direction—deciding what stories to tell, what structure to use, what personal touches to add—not sentence-level word-smithing from a blank page.
- [ ] I spend my creative energy on the 20% that only I can do: personal anecdotes, timely references, narrative judgment. The AI handles the structural 80%.
- [ ] The pact is bilateral. If I fail to provide a Hub or a clear format rule, the AI’s generic output is on me. If I provide everything and it still violates my voice, I refine my prompt and try again.

**Daily Micro-Practice:** For the next week, before each content session, choose one assumption from this list and consciously act from it. At the end of the session, jot down one thing that shifted because of that assumption.
