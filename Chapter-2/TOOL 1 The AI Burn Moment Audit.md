## TOOL 1: The AI Burn Moment Audit

**Purpose:** Rewrite your brain's narrative about AI failure. Every time AI output felt "wrong," it wasn't the tool being incompetent. It was a missing constraint. This audit makes that visible.

**Time needed:** 20–25 minutes

**What you need:** Memory of (or screenshots of) the last three times you tried an AI tool for content and walked away disappointed. If you've never used AI for content, think about the last three times you *almost* tried and talked yourself out of it.

### The Pattern Across All Three

Look at your three "missing constraints." Is there a theme?

☐ I never specified my **hook style** → AI defaulted to generic openers
☐ I never specified my **vocabulary / word choices** → AI used corporate language
☐ I never specified my **sentence rhythm** → AI wrote in uniform, flat cadence
☐ I never specified my **argument structure** → AI organized the thought in a generic list
☐ I never specified my **emotional register** → AI came back emotionally neutral
☐ I never gave it the **actual content components** (just a topic or a raw transcript)
