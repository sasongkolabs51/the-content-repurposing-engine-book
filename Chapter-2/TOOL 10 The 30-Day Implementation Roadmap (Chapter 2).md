## TOOL 10: The 30-Day Implementation Roadmap (Chapter 2)

**Purpose:** Turn the chapter's principles into a lived practice over four weeks. Because reading about the Cocreator Pact and actually *operating* under it are two different things.

---

### Week 1: Audit and Extract

- [ ] Complete the **AI Burn Moment Audit** (Tool 1). Identify your pattern of missing constraints.
- [ ] Pull your 5 best posts. Complete the full **Voice Fingerprint Extraction** (Tool 2). All five dimensions.
- [ ] Write your **Condensed Master Fingerprint** (the one-page version).
- [ ] Run the **Voice Fingerprint Validator prompt** (Tool 7, Prompt A). Let the AI stress-test your spec sheet. Revise based on feedback.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~90 minutes across the week

### Week 2: Draft the Pact and Test It

- [ ] Write your **Cocreator Pact** (Tool 3). Print it. Put it near your workspace.
- [ ] Do the **Authenticity Reframe Practice** (Tool 4). Time yourself. Feel the difference.
- [ ] Generate ONE platform asset using the **Master Prompt** (Tool 7, Prompt B) with your Fingerprint and Hub components.
- [ ] Run the **"Is This Voice-True?" Checklist** (Tool 8) on the output.
- [ ] Edit the draft. Note how long the edit took vs. writing from scratch.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes across the week

### Week 3: Operate Under the Pact

- [ ] Fill out the **Pre-Prompt Session Planner** (Tool 6) before every AI interaction this week.
- [ ] Create at least 3 platform assets using the Pact workflow (Hub → Fingerprint → Prompt → Draft → Checklist → Edit → Publish).
- [ ] Use the **"This Doesn't Sound Like Me" Revision Prompt** (Tool 7, Prompt C) at least once instead of scrapping a draft.
- [ ] Complete the **Writer-to-Orchestrator Self-Check** (Tool 5) on Friday.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~2 hours across the week (but replacing 3+ hours of manual work)

### Week 4: Refine and Lock In

- [ ] Run the **Cocreator Pact Compliance Check** (Tool 7, Prompt E) on your best and worst AI interaction from the month.
- [ ] Do the **Monthly Voice Fingerprint Refinement** (Tool 9) — yes, even though it's only been a month. Patterns will already be clearer.
- [ ] Re-do the **Writer-to-Orchestrator Self-Check** (Tool 5). Compare to Week 3.
- [ ] Write a one-paragraph "letter to future me" about what you now know about AI collaboration that you didn't know 30 days ago. Tape it to the Pact.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~60 minutes across the week

---

### The 30-Day Proof

At the end of Day 30, you should be able to answer "yes" to these:

1. Can I describe my voice in mechanical, teachable terms to another person (or an AI) in under 60 seconds?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Getting there ☐ Not yet

2. Do I have a working Cocreator Pact that I actually reference before prompt sessions?

   ☐ Yes ☐ It exists but I forget to use it ☐ Not yet

3. Can I take an AI draft that feels "off" and fix it in under 10 minutes using my Fingerprint, rather than rewriting from scratch?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Sometimes ☐ Not yet

4. Do I feel like an editorial director who delegates structural execution, rather than a writer who's "cheating" by using AI?

   ☐ Yes ☐ Mostly ☐ Still fighting the guilt ☐ Not yet

5. Has my time-per-asset decreased while voice fidelity stayed the same or improved?

   ☐ Yes ☐ About the same ☐ No

> *If you hit 4 or 5 "yes" answers, the Pact is installed. You're operating under a new creative identity. If you're at 2–3, that's fine. The patterns are forming. Keep going. If you're at 0–1, go back to Tool 1 and redo the Burn Audit. The old belief is still running the show, and it needs to be confronted with specific evidence before the new system can take hold.*
