## 9. 7-Day Cocreator Pact Installation Plan

**Day 1 – Audit and Reframe**  
- [ ] Complete the AI Burn Moment Audit (Section 1).  
- [ ] Write down one limiting belief about AI that this audit challenged.  
- [ ] Read the Authenticity Reframe paragraph from Chapter 2 aloud (the section comparing your role to a film director).

**Day 2 – Extract Your Voice**  
- [ ] Gather your five best posts.  
- [ ] Fill out the Voice Fingerprint Extraction Workbook (Section 2) in full.  
- [ ] Write your consolidated Voice Fingerprint (Section 2.6). This is your new creative asset.

**Day 3 – Draft the Pact**  
- [ ] Fill out and sign the Cocreator Pact Template (Section 3).  
- [ ] Write the pact’s one-liner on a sticky note and place it on your screen.  
- [ ] Reflect: Which side of the pact have you historically been weak on—providing clear constraints, or trying to do the AI’s job yourself?

**Day 4 – First Guided Edit**  
- [ ] Complete the Authenticity Reframe Practice Worksheet (Section 4) with an old AI draft.  
- [ ] Note the time saved compared to your usual from-scratch work.  
- [ ] Celebrate: you just orchestrated instead of labored.

**Day 5 – Prompt With Your Fingerprint**  
- [ ] Take a recent Hub (or rough notes from a pillar piece) and use Prompt 5.1 to generate a social post.  
- [ ] Run it through the Voice Fingerprint Debugger (Prompt 5.2) if the first output feels off.  
- [ ] Refine your fingerprint document based on what you learn.

**Day 6 – Multi-Platform Experiment**  
- [ ] Try the Orchestrator’s Multi-Platform Brief Prompt (5.3) with the same Hub.  
- [ ] Compare the three outputs. Do they feel like different adaptations of the same idea, or generic copies?  
- [ ] Adjust your fingerprint or platform instructions accordingly.

**Day 7 – Identity Anchor and Share**  
- [ ] Write your own version of the Orchestrator Identity Script (Section 6) that feels fully yours.  
- [ ] Record a voice memo explaining the Cocreator Pact to an imaginary creator friend. Teaching cements the shift.  
- [ ] Post one of the AI-assisted drafts from this week—and notice that your audience responds to the voice, not the keystrokes.
