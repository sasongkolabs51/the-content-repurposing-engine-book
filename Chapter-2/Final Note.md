## Final Note

Here's the thing nobody tells you about the shift from "AI is a threat to my voice" to "AI is an instrument I play": it's not a one-time decision. It's a daily practice. Some mornings you'll open a prompt window and the old suspicion will creep in. *This won't sound like me. I should just write it myself. What if my audience finds out?*

When that happens—and it will—look at the Pact on your wall. Look at your Voice Fingerprint. Remember the Greta Gerwig principle: the authenticity is in the vision, the constraints, and the final edit. Not in the keystrokes. You are the director. The AI is the crew. And your audience doesn't care who held the boom mic. They care whether the scene made them feel something true.

You've got your Fingerprint. You've got your Pact. You've got the prompts. Now go make something that sounds unmistakably like you—and took a fraction of the time it used to.
