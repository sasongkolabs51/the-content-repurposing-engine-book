**Purpose:** Build the actual spec sheet for your voice. This is the single most important document in the entire Content Transformer system. Everything downstream—every prompt, every AI interaction, every delegation—depends on this being concrete and specific.

**Time needed:** 40–50 minutes (first pass). You'll refine it over the following weeks.

**What you need:** Your five highest-performing posts from the past 12 months, across at least two platforms. "Highest-performing" means whatever metric matters to you: engagement rate, shares, replies, saves, revenue attributed. Pull them up. Read them aloud. Seriously—out loud. Your ear catches patterns your eyes glide over.
