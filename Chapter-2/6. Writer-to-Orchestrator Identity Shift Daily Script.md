## 6. Writer-to-Orchestrator Identity Shift Daily Script

Use these phrases to start your content sessions. Say them aloud, write them in a journal, or use them as a visual anchor before opening any AI tool.

**Full Morning Script:**
> I am an editorial orchestrator, not a solitary writer.  
> My voice is a documented system I can teach to any tool.  
> My creative contribution is the brief, the constraints, and the final human touch—not every keystroke.  
> When AI output fails, I ask: “What constraint was missing?” I do not retreat into manual labor.  
> Today, I direct; I don’t type everything myself. And that makes my work more authentically mine, not less.

**Pre-Prompt Mini Anchor:**  
“Brief, voice, platform. I provide those three; the AI handles the structural first draft. Then I edit like an editor, not a ghostwriter’s cleanup crew.”
