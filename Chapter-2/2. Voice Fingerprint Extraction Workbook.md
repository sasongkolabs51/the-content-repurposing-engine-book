Combine your findings into a single, prompt-ready description. You’ll refine this over time, but the first draft is what matters most. Write it as if you were briefing a ghostwriter.

“My voice fingerprint:  
- Hook: ____________________________________________________________  
- Rhythm: __________________________________________________________  
- Lexical: __________________________________________________________  
- Argument: ________________________________________________________

Example:  
“My voice fingerprint: Hook with a blunt, counterintuitive sentence under 10 words. Use short paragraphs, mix of punchy lines and one longer sentence for rhythm. Use contractions, colloquial phrases like ‘here’s the thing,’ and avoid corporate jargon like ‘leverage’ or ‘synergy.’ Structure is problem→insight→reflective question. Tone is irreverent but warm, with self-deprecating humor and direct address throughout.”

Keep this somewhere you can copy-paste into prompts. You just turned your voice into a spec sheet.
