## TOOL 3: The Cocreator Pact

**Purpose:** The formal "contract" between you and your AI. Print it. Tape it to the wall. Read it before every prompt session. It's your psychological anchor against the old mistrust.

**Time needed:** 15 minutes to draft. 2 minutes to re-read before each session.

---

### THE COCREATOR PACT
#### Between [Your Name], Editorial Director, and [AI Tool(s)], Structural Adaptation Engine

**Effective date:** _______________
**Review date (revisit and revise):** _______________

---

#### ARTICLE 1: What I Provide (The Editorial Director's Responsibilities)

Before any AI interaction, I will supply:

- [ ] **The Creative Brief (Hub Document):** The preprocessed intellectual components of my pillar content—core thesis, stories, data, quotable lines, objections. I will NOT hand over a raw transcript and say "do something with this."

- [ ] **The Voice Fingerprint:** My documented voice constraints (hook style, rhythm, vocabulary, structure, register), pasted directly into the prompt. I will NOT assume the AI "gets" my voice from context.

- [ ] **The Platform Format Rules:** The specific structural requirements of the target platform (thread length, carousel slide count, video duration, newsletter word count, native conventions). I will NOT treat all platforms as the same bucket.

- [ ] **The Creative Intent Note:** One or two sentences on what this specific asset should *do* for the reader. (e.g., "This LinkedIn post should make a mid-career manager feel seen in their decision fatigue, then offer one reframe they haven't considered.")

- [ ] **Final Editorial Judgment:** I will review every AI output. I will approve, lightly edit, flag for rewrite, or discard. The AI does not publish. I do.

---

#### ARTICLE 2: What AI Executes (The Structural Adaptation Engine's Responsibilities)

Given my inputs, the AI will:

- [ ] **Restructure** the provided Hub components to fit the target platform's native narrative grammar. It will NOT simply summarize or trim.

- [ ] **Follow the Voice Fingerprint constraints** precisely. If the Fingerprint says "open with a two-word fragment," it opens with a two-word fragment. No creative override.

- [ ] **Stay within the creative brief.** It will NOT invent new core ideas, opinions, or arguments that aren't in the Hub. It rearranges and reframes what I've given it.

- [ ] **Flag uncertainty.** If a component is ambiguous or if it's unsure whether a reframe stays true to my intent, it will note this rather than guess.

- [ ] **Produce a first structural draft**, not a final publication-ready piece. The draft is raw material for my editorial pass.

---

#### ARTICLE 3: What Neither Party Does (The Boundaries)

- The AI does NOT decide my opinions.
- The AI does NOT override my voice rules because it "thinks" something else would perform better.
- The AI does NOT add generic filler ("In today's digital landscape…") to pad length.
- I do NOT expect the AI to read my mind or infer my voice from a vague topic prompt.
- I do NOT treat a first draft as a final product or judge the system by its unedited output.
- I do NOT skip providing the Voice Fingerprint because "it should just know by now."

---

#### ARTICLE 4: The Quality Standard

An AI-generated draft is considered **successful** if:

☐ It follows the structural format of the target platform
☐ The hook matches my documented hook architecture
☐ The sentence rhythm approximates my documented cadence
☐ No words from my "anti-vocabulary" appear
☐ The argument follows my documented structure
☐ The emotional register matches my documented tone
☐ It reads like something I *could* have written, even if I'd phrase 20% of it differently

An AI-generated draft is considered **a failure requiring a prompt revision** if:

☐ It opens with a generic / cliché hook
☐ It uses corporate language I'd never use
☐ It invents ideas or opinions not in the Hub
☐ It ignores the platform's native structure
☐ It reads like a summary rather than a re-architecture

**When a draft fails, the problem is almost always a missing or unclear constraint in my prompt—not the AI's inherent inability.** I will diagnose the missing constraint and revise the prompt before concluding "AI just can't do this."

---

#### ARTICLE 5: The Authenticity Clause

Authenticity, for the purposes of this pact, is defined as:

> *Output that is platform-true and voice-consistent, regardless of which fingers (or algorithms) generated the first draft.*

I am not a typist. I am an editorial director. My authenticity lives in my ideas, my perspective, my structural choices, and my final editorial pass—not in the mechanical act of keystroke generation. A film director is not less authentic because she didn't operate the camera. I am not less authentic because the first structural draft came from an AI working within my constraints.

**The test is not "Did I type this?" The test is "Does this sound like me, land like me, and serve my audience like me?"**
