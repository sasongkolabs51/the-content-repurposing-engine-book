## Tool 2: The Voice Fingerprint Extraction Worksheet

**Purpose:** To turn your unique voice into a teachable, repeatable spec sheet—the single highest-leverage document you can create before writing another AI prompt.

**Instructions:** Gather your five highest-performing pieces of content from the last year (ideally from at least two different platforms). These can be tweets, LinkedIn posts, newsletter issues, video scripts—whatever you’re most proud of and that your audience responded to powerfully. Read them aloud, then fill in each of the five dimensions below. Work from what you *consistently* do, not what you wish you did.

### Dimension 1: Hook Architecture  
How do you typically open a piece of content? Look at the first sentence or first line across all five pieces. Categorize the pattern.

**Check the pattern(s) that appear most often:**
- [ ] A bold, declarative statement that challenges a common belief
- [ ] A short, scene-setting fragment (e.g., “It’s 2 a.m. and you’re still scrolling.”)
- [ ] A direct question that implies shared experience (“You know that feeling when…”)
- [ ] A surprising stat or data point
- [ ] A personal confession or vulnerable admission
- [ ] Other: ______

**Now describe your hook pattern in one sentence, with an example from your own work:**  

*My hook pattern: I usually open with [pattern description]. For example: “_________________________”*

---

### Dimension 2: Sentence Rhythm & Length  
Read a paragraph from each post aloud. Tap your desk with each sentence. Do you favor short bursts? Long, flowing sentences that build momentum? A pattern of short-long-short?

**Describe your dominant rhythm (be specific—mention average sentence length if you can estimate):**  

*My rhythm: _________________________________________________________*  
*(e.g., “Short, punchy sentences (8–12 words), with occasional longer ones (20+ words) that land a deeper insight. I almost never write a sentence over 30 words.”)*

### Dimension 4: Argument Structure  
How do you typically organize your ideas? Look at the skeleton of your posts, not the topic.

**Identify the structure you reach for most often:**
- [ ] Problem → Insight → Action (or Reflective Question)
- [ ] Story → Principle → Application
- [ ] Tension → Payoff → Broader Implication
- [ ] Common Belief → Why It’s Wrong → What to Do Instead
- [ ] Personal Journey → Lesson Learned → Invitation to Reader
- [ ] Other: ______

**Describe your argument structure in a sentence or two, with a mini-outline:**

*My structure: ______________________________________________________*  
*(e.g., “I start with a relatable problem, offer a counterintuitive reframe, then end with a single, actionable directive. No summary, no fluff.”)*

---

### Dimension 5: Emotional Register  
What emotional temperature do your best posts carry? Are they warm and empathetic? Sharp and contrarian? Playful and self-deprecating? Righteously angry? Quietly reflective?

**Choose the 1–2 registers that dominate your work:**
- [ ] Warm & empathetic (“I get it. I’ve been there too.”)
- [ ] Challenging & contrarian (“Everything you’ve been told about X is backwards.”)
- [ ] Playful & irreverent (humor, sarcasm, not taking self too seriously)
- [ ] Earnest & deeply personal (high vulnerability)
- [ ] Professorly & analytical (lots of frameworks, logic)
- [ ] Cheerleader & motivational (“You’ve got this. Here’s how.”)

**Describe your emotional register in one sentence, with a snippet from your content that exemplifies it:**  

*My register: ________________________________________________________*  
*Example: “_________________________”*

### Your Draft Voice Fingerprint – Summary Box

Copy the answers above into a compact, copy-paste-friendly summary. This is the document you’ll hand to any AI before you ask it to draft anything.

**Voice Fingerprint – [Your Name/Handle]**

- **Hook Architecture:** _______________
- **Sentence Rhythm:** _______________
- **Lexical Fingerprints:** _______________
- **Argument Structure:** _______________
- **Emotional Register:** _______________

Now, beneath this summary, add a “Never Use” list (banned words/phrases like “In today’s fast-paced world,” “unlock your potential,” “leverage,” etc.).

Keep this fingerprint open in a pinned note. You’ll refine it over time, but it’s already enough to transform your AI output.
