## 7. Voice Fingerprint Refinement Checklist

Your first Voice Fingerprint is a draft. As you use it, you’ll discover patterns you missed. Use this checklist every few weeks to tighten your spec sheet until it becomes a precise instrument.

- [ ] **Hook test:** Feed your hook pattern to the AI in isolation. Does it generate openings that sound like you? If not, the description needs more detail (e.g., don’t just say “blunt”—say “blunt and often beginning with ‘The truth is’ or ‘Most people…’ ”).
- [ ] **Rhythm test:** Ask the AI to rewrite a long paragraph in your rhythm. Compare side-by-side with your own writing. Adjust the rhythm instruction until the AI version “feels” right when read aloud.
- [ ] **Lexical test:** Run a draft and highlight any words that still feel off-brand. Add those to your banned list. If the AI overuses a phrase you like, specify a frequency rule (“use ‘here’s the thing’ no more than once per post”).
- [ ] **Structure test:** Paste one of your classic posts and ask the AI to identify the argument structure. If it can’t see it, your structural description isn’t clear enough. Iterate until the AI correctly maps your pattern.
- [ ] **Emotion test:** Ask a trusted reader or peer to compare an AI-generated draft (with your fingerprint) to your actual writing. Can they tell which is which? If so, dig into what gave it away and encode that too.
