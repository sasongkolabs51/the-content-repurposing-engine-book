## 3. Cocreator Pact Template

**Purpose:** Formalize the division of labor between you and your AI cocreator. When output goes wrong, you’ll return to this pact to find the missing piece instead of blaming the tool.

**Fill out and keep visible:**

### What I Provide (The Creative Director’s Brief)
- ☐ **The Hub document** – pre-extracted modular components of my pillar content (thesis, stories, data, quotes, objections). I never ask the AI to invent ideas; I give it processed ingredients.
- ☐ **My Voice Fingerprint** (from Section 2) – encoded constraints on hook style, rhythm, vocabulary, argument structure, and emotional register.
- ☐ **Target platform format rules** – the specific narrative grammar for the platform I’m creating for (e.g., Twitter thread with cliffhangers; LinkedIn carousel with diagnostic questions; see Chapter 3 for full rules).
- ☐ **Editorial judgment** – I am the final authority. I review every draft and apply my human touch where it matters most: personal anecdotes, cultural references, and the final emotional polish.

### What the AI Executes (The Structural Adaptation Engine)
- ☐ **Structural adaptation** – rearranges my Hub components to match the target platform’s native format.
- ☐ **Voice compliance** – writes a first draft that follows my Voice Fingerprint rules as closely as possible.
- ☐ **First-draft generation only** – it does not publish, does not decide my opinions, does not override my constraints because it “thinks” something would sound better.

**The Pact, in one sentence:**  
“I provide the brief, the voice, and the final edit; the AI provides structurally sound, voice-compliant first drafts that I elevate with human specificity.”
