## TOOL 1: The Content Transformer Manifesto

**Purpose:** Your editorial constitution. A single page, in your own words, that declares who you are as a creator, how you work with AI, what rhythms you commit to, and what you're building toward. This is the document you read when the noise gets loud. When you're tempted to skip the sprint. When a new tool makes you question everything. When someone asks "how do you do what you do?" and you need an answer that's deeper than "I have a system."

**Time needed:** 45–60 minutes. Write it in one sitting. Don't overthink. Don't make it perfect. Make it *true*.
