A concrete plan to cement everything from this chapter.

- [ ] Complete your Content Transformer Manifesto (Section 1). Display it.
- [ ] Compute your Before‑and‑After Metrics (Section 5). Publish the most striking one in a post.
- [ ] Send one invitation for a Transformer Circle (Section 3.1) or find an accountability partner.

**Next 30 Days – Optimize & Connect**

**Following 30 Days – Teach & Reflect**
- [ ] Publish your Legacy Action piece.
- [ ] Conduct your first Quarterly Optimization Loop (even if you’re early). Use the kit in Section 2.
