## 7. AI Prompt Templates for Identity and Reflection

Use these prompts with your AI cocreator to help you draft your Manifesto, prepare for the Optimization Loop, or reflect on your journey.

### 7.1 Manifesto Drafting Assistant

```
I am a content creator who has fully implemented the Content Transformer system. I want to write a personal manifesto that captures my editorial philosophy, my AI collaboration rules, my non-negotiable creative rhythms, and my growth commitment.

Here are my answers to a few prompts:
- What I believe about repurposing: [paste your answers from Section 1A]
- My AI collaboration rules: [paste 1B]
- My rhythms: [paste 1C]
- My growth commitment: [paste 1D]

Please synthesize these into a 5–8 sentence manifesto that is bold, personal, and declarative. Write it in my voice (I will describe it below).

[PASTE YOUR VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK]

The tone should be confident, slightly poetic but not abstract, and grounded in real practice. Do not use corporate language.
```

After the AI generates a draft, edit it heavily. It’s a starting point—your own words are what matter. But the AI can help you see patterns and phrasings you might miss.

### 7.2 Quarterly Optimization Pre‑Audit Survey

Use this to generate a quick summary of what you should focus on before you dive into the manual audit.

```
I am about to do my quarterly Content Transformer optimization. Below is a summary of:
- Any voice drift or evolution I’ve noticed (or "none")
- Key platform changes or new features
- My current business goals and whether they’ve shifted
- The top and bottom performing pillar pieces from this quarter

Please suggest:
1. The single most important Voice Fingerprint adjustment I should consider.
2. One platform format rule that likely needs updating based on trends.
3. A possible reweighting of my Decision Matrix dimensions given my shifting goals.

My notes:
[PASTE YOUR OBSERVATIONS]

Keep your suggestions concise. I will make the final decisions.
```
