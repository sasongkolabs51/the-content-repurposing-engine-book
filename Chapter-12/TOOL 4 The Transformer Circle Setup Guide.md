## TOOL 4: The Transformer Circle Setup Guide

**Purpose:** Find or build your small group of fellow Content Transformers. The accountability, cross-pollination, and "you're not alone" that solo practice can't provide.

**Time needed:** 30 minutes to set up. 60 minutes per month for the meeting.

---

### Step 1: Find Your People

**Where to look:**

☐ Post your Before-and-After metrics publicly (Tool 2). Add: "If you've built a similar system, I'd love to compare notes. DM me."
☐ Share in creator communities you're already part of (Discord, Slack, Facebook groups, Twitter/X communities)
☐ Ask 2–3 creators you admire: "Have you systematized your repurposing? Want to compare approaches monthly?"
☐ Use a hashtag: #ContentTransformer or create your own
☐ Other: _______________

**What you're looking for:**

- 3–5 creators (not 20. Small is the point.)
- They've implemented a repurposing system (or are actively building one)
- They're generous with knowledge (not gatekeepers)
- They're at a similar stage (not beginners who need teaching, not gurus who perform)
- They'll be honest ("this prompt is mid" is more valuable than "everything's amazing!")

### Step 3: The Monthly Meeting Agenda

**60 minutes. Four segments. Keep it tight.**

| Time | Segment | What Happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | **Check-in** | One sentence per person: "This month, my system felt ___." (Honest. Not performative.) |
| 0:05–0:25 | **Prompt Swap** | Each person shares ONE prompt that worked well. Paste the actual text in the chat. Explain what it does and why it worked. (5 min per person.) |
| 0:25–0:40 | **Failure Autopsy** | Each person shares ONE thing that broke or underperformed. The group diagnoses together. "What constraint was missing?" (5 min per person.) |
| 0:40–0:55 | **One Adaptation** | Each person shares ONE change they made to their system this month. (3 min per person.) |
| 0:55–0:60 | **Next Month's Focus** | Each person states their One Percent improvement goal for next month. Accountability. |

---

### Step 4: The Sharing Protocol

**What we share:**

☐ Actual prompt text (not just "I tweaked my hook")
☐ Before-and-after outputs (the generic version vs. the magnetic version)
☐ Performance data (engagement rates, not just "it did well")
☐ System adaptations (new workflow steps, tool changes, format experiments)
☐ Failures and diagnoses (the most valuable thing you can share)

**What we don't do:**

☐ Perform. ("Everything's amazing!" when it's not.)
☐ Gatekeep. (If a prompt works, share it. Abundance, not scarcity.)
☐ Compare audiences. (10K followers and 500 followers are both valid. The system scales.)
☐ Make it a masterclass. (We're peers, not teacher-student.)

---

### Step 5: The First Meeting Invitation

*Copy, personalize, and send:*

> *"Hey [name],*
>
> *I've been building a content repurposing system over the last [X months]—Hub documents, AI prompt libraries, a 60-minute sprint, a decision matrix for prioritization. It's changed how I work. I went from [before metric] to [after metric].*
>
> *I'm putting together a small group (3–5 people) of creators who've done similar systematization work. The idea: meet once a month for an hour, swap prompts that worked, autopsy ones that didn't, and keep each other honest about maintaining the system.*
>
> *No selling. No performing. Just practitioners comparing notes.*
>
> *Would you be interested? First meeting would be [date/time].*
>
> *[Your name]"*
