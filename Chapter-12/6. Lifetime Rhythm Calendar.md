## 6. Lifetime Rhythm Calendar

**Purpose:** A visual overview of the recurring commitments that keep the flywheel spinning. Print this and check items off as you go.

### Weekly
- [ ] **Sprint (60 min):** __________ (day/time)
- [ ] **Monday Sprint Plan (10 min):** Choose this week’s pillar using the Decision Matrix.
- [ ] **Daily 5‑Minute Tune‑Up (weekdays):** Quick scan of today’s posts and one micro‑adjustment.

### Monthly (First Monday)
- [ ] **Exemplar Library Update (15 min):** Add top 3 posts. Rotate Reference Examples if needed.
- [ ] **Prompt Tweak (15 min):** One measurable improvement based on this month’s patterns.
- [ ] **Transformer Circle Meeting (60 min):** If active.
- [ ] **Monthly Voice Check (15 min):** Quick three‑marker audit on one platform.

### Quarterly (First week of Jan, Apr, Jul, Oct)
- [ ] **Optimization Loop (90 min):** Voice Calibration, Format Evolution, Matrix Refinement.
- [ ] **Quarterly External Audit (arrange with peer):** Send sample posts and a single question.

### Annually (On your Content Transformer anniversary)
- [ ] **Manifesto Review:** Reread and revise your Content Transformer Manifesto.
- [ ] **Before‑and‑After Metrics Update:** Add a new row to your tracker. Publish a celebratory post.
- [ ] **Legacy Reflection:** What did I teach this year? What will I teach next year?

**Calendar integration:** Transfer these into your digital calendar with recurring events and reminders. Color‑code: blue for creation, green for review, orange for connection (Circle).
