## TOOL 8: The "Who I Am Now" Identity Anchor Card

**Purpose:** A small card (physical or digital) that you read when you forget who you've become. When the old identity whispers "you're just someone who found a hack." When imposter syndrome says "you're not a real creator if AI helps you." When the noise says "you should be doing more, faster, louder." You read the card. You remember.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes to write. 30 seconds to read.

---
