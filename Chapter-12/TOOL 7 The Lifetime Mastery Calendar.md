## TOOL 7: The Lifetime Mastery Calendar

**Purpose:** The full annual rhythm map. Every ritual, every review, every loop, mapped across 12 months. Print it. Tape it to your wall. This is the operating system for the next year (and every year after).

---

### THE ANNUAL RHYTHM

```
DAILY (every workday):
  └─ 5-min Tune-Up: 3 questions. 1 micro-adjustment.

WEEKLY:
  └─ Hub-Building Block: 30 min (after pillar creation)
  └─ Sprint Window: 60 min (protected calendar block)
  └─ Weekly Data Whisper: 10 min (end of publishing week)
  └─ One Percent Improvement: 1 change, logged

MONTHLY (1st Monday):
  └─ Monthly Voice Check: 20 min
  └─ Monthly Evolution Ritual: 90 min
      ├─ Review top 3 posts → Exemplar Library
      ├─ Update 1 prompt template
      ├─ Transformer Circle meeting (60 min)
      └─ Log in Progress Tracker

QUARTERLY (1st week of Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct):
  └─ Quarterly Optimization Loop: 90 min
      ├─ Station 1: Voice Calibration
      ├─ Station 2: Format Evolution
      └─ Station 3: Matrix Refinement

ANNUALLY (system anniversary):
  └─ Annual Manifesto Review: 60 min
      ├─ Re-read Manifesto. Revise.
      ├─ Compute annual metrics.
      ├─ Publish Before-and-After update.
      └─ Set next year's growth commitment.
```
