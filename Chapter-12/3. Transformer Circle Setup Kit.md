## 3. Transformer Circle Setup Kit

**Purpose:** Create or strengthen a small, committed group of fellow Content Transformers for accountability, prompt sharing, and mutual growth.

### 3.1 Invitation Template

*Use this (customized) to reach out to 1–4 creators you respect who are also implementing AI‑enabled editorial systems.*

> Hi [name],  
> I’ve been building a content repurposing system based on the Content Transformer approach—Hub, Voice Fingerprint, AI co‑creator, 60‑minute sprints. I’m looking to start a tiny “Transformer Circle”: 3–5 people who meet once a month for 60 minutes to swap one killer prompt, one that flopped (and why), and one system tweak.  
> The goal is accountability and faster learning. No posturing, no big group. Would you be interested in trying it for a quarter? If at least one other person says yes, I’ll set up the first call.  
> No pressure—just thought of you because [genuine reason].  
> Warmly,  
> [Your name]

### 3.2 Circle Meeting Agenda Template (60 minutes)

**Frequency:** Monthly. **Format:** Video call. Shared notes doc for prompts.

- **0–5 min:** Check‑in. Each person shares one sentence: “My biggest content win or struggle this month.”
- **5–25 min:** Prompt Spotlight. Each person shares one prompt that worked exceptionally well. Paste it in the shared doc. Briefly explain why it worked (hook pattern? conditional logic? exemplar?). One question from the group allowed.
- **25–40 min:** Flop Clinic. Each person shares one prompt or asset that underperformed. The group helps diagnose (use the Failure Diagnostic Cards from Ch. 5 and 8). Note the fix in the doc.
- **40–55 min:** System Tweaks. Each person shares one adaptation they made to their Hub, sprint, or workflow this month. Rapid‑fire—2 min each.
- **55–60 min:** Commitments & next call. Each person states one thing they’ll improve before next meeting. Confirm next date.

**Shared resources:** A single running document with dated entries for prompts and fixes.

### 3.3 If You Don’t Have a Circle Yet

- [ ] Write and publish your “Before‑and‑After Metrics” post (see Section 5). End the post with: “If you’re doing similar work, I’d love to hear from you.”
- [ ] Comment genuinely on posts by creators who talk about AI, repurposing, or editorial workflows. Over time, DM the ones you resonate with and propose a small mastermind.
- [ ] Start with a “circle of two”—just you and one accountability partner. Even that multiplies your growth.
