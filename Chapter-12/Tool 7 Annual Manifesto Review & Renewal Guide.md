## Tool 7: Annual Manifesto Review & Renewal Guide

**Purpose:** To ensure your Manifesto stays true to who you are becoming, and to quantify your growth each year. This ritual closes the loop on Chapter 12 and opens the next chapter of your journey.

**Instructions:** On the anniversary of when you wrote your Manifesto (or started implementing this system), set aside an hour. Re‑read your original Manifesto, review your Progress Evolution Tracker (Chapter 11, Tool 9), and answer the reflection questions.

### Annual Review Checklist

- [ ] Read my original Manifesto aloud. What still resonates deeply? What feels slightly off or outdated?
- [ ] Review my Progress Evolution Tracker (or Before‑and‑After Metrics Tracker). What are the year’s key numbers?
  - Total pillar pieces produced: _______
  - Average sprint time: _______ minutes
  - Total repurposed assets published: _______
  - Platforms consistently active on: _______
  - Any measurable audience growth or revenue attributable to the system: _______
- [ ] What’s the single biggest improvement I made to my system this year? (Check One Percent Rule log)
- [ ] How has my voice, audience, or business goal evolved? Does my Voice Fingerprint and Decision Matrix need a major update?
- [ ] Revise the Manifesto if needed. Add a new section if your philosophy has deepened (e.g., “On Teaching,” “On AI Ethics,” “On Rest”).
- [ ] Set one intention for the coming year—not a metric, but a direction: “This year, I want my content to be more ______.”
