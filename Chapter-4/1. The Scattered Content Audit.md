## 1. The Scattered Content Audit

**Purpose:** Expose exactly how many different documents, transcripts, and half-finished notes you’re currently working from. Naming the chaos is the first step toward replacing it.

**Instructions:** Think about your last pillar piece (a video, long-form blog, or podcast episode). List every separate location where relevant material currently lives. Check all that apply.

- [ ] Raw transcript (Descript, Otter, Rev, etc.)
- [ ] Rough show notes or bullet points (Notion, Apple Notes, Google Keep)
- [ ] A partially written blog draft
- [ ] A social media scheduler queue with some ideas already pasted
- [ ] Voice memos or recorded rants that didn’t make it into the final piece
- [ ] A DM or Slack thread where you talked about the idea
- [ ] A whiteboard photo or mind map
- [ ] Physical notebook pages
- [ ] Other: ______________________________

Now, estimate how many minutes you typically spend searching for the right quote, stat, or story when you repurpose a pillar piece: _____ minutes.

**Reflection:**  
If you had all of that material extracted and labeled in one place, what would it free up?
