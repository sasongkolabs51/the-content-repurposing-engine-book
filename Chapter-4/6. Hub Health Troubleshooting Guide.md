## 6. Hub Health Troubleshooting Guide

When something doesn’t feel right later—a prompt produces weak output, or you can’t quickly find what you need—use this diagnostic card.

| Symptom | Likely Issue | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| AI output feels shallow, no stories | Key Stories block is too sparse or just says “story about CEO” | Expand each story into a full narrative paragraph with setup, conflict, resolution |
| LinkedIn carousel stats feel generic or unsourced | Data Points block missing attribution or full context | Rewrite each data point as a complete sentence with source |
| Posts don’t spark debate or comments | Objections block is empty or uses weak strawman arguments | Brainstorm the *real* pushback someone would have; use language they’d actually use |
| AI-generated hooks feel bland, not quotable | Quotable Lines block has no lines, or lines are too long | Distill each line to under 15 words; read them aloud for rhythm |
| You keep going back to the transcript because Hub feels incomplete | You rushed the extraction; blocks are placeholders | Schedule a 15-minute “Hub tune-up” session and fill gaps before next repurposing sprint |
| AI mixes blocks or ignores structure | Headings are not clear or not used consistently | Use ## or ### heading marks; keep block titles exactly consistent across all Hubs |
