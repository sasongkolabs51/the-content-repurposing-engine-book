## Tool 4: Hub Integration Checklist (Voice Fingerprint & Platform Playbook)

**Purpose:** To wire your Hub into the larger system you’ve been building, so that your prompts in Chapter 5 can pull all three components—content, voice, format—without friction.

**Instructions:** Go through this checklist once for your Hub template (so it’s baked into every future Hub) and once for the first Hub you populated.

### Integration Checklist

- [ ] **Voice Fingerprint linked:** At the top or bottom of my Hub document, there is a clickable link to my Voice Fingerprint from Chapter 2. (Or I embedded the fingerprint as a collapsed section.)
- [ ] **Platform‑Format Playbook linked:** Right next to the voice link, I have a clickable link to my Platform‑Format Playbook from Chapter 3.
- [ ] **Hub headings match my planned prompt structure:** The heading names (e.g., “Core Thesis”) are exactly what I’ll reference when I say “extract the Core Thesis and Quotable Lines” in an AI prompt.
- [ ] **No stray notes in the blocks:** Any “notes to self” have been moved to a separate section at the end labeled “Editor’s Comments” (which I can instruct the AI to ignore).
- [ ] **One test prompt run:** I copied a block from my Hub, opened my AI tool, and gave it a quick instruction (e.g., “Rewrite this Core Thesis in my voice, using the attached Voice Fingerprint, as a Twitter thread hook”). Did the output respect the structure? (Yes/No – if No, I need to check my Voice Fingerprint formatting.)
