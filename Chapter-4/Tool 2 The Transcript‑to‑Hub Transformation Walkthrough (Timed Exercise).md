## Tool 2: The Transcript‑to‑Hub Transformation Walkthrough (Timed Exercise)

**Purpose:** To prove to yourself that you can transform a raw transcript into a full Hub in under 30 minutes—and to make the process physical so it becomes a reflex.

**Instructions:**  
1. Select your most recent pillar piece and open its transcript or rough notes.  
2. Duplicate the Master Hub Template (Tool 1) and rename it with your pillar title.  
3. Set a timer for 30 minutes.  
4. Work through the five blocks in order. Don’t edit as you go; just capture.  
5. When the timer goes off, stop. Note how far you got and any friction points.
