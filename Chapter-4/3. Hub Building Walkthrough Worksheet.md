## 3. Hub Building Walkthrough Worksheet

**Purpose:** A guided, step-by-step process to transform a raw transcript or rough notes into a fully populated Hub. Use this the first few times until the five-block extraction becomes automatic.

**Instructions:** Have your raw source material open (transcript, blog draft, show notes). Work through one block at a time. Time yourself—aim for 25–30 minutes total for your first Hub.

### Block 1: Core Thesis

*Your goal is to answer: “If I had to summarize my entire argument in a few sentences, without any storytelling, what would I say?”*

- Scan your transcript or notes. Look for the moment where you clearly state the main idea.  
- Write a 2–4 sentence draft. Don’t wordsmith yet. Get the logic down.

### Block 3: Data Points & Research

*Your goal: every stat, study, or external reference, written as a complete, standalone sentence.*

- Scan for numbers, percentages, study names, quoted authorities.  
- Write each as a bullet: full sentence, include attribution even if informal (e.g., “A 2011 study by… found that…” or “According to researcher X…”).

*Your goal: surface the resistance you addressed—or should address.*

- Ask: “What would someone who disagrees with my thesis say?” and “What fears or doubts might stop someone from implementing this?”
