## TOOL 2: The Transcript-to-Hub Conversion Worksheet

**Purpose:** A guided, step-by-step worksheet for building your first Hub from an actual transcript. This is the "hold your hand through the first rep" document. After you've done it once, you won't need it. But the first time, having the prompts in front of you prevents the "stare at the transcript for twenty minutes" freeze.

**Time needed:** 30–40 minutes for your first Hub. (Gets to 20 minutes by the third one.)

**What you need:** Your most recent pillar piece's transcript (or raw notes), the Master Hub Template (Tool 1) open in a second tab/window, and a timer.

### Step 1: Core Thesis (5 minutes)

**The prompt to yourself:** *If someone who never saw this content asked me, "What was the one argument you made?"—what would I say in 2–4 plain sentences?*

**Don't:** Try to be clever. Write a hook. Summarize the whole video chronologically.

**Do:** State the claim. State why it matters. State the implication or solution.

### Step 4: Audience Objections & Counterarguments (5 minutes)

**The prompt to yourself:** *If a smart, skeptical person watched this content, what would they push back on? What's the "yeah, but…" they'd mutter under their breath?*

**Common objection types:**
- "This doesn't apply to MY situation because…"
- "Isn't this just [simpler thing] repackaged?"
- "What about [competing value]?"
- "This sounds great in theory, but in practice…"
- "Who is this actually for? Because it's not for me."

### Step 6: Transfer to Master Template (2 minutes)

Copy everything you just wrote into the Master Hub Template (Tool 1). Match the headings exactly. Keep the formatting conventions.

**Done? Timer check:** _____ minutes elapsed.

**If you're over 35 minutes:** Note where you got stuck. That's your bottleneck for next time.
**If you're under 25 minutes:** You're a natural. Or you under-wrote the stories. Check Step 2.
