## 7. AI Prompt Template: Hub Builder Assistant

**Purpose:** Use your AI cocreator to dramatically speed up Hub building. This prompt turns a raw transcript into a first draft of all five blocks, which you then refine. It respects the Cocreator Pact—you provide the creative direction and final judgment.

**When to use:** You have a transcript (from Descript, Otter, etc.) and want a head start on your Hub.

```
You are my editorial assistant under our Cocreator Pact. I will provide a raw transcript from my pillar content. Your task is to extract and organize it into a structured Hub using the five blocks below. Do not add any external information or opinions. Work only from the transcript.

The five blocks to extract:

1. Core Thesis (2-4 sentences): What is the central argument? Distill it clearly.

2. Key Stories & Anecdotes: For each story or example in the transcript, write a self-contained paragraph capturing setup, conflict, and resolution. Label them as Story 1, Story 2, etc.

3. Data Points & Research: List every statistic, study, or external reference as a complete, attributed sentence. Use bullet points.

4. Audience Objections & Counterarguments: Identify any objections the speaker addresses or implies. Write each as an objection statement followed by the response.

5. Quotable Lines & Hooks: Extract or synthesize the most punchy, memorable sentences—one per line.

Format your output using these exact headings:
## Core Thesis
## Key Stories & Anecdotes
## Data Points & Research
## Audience Objections & Counterarguments
## Quotable Lines & Hooks

Transcript:
[Paste your transcript here]
```

After the AI generates the draft, move it into your Hub template. Then spend 10–15 minutes editing: tighten the thesis, fill in any missed stories, ensure data points have attribution, and sharpen the quotable lines. The AI did the heavy extraction; you add the editorial finesse.
