## 9. Editorial Director Hub Affirmation

Use this as a pre-session anchor before you build a new Hub or before a repurposing sprint. Write it on a sticky note, or read it aloud.

> I am an editorial architect, not an archaeologist.  
> I don’t dig through transcripts—I build structured containers for my ideas.  
> My Hub is the permanent home of my intellectual property.  
> Every minute I invest in building it now saves me hours of reconstructing later.  
> Today, I assemble; I don’t scramble.
