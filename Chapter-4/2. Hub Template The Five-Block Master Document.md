**Instructions:** Copy the following template into your preferred tool (Notion, Google Docs, Craft, Obsidian—anything that supports clear headings). Do not fill it in yet; that comes next.

### Hub: [Title of Pillar Piece]

*Distill the central argument into 2–4 declarative sentences. No hooks, no fluff—just the clear claim the entire piece supports.*

*List each story or example as a self-contained paragraph capturing the setup, conflict, and resolution. One story per bullet or numbered item.*

**Data Points & Research**
*Capture every statistic, study, or external reference as a complete, attributed sentence. Use bullets for quick scanning.*

**Audience Objections & Counterarguments**
*Surface the arguments a skeptical reader would raise. For each, include the objection and a concise response.*

**Quotable Lines & Hooks**
*Pull out the most punchy, tweetable, overlay-able sentences. One per line. These are your raw material for platform hooks.*
