## TOOL 8: The Hub-Building Troubleshooting Guide

**Purpose:** A quick-reference for the five most common problems that show up during Hub construction. When you're stuck, scan this list before you spiral.

---

### Problem 1: "I can't distill the Core Thesis. It's too nuanced."

**The symptom:** You write 8 sentences. Then 12. Then you delete them all and start over. Twenty minutes pass.

**The fix:** You're confusing the *thesis* with the *content*. The thesis is the CLAIM. The content is the PROOF. You don't need to include the proof in the thesis block.

**Try this sentence stem:** "The argument of this piece is that _______________, and the implication is _______________."

Two sentences. Done. The stories, data, and objections in the other blocks ARE the nuance. The thesis is the spine they hang on.

---

### Problem 2: "My stories feel flat when I summarize them."

**The symptom:** You write "the story about the CEO who batched her decisions" and it reads like a Wikipedia entry.

**The fix:** You're summarizing the *plot* but losing the *moment*. Every story has one specific image or line of dialogue that makes it feel real. Find that.

**Try this structure:**
- Setup: Who, where, when (1 sentence)
- The specific moment: The image, the quote, the detail that makes it a SCENE (1–2 sentences)
- Resolution: What changed (1 sentence)

Example:
- ❌ "A CEO started batching her decisions and saved time."
- ✅ "Sarah, a Series B founder, was making 40+ micro-decisions before 9am—what to wear, what to eat, which Slack thread to answer first. One Sunday, she laid out five days of outfits, prepped every lunch, and wrote a 'decision menu' for her team. By Wednesday, she'd reclaimed ninety minutes of morning focus she didn't know she'd lost."

The second version has a *scene*. You can see Sarah. You can see the outfits laid out. That's what makes it usable in a carousel or a TikTok script.

---

### Problem 3: "I don't have enough data points."

**The symptom:** Your Data Points block has one entry, or none. You feel like the Hub is incomplete.

**The fix:** Two options.

Option A: You don't need data for every Hub. If your pillar is story-driven or opinion-driven, the Data Points block might have one entry or say "N/A — this pillar is narrative-based." That's fine. Not every spoke needs a statistic.

Option B: Go find one. Spend 5 minutes Googling one relevant study or statistic that supports your thesis. Add it with full attribution. You don't need five. You need one that's solid.

**The rule:** The Hub captures what's IN the content. If you didn't cite data in the video, the block is short. You can add to it later, but don't fabricate entries.

---

### Problem 4: "I can't think of audience objections."

**The symptom:** You stare at the Objections block. You think, "Well, nobody would really disagree with this." (They would. They always do.)

**The fix:** Try these prompts:

- "What would a smart 25-year-old skeptic say after watching this?"
- "What's the 'yeah, but what about…' that a friend would raise over dinner?"
- "If someone wanted to write a rebuttal tweet, what would it say?"
- "What's the version of this advice that DOESN'T apply to someone's specific situation?"
- "What's the 'this is just [simpler thing] repackaged' objection?"

**The minimum:** Two objections. Even for the most "obvious" content. If you truly can't find two, your thesis might be too safe. (That's a content problem, not a Hub problem.)

---

### Problem 5: "This is taking way longer than 30 minutes."

**The symptom:** You're 50 minutes in and still on the Stories block. You're rewriting sentences. You're going back to the transcript to "get it right."

**The fix:** You're editing, not extracting. The Hub is not a publication. It's an internal document. The stories don't need to be *beautiful*. They need to be *complete*. The quotable lines don't need to be *perfect*. They need to be *present*.

**The rule:** Write it at 80%. Ship it. You'll refine it when you build the first spoke and notice what's missing. The Hub is a living document. It gets better with use, not with a single heroic writing session.

**Set a hard timer:** 30 minutes. When it goes off, you're done. Whatever state the Hub is in, that's the state. You can add to it later. But you cannot keep polishing.
