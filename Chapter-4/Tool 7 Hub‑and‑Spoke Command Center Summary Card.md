## Tool 7: Hub‑and‑Spoke Command Center Summary Card

Cut out or screenshot this card. Pin it above your workspace or save it in your project folder until the five‑block pattern becomes second nature.

**The Hub in Five Blocks**

- **Core Thesis:** 2–4 declarative sentences. The single argument everything else serves.
- **Key Stories & Anecdotes:** Self‑contained narrative paragraphs. Setup, tension, resolution.
- **Data Points & Research:** Standalone stats with attribution. Your evidence bank.
- **Audience Objections:** What the skeptic says, plus your response. The persuasion engine.
- **Quotable Lines:** Punchy, tweetable sentences. The raw material for every hook.

**The Golden Rule:** Do the decomposition once, in a structured document, and never again. Repurposing becomes assembly, not archaeology.

**For the AI:** Use consistent headings, bullet lists for data and quotes, and no notes‑to‑self inside the blocks. Link your Voice Fingerprint and Platform Playbook at the top.
