## TOOL 3: The Quick-Access Test Card

**Purpose:** Verify that your Hub actually works as a retrieval system. If you can't find what you need in 60 seconds, the Hub's structure needs adjustment.

**Time needed:** 3 minutes per test. Run it after every new Hub build.

---

### Test Date: _______________ Hub Title: _______________

**The scenario:** You're about to build a LinkedIn carousel from this Hub. You need three components. Find them. Timer starts NOW.

| Find This | Time to Locate | Where It Was (block + line) | Found It? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. A statistic or data point that supports the thesis | _____ sec | | ☐ Yes ☐ No |
| 2. A story with a clear narrative arc (setup → tension → resolution) | _____ sec | | ☐ Yes ☐ No |
| 3. A quotable line that could be a carousel slide headline | _____ sec | | ☐ Yes ☐ No |

**Total time:** _____ seconds

**Pass threshold:** Under 60 seconds for all three.

---

### If You Failed (took over 60 seconds or couldn't find something):

**Diagnosis:**

☐ The block headings aren't clear enough → Rename or reformat
☐ The stories are too vague / under-written → Expand to full narrative paragraphs
☐ The data points aren't complete sentences → Rewrite with full attribution
☐ The quotable lines are buried in paragraphs → Pull them into a clean bullet list
☐ There's too much extra content in the Hub → Remove tangents, notes-to-self, raw transcript fragments
☐ The document is too long / cluttered → Add white space between blocks

**The fix I'll make right now:**

_______________________________________________________________

**Re-test after fix:** _____ seconds. ☐ Pass ☐ Still failing → Deeper restructure needed.

---

### Bonus Test: The AI Retrieval Check

Paste this into your AI tool with your Hub document attached:

```
I'm attaching my Hub document. Without summarizing the whole thing,
answer these three questions using ONLY the information in the Hub:

1. What is the core thesis in one sentence?
2. Give me the second story listed, including its narrative arc.
3. What is the first audience objection and the counter to it?

If you can't find any of these clearly, tell me which one is unclear
and why.
```

**Did the AI retrieve all three correctly?** ☐ Yes ☐ No → Which failed: _______________

**If the AI failed, the formatting is the problem.** Fix the heading structure or bullet formatting and re-test.
