## TOOL 1: The Master Hub Template

**Purpose:** This is the document you duplicate for every pillar piece. It's your permanent editorial container. Build it once in your tool of choice (Notion, Google Docs, Obsidian, whatever you actually use daily), save it as a template, and never start from a blank page again.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes to set up. Then 20–30 minutes per pillar piece to populate.

---

### Setup Instructions

1. Open your preferred document tool.
2. Create a new document titled: **"HUB TEMPLATE — [Duplicate for Each Pillar Piece]"**
3. Copy the structure below exactly. Keep the headings, the prompt-to-yourself notes, and the formatting conventions.
4. Save as a template (in Notion: "Save as template"; in Google Docs: File → Make a copy; in Obsidian: save in a Templates folder).
5. For each new pillar piece, duplicate this template and rename it: **"HUB — [Pillar Title] — [Date]"**

### Formatting Conventions (Read Before First Use)

These aren't optional. They're what make the Hub readable by both you and your AI.

| Convention | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Use `##` headings for the five blocks exactly as written | AI parses section boundaries through headings. If you rename them or bury them in prose, the AI loses the structure. |
| Each data point and quotable line gets its own bullet (`-`) | Prevents the AI from concatenating separate items into one blob. |
| Stories are full paragraphs, separated by white space | Gives the AI enough narrative context to retell the story without the source transcript. |
| Objections use the **They say / I counter** format | Creates a clean, parseable structure the AI can pull from when building "tension" beats in threads or carousels. |
| The "One-line version" under Core Thesis is always present | Gives you (and the AI) a quick-reference anchor without re-reading the full paragraph. |
| No tangents, no notes-to-self, no unedited conversation in the five blocks | The Hub is a polished input document. Raw material goes in the source file. The Hub is the refined extract. |
