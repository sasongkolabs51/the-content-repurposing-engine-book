## TOOL 3: The Hub-and-Spoke Thought Experiment

**Purpose:** Prove to yourself that your content is already modular—you just haven't decomposed it yet. Build the neural pathway for the Hub structure before you build the actual document.

**Time needed:** 20 minutes

**Instructions:** Pick one pillar piece you've already published. Do NOT open the transcript, the blog draft, or the video. Work from memory. If you get stuck, that's the data point.
