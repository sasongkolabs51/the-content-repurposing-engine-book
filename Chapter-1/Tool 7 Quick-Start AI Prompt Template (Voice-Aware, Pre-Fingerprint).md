## Tool 7: Quick-Start AI Prompt Template (Voice-Aware, Pre-Fingerprint)

**Purpose:** You don’t have a full Voice Fingerprint yet (that’s Chapter 2), but you can already give an AI enough of your voice mechanics to stop it from sounding generic. Use this template whenever you ask an AI to help you draft a Spoke.

**Instructions:** Copy the template below and fill in the bracketed details from your Voice-as-System Inventory (Tool 4). Then paste the whole thing at the start of a ChatGPT or Claude conversation, right before you give the specific task.

---

*You are my editorial assistant. I’m going to give you a Hub (a structured set of intellectual components) and ask you to draft a platform-specific asset. I need you to write in a voice that matches the following mechanics. Do not use generic corporate language. Follow these patterns closely:*

- *Opening sentence style: [e.g., “start with a short, concrete scene that creates a curiosity gap”]*
- *Sentence length and rhythm: [e.g., “mostly short sentences, 10–15 words, with occasional longer ones for emphasis”]*
- *Transitions: [e.g., “use direct questions like ‘But what does that actually mean?’ or sharp turns like ‘Here’s the uncomfortable truth.’”]*
- *Phrases I rely on: [e.g., “the truth is,” “you know the feeling,” “here’s the thing”]*
- *Argument structure: [e.g., “problem → insight → action, ending with a reflective question”]*
- *Emotional register: [e.g., “warm but direct, no sarcasm, occasional earnestness”]*

*Now, here’s the Hub and the specific Spoke I need…*

---

This template is a bridge. It won’t be perfect, but it will already produce output that sounds far more like you than any out-of-the-box AI text. In Chapter 2, you’ll build the complete Voice Fingerprint and the Authentic AI Co-Creator Pact that makes this collaboration airtight.
