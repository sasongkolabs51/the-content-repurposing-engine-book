## 2. Platform‑Skipping Pattern Reveal Worksheet

**Purpose:** Translate “I just didn’t get to it” into a tangible cost—lost audience growth, missed revenue, or diminished authority. This makes the economic weight of your current mental model impossible to ignore.

**Instructions:**  
List every platform you consider “active” (even if you skip it often). For the last six weeks, estimate how many times you intended to post but didn’t because adaptation took too long or felt overwhelming. Then estimate what that absence might have cost you in followers, leads, or sales. Rough numbers are perfect.
