## Tool 5: Identity Installation Checklist

**Purpose:** To move the Editorial Director identity from intellectual understanding to daily practice. Print this, stick it on your wall, or keep it in a digital note that you see every morning before you create.

**My New Operating Assumptions (Check when internalized)**

- [ ] I am an Editorial Director, not a content factory worker. I measure my day by the coherence and distinctiveness of audience experiences, not by raw post count.
- [ ] Repurposing is editorial transformation—re-architecting a single idea into platform-native narratives. Distribution thinking is the enemy of growth.
- [ ] The Hub-and-Spoke model is my architecture. I invest in building a structured Hub once, so I can assemble Spokes in minutes, not hours.
- [ ] My voice is not a fragile mystery; it is a teachable system of patterns. I can extract those patterns and use them to guide any tool or collaborator.
- [ ] Skipping a platform isn’t a time-management failure; it’s a signal that I haven’t yet built the Spoke workflow for that platform. I’ll design it, not avoid it.
- [ ] AI is not a threat to my authenticity when I provide the creative brief. It’s an accelerator that handles structural adaptation, freeing me to make high-level editorial choices and add the human moments.

**Daily Micro-Practice**  
Each morning this week, before you open a content draft, read this checklist aloud and pick one assumption to consciously operate from for the day. At the end of the day, jot one sentence about how that shift changed your decisions (or your stress level).
