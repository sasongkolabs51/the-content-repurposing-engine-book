## Tool 2: The Platform-Skipping Pattern Reveal

**Purpose:** To make the real cost of the distribution mindset tangible. When you attach numbers—even rough ones—to the platforms you’ve been abandoning, the belief that “repurposing is just a logistics chore” starts to lose its grip.

**Instructions:** List every platform you actively maintain (or intend to maintain). For each, note the number of times in the last six weeks you skipped posting because adaptation took too long or felt creatively painful. Then estimate the audience growth, engagement, or revenue you believe you lost by being absent during those windows.

**Reflection:**
- If I could reclaim even 50% of that lost opportunity by spending *one focused hour a week* on transformation instead of manual rewriting, what would that mean for my business?
- Which platform absence hurts the most, and why? That’s your first candidate for a Spoke workflow.
