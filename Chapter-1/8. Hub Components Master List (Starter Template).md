## 8. Hub Components Master List (Starter Template)

When you eventually build a full Hub (Chapter 4 will walk you through it in detail), these are the modular building blocks you’ll extract from your pillar content. Use this list now to practice identifying components in your existing pieces.

**Core intellectual assets:**
- [ ] Primary thesis (the single, defendable claim)
- [ ] Counter‑argument or common misconception you’re debunking
- [ ] Supporting story #1 (personal anecdote)
- [ ] Supporting story #2 (client/case study)
- [ ] Data point or research citation
- [ ] Metaphor or analogy that makes the idea sticky
- [ ] Audience objection and your response
- [ ] Quotable one‑liner (the “tweetable” line)
- [ ] Actionable framework or steps

**Emotional / tonal components:**
- [ ] Vulnerability moment (where you admit a past failure)
- [ ] Humor beat (the funny aside that breaks tension)
- [ ] High‑energy “call to arms” paragraph
- [ ] Quiet reflective moment

Having these clearly separated is what makes the AI prompts in Section 6 work. Instead of feeding a messy transcript, you’ll drop the relevant modules into the prompt and get a platform‑native draft in seconds.
