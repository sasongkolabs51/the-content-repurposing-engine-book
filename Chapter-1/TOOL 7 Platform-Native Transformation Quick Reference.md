## TOOL 7: Platform-Native Transformation Quick Reference

**Purpose:** A one-page cheat sheet you tape above your desk. When you're building a spoke, glance at this to make sure you're actually *transforming*, not just trimming.

---

### Twitter / X Thread

| Element | Distribution Move (DON'T) | Transformation Move (DO) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (Tweet 1) | Paste the video title or first sentence | Write a pattern interrupt: a contrarian claim, a "you're doing X wrong" frame, or a curiosity gap that only makes sense in text |
| Structure | Chop the transcript into tweet-sized chunks | Build a tension arc: problem → escalation → insight → proof → reflective close |
| Pacing | One idea per tweet, uniform rhythm | Vary sentence length. Short punch. Then a longer explanation. Then a one-word tweet for emphasis. |
| Close | "Watch the full video 👇" | End on a question the reader sits with. Make the last tweet feel like the exhale. |

### LinkedIn Carousel / Post

| Element | Distribution Move (DON'T) | Transformation Move (DO) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (Slide 1 / Line 1) | "In my latest video, I talk about…" | A professional pain point stated as a diagnostic question: "When's the last time you…?" or a surprising professional reframe |
| Structure | Summarize the video in bullet points | Build a narrative arc: relatable mistake → why it happens → the reframe → 2–3 diagnostic questions → takeaway |
| Tone | Same as your video script | Slightly more "peer-to-peer professional." You're a colleague sharing insight at a conference, not a YouTuber talking to subscribers |
| Close | "Link in comments" | A soft engagement prompt: "Which of these three is your blind spot?" or "I'd genuinely love to hear how you handle this." |

### TikTok / Reels / Shorts

| Element | Distribution Move (DON'T) | Transformation Move (DO) |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–1.5 sec) | Start talking like you do on YouTube | A VISUAL hook first: a prop, a text overlay, a physical gesture, a jump cut mid-sentence. The viewer's thumb stops because of what they SEE, not what they hear |
| Structure | Talk through your three main points | One idea. One reframe. Delivered with energy and a single visual metaphor. If you need more than 45 seconds, you haven't distilled enough |
| Delivery | Same cadence as your long-form content | Faster. More direct address. Body language does 30% of the work. Lean in. Use your hands. Break the fourth wall |
| Close | "Follow for more" | A one-line reframe that lands like a mic drop, OR a question that makes them rewatch |

### Newsletter / Email

| Element | Distribution Move (DON'T) | Transformation Move (DO) |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | "Hey, new video is up!" | A personal, unscripted-feeling observation that connects to the week's theme. You're writing a letter, not a press release |
| Body | Summarize the video with timestamps | Add original commentary you DIDN'T put in the video. The "director's cut" thinking. The aside. The thing you cut for time |
| Structure | Linear summary | Thematic. Pull one story from the Hub, add your reflection, connect it to something the reader is experiencing THIS week |
| Close | "Watch here: [link]" | A genuine question or invitation. Make the reply feel possible. "Hit reply and tell me if this landed or if I'm way off." |
