## TOOL 10: The 30-Day Implementation Roadmap

**Purpose:** A step-by-step plan to move from "I read Chapter 1 and felt inspired" to "I actually operate differently." Because inspiration without a schedule is just entertainment.

---

### Week 1: See the Old System Clearly

- [ ] Complete the **Distribution-vs-Transformation Audit** (Tool 1) for your last 3 pillar pieces
- [ ] Complete the **Platform-Skipping Pattern Reveal** (Tool 2)
- [ ] Sit with the numbers. Don't fix anything yet. Just let the data land.
- [ ] Write your ratio on a sticky note. Put it on your monitor. (e.g., "90/10")
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~60 minutes total across the week

### Week 2: Build Your First Hub

- [ ] Complete the **Hub-and-Spoke Thought Experiment** (Tool 3) from memory for one old piece
- [ ] Then build a **full Hub Document** (Tool 6) for your NEXT pillar piece, before you create any derived assets
- [ ] Time the Hub build. Write down how long it took: _______ minutes
- [ ] Create ONE spoke (pick your easiest platform) using the Hub. Notice the difference.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes total

### Week 3: Catalog Your Voice

- [ ] Complete the **Voice-as-System Inventory** (Tool 4) with your 3 best posts
- [ ] Write your **5-Sentence Voice Summary**
- [ ] Run the **Editorial Director Identity Self-Assessment** (Tool 5). Note your score.
- [ ] Tape the **Platform-Native Transformation Quick Reference** (Tool 7) above your desk or set it as a desktop background
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~50 minutes total

### Week 4: Run the Full System Once

- [ ] Fill out the **Weekly Editorial Director Planning Sheet** (Tool 9) on Monday
- [ ] Build the Hub for your pillar piece (30 min)
- [ ] Use at least TWO of the **AI Prompt Templates** (Tool 8) to draft spokes
- [ ] Run the **"Am I Actually Transforming?" Quality Check** (Prompt E) on each AI draft before personalizing
- [ ] Publish a full spoke set from one Hub
- [ ] Complete the Friday **End-of-Week Check**
- [ ] Re-run the **Identity Self-Assessment** (Tool 5). Compare to Week 3 score.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~2.5 hours across the week (but it replaces the 4+ hours you were spending before)

---

### The 30-Day "Did It Stick?" Test

At the end of Day 30, answer these three questions honestly:

1. When you finished a pillar piece this month, was your first instinct to open the transcript and start cutting, or to build the Hub?

   _______________________________________________________________

2. Did you skip a platform this month? If yes, was it because the system failed, or because you fell back into the old pattern?

   _______________________________________________________________

3. Can you describe your voice in three mechanical sentences to another person (or an AI) without saying "it's just… a vibe"?

   _______________________________________________________________

> *If you can answer those three questions with evidence of the new system, you've installed the operating system. Everything in Parts 2–4 now has soil to grow in. If the answers are shaky, that's fine. Go back to the tool that corresponds to the gap and do it again. The system isn't a one-time event. It's a practice.*
