## TOOL 1: The Distribution-vs-Transformation Audit

**Purpose:** See, in black and white, how much of your "repurposing" has actually been mechanical redistribution. No judgment. Just data.

**Time needed:** 25–35 minutes

**What you need:** Your last three pillar content pieces (videos, blog posts, podcast episodes) and every social/derived asset you created from each one.
