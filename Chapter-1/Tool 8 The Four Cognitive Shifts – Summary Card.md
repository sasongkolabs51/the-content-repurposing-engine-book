## Tool 8: The Four Cognitive Shifts – Summary Card

Cut out or screenshot this card. Tape it to your monitor, keep it in your notebook, or set it as your phone wallpaper until the shifts become automatic.

**1. From Distribution to Editorial Transformation**  
*Stop moving content; start re-architecting ideas for each platform’s native narrative grammar.*

**2. From Scattered Transcripts to Hub-and-Spoke**  
*One structured master document becomes the single source of truth. Spokes are assembled, not excavated.*

**3. From Content Factory Worker to Editorial Director**  
*Measure success by audience experiences orchestrated, not posts published. Delegate structure to tools; own the editorial vision.*

**4. From Voice as Mystery to Voice as System**  
*Your voice is a teachable set of patterns. Extract it, encode it, and let AI amplify it without losing your soul.*
