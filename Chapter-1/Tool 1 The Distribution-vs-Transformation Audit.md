## Tool 1: The Distribution-vs-Transformation Audit

**Purpose:** To see, in black and white, how much of your current “repurposing” is actually just redistribution—and to begin training your eye to spot editorial transformation opportunities.

**Instructions:** Pick your last three pillar content rollouts (e.g., a YouTube video, a blog post, or a podcast episode, plus all the social assets you created from them). For each rollout, fill in the table below. Be ruthlessly honest; nobody’s judging. This is diagnostic, not a performance review.

**Reflection after completing all three rollouts:**
- My overall ratio felt roughly ___% distribution / ___% transformation.
- The platforms where I defaulted most heavily to distribution: _______________
- One piece of content that *could* have been an editorial transformation but I didn’t have the time/mental model: _______________
- What did the transformation instances have in common? (e.g., “I had a fresh headline,” “I restructured the argument as a story,” “I included a counterintuitive hook.”)

> Keep this audit somewhere visible. When you start building Hubs in Part 2, you’ll check back and see how the ratio flips.
