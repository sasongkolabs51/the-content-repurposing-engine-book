## 5. Editorial Director Identity Reset Script

Use this script as a daily morning prompt—speak it aloud, write it in your journal, or tape it to your monitor. The goal is to anchor the new identity before you touch any content.

> **Today, I am not a content factory worker.**  
> My worth is measured by the distinctiveness of the audience experiences I orchestrate, not by the number of posts I publish.  
> Every platform is a different editorial format with its own reader expectations, and I am the director who composes each one deliberately.  
> Repurposing is an act of editorial transformation. I work from a single intellectual asset, re‑architecting it for each canvas.  
> My voice is a system. I can extract its mechanics, encode them, and confidently delegate structural drafts to an AI, freeing my creative energy for the layers only I can add.  
> Today I will build, not just distribute.

You might also try a shorter version before each repurposing session:  
*“I’m an editorial director now. The hub holds the gold; I’m here to shape it, not mine it.”*
