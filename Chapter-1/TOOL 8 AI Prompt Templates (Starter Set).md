## TOOL 8: AI Prompt Templates (Starter Set)

**Purpose:** Once your Hub is built and your Voice Fingerprint is drafted (Chapter 2), these prompts let you hand off structural adaptation to AI while staying in the Editorial Director's chair. These are *starter* templates. You'll refine them as your voice documentation gets sharper.

**How to use:** Fill in the bracketed sections. Paste into your AI tool of choice. Treat the output as a *first structural draft* that you then edit for the personal touches only you can add.

---

### Prompt A: Twitter/X Thread from Hub Components

```
You are writing a Twitter/X thread for me. You are NOT summarizing a video.
You are building a standalone text-native experience.

VOICE CONSTRAINTS (follow these exactly):
[Paste your 5-sentence Voice Summary from Tool 4, or your full Voice
Fingerprint from Chapter 2 once you have it]

HUB COMPONENTS TO USE:
- Core thesis: [Paste from Hub Section A]
- Story/Example to anchor: [Paste from Hub Section B — pick ONE]
- Objection to address: [Paste from Hub Section C]
- Quotable line to weave in: [Paste from Hub Section A]

STRUCTURAL REQUIREMENTS:
- 8 tweets total
- Tweet 1: A pattern interrupt. NOT the thesis stated plainly. Make the
  reader think "wait, what?" and need to click "more"
- Tweets 2–3: Escalate the problem or tension
- Tweets 4–5: Deliver the insight via the story. Show, don't tell.
- Tweet 6: Address the objection conversationally ("Now, I get it—you're
  thinking...")
- Tweet 7: The quotable line, given breathing room. Short tweet. Let it land.
- Tweet 8: A reflective question. NOT a CTA. NOT "follow for more." End
  on the exhale.

FORMATTING:
- No hashtags
- Use line breaks within tweets for rhythm
- Contractions always. Never "do not" — always "don't"
- Write like you're talking to one smart friend, not broadcasting

OUTPUT: The 8 tweets, numbered. Then a one-line note on what you'd
flag for me to personalize.
```

### Prompt B: LinkedIn Carousel from Hub Components

```
You are writing a LinkedIn carousel (7 slides) for me. This is NOT a
summary of my video. This is a professional narrative that happens to
share the same underlying insight.

VOICE CONSTRAINTS:
[Paste Voice Summary / Fingerprint]
ADDITIONAL TONE NOTE FOR LINKEDIN: Slightly more "peer-to-peer
professional." I'm a colleague sharing a hard-won insight, not a
creator talking to an audience. Warm but not performative.

HUB COMPONENTS:
- Core thesis: [Hub Section A]
- Story to anchor: [Hub Section B — pick the one tagged "LinkedIn narrative"]
- Objection: [Hub Section C]
- Data/proof: [Hub Section C]

STRUCTURE:
- Slide 1: A diagnostic question or surprising professional reframe.
  Must make a scrolling professional STOP. No "In my latest video..."
- Slide 2: "Here's what I used to believe..." (the old assumption)
- Slide 3: The moment / story that broke that belief
- Slide 4: The reframe (the thesis, stated as a professional insight)
- Slide 5: The proof / data point
- Slide 6: "The mistake I see most people make..." (the objection,
  reframed as a common error)
- Slide 7: One takeaway + a genuine engagement question. NOT "Like and
  repost." Something like "Which of these three is your current blind
  spot? Genuinely curious."

FORMATTING:
- Each slide: max 3 short sentences or one sentence + a visual cue note
- Write the text AND a brief [VISUAL NOTE] for each slide describing
  what the graphic/layout should convey
- Keep it scannable. A tired professional should get the point in 15 seconds

OUTPUT: All 7 slides with text + visual notes. Flag anything that
needs my personal anecdote or a line only I would say.
```

### Prompt C: TikTok / Reels Script from Hub Components

```
You are writing a 40-second TikTok/Reels script for me. This is NOT a
condensed version of my YouTube video. This is a native short-form
experience that uses ONE idea from my content.

VOICE CONSTRAINTS:
[Paste Voice Summary / Fingerprint]
DELIVERY NOTE: High energy, direct to camera, conversational. I talk
fast when I'm excited. I use my hands. I lean in when I'm making the
key point.

HUB COMPONENTS:
- The ONE idea to build around: [Pick the single most visceral insight
  from Hub Section A or B — NOT the full thesis. One angle.]
- Visual metaphor or physical prop idea: [If you have one, note it.
  If not, ask the AI to suggest one]

SCRIPT FORMAT:
[0:00–0:01.5] VISUAL HOOK: [Describe what the viewer SEES first.
Text overlay? Prop? Jump cut mid-action? Physical gesture?]
[0:01.5–0:05] SPOKEN HOOK: [First words out of my mouth. Pattern
interrupt. NOT "Hey guys" or "So today I want to talk about..."]
[0:05–0:20] THE REFRAME: [The core insight delivered conversationally.
One idea. Build to the "aha" moment]
[0:20–0:30] THE PROOF or STORY BEAT: [One quick example or "I learned
this when..."]
[0:30–0:40] THE CLOSE: [A one-line reframe that lands. OR a direct
question to camera. NOT "follow for more." Make them rewatch or comment.]

INCLUDE:
- [BODY LANGUAGE CUES] in brackets throughout
- [TEXT OVERLAY] suggestions where a key phrase should appear on screen
- One [PATTERN INTERRUPT] note at the ~15-second mark (a cut, a zoom,
  a tone shift)

OUTPUT: The full script with timestamps, cues, and overlay notes.
Then flag the one line I should ad-lib or personalize in my own words.
```

### Prompt D: Newsletter Section from Hub Components

```
You are drafting a newsletter section for me. This is the "director's
cut" version of my content — the thinking I didn't put in the video
because it was too tangential, too personal, or too "inside baseball."

VOICE CONSTRAINTS:
[Paste Voice Summary / Fingerprint]
TONE: This is a letter to a friend. Unscripted-feeling. I can be more
meandering here, more reflective. It's okay to start with "So I was
thinking about this on a walk..." energy.

HUB COMPONENTS:
- Core thesis: [Hub Section A]
- Story to expand: [Hub Section B — pick one and note: "expand this
  beyond what I said in the video. Add the behind-the-scenes thinking."]
- Original commentary to add: [Write 2–3 sentences of YOUR reflection
  that was NOT in the source content. The AI should build around these.]
- Quotable line: [Hub Section A — weave it in naturally, don't feature it]

STRUCTURE:
- Open with a personal, off-the-cuff observation (NOT "new video is up")
- Transition into the story/insight with a "which made me think about..."
  bridge
- Deliver the insight with the original commentary layered in
- Close with a genuine question or invitation to reply. Make it feel
  like the reader could actually hit "reply" and I'd read it.

LENGTH: 250–350 words. Tight but not rushed.

OUTPUT: The newsletter section. Then a [PERSONALIZE] note telling me
where to insert a specific memory, a reader's name, or a reference
only my audience would get.
```

### Prompt E: The "Am I Actually Transforming?" Quality Check

*Use this AFTER you've drafted (or AI-drafted) a platform asset. Paste the draft in and ask the AI to audit it against transformation criteria.*

```
I'm going to paste a [platform] post I drafted. I need you to audit
it against these TRANSFORMATION criteria. For each one, tell me
PASS or FAIL and why. If FAIL, suggest a specific fix.

1. HOOK TEST: Would a native user of [platform] stop scrolling for
   this opening? Or does it read like a transplant from another platform?
2. STRUCTURE TEST: Does the narrative structure match [platform]'s
   native format? (Thread arc for Twitter, narrative carousel for
   LinkedIn, visual-first for TikTok, letter-style for newsletter)
3. VOICE TEST: Does this sound like ME? Check against these voice
   markers: [Paste 3–4 key mechanics from your Voice Inventory]
4. STANDALONE TEST: If someone saw ONLY this post and had never seen
   my video/blog, would it land completely? Or does it feel like a
   "part 2" that requires context?
5. PLATFORM-TRUTH TEST: Does this use the platform's native strengths?
   (Text rhythm for Twitter, professional framing for LinkedIn,
   visual/physical for TikTok, intimacy for newsletter)

HERE'S THE DRAFT:
[Paste your draft]

Be direct. If it's still distribution wearing a transformation costume,
say so.
```
