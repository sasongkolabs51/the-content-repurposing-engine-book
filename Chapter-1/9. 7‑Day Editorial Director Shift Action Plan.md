## 9. 7‑Day Editorial Director Shift Action Plan

Use this checklist to install the new operating system over one week. Do each day’s exercise, even if it’s only 20 minutes. The goal is to make the mental model feel like your default.

**Day 1 – Audit Day**  
- [ ] Complete the Distribution‑vs‑Transformation Audit (Section 1) for your last three rollouts.  
- [ ] Note your ratio and the primary platform where you default to distribution.  
- [ ] Write one paragraph in your journal: “What would change if I treated repurposing as editorial craft, not distribution?”

**Day 2 – Platform Reality Check**  
- [ ] Fill out the Platform‑Skipping Pattern Reveal (Section 2).  
- [ ] Circle the platform with the biggest skipped‑posting cost. That’s your first target for the new workflow.  
- [ ] Read aloud the Editorial Director Identity Reset Script (Section 5) three times.

**Day 3 – Taste the Hub‑and‑Spoke**  
- [ ] Do the Hub‑and‑Spoke Thought Experiment (Section 3) with a new pillar piece.  
- [ ] Identify which modules would be most valuable for the target platform from Day 2.  
- [ ] Notice how much faster the asset ideas come when you’re working from modules.

**Day 4 – Voice Mechanics Hunt**  
- [ ] Complete the Voice‑as‑System Inventory (Section 4).  
- [ ] Add at least three new mechanical observations that weren’t on your first pass.  
- [ ] Read your observations and say out loud: “My voice is a system. I can teach it.”

**Day 5 – Prompt Practice (Low Stakes)**  
- [ ] Using a recent pillar’s rough Hub notes, try one AI prompt from Section 6 (choose the platform that feels scariest).  
- [ ] Do not edit the AI output immediately. Just observe what it does well and where your voice mechanics need tightening in the prompt.  
- [ ] Adjust the voice mechanics in the prompt and run it again. Notice the difference.

**Day 6 – Identity Anchor**  
- [ ] Write your own, shorter version of the Editorial Director Identity Reset that feels fully yours.  
- [ ] Record it as a voice memo and listen to it before your next content session.  
- [ ] Share your Distribution‑vs‑Transformation audit insight with a creator friend (or on social) – teaching cements learning.

**Day 7 – Reflect and Pledge**  
- [ ] Review your notes from the week. What’s different in how you *feel* about repurposing?  
- [ ] Write down one specific promise: “I will no longer _________ (e.g., skip TikTok because adaptation feels overwhelming). Instead, I will _________ (e.g., use a hub‑fed prompt to create a first draft every Tuesday).”  
- [ ] Put the Platform Narrative Grammar Cheat Sheet (Section 7) somewhere you’ll see it during your next creation session.
