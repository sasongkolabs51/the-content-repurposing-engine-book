## Tool 6: Editorial Director Identity Script (Mental Rehearsal)

**Purpose:** To replace the internal monologue that says, “I have to churn out posts for all these platforms” with a calm, systems-level perspective. Use this script as a centering exercise before a repurposing session, or record it in your own voice.

---

*I am not a machine that moves content around. I am an editorial architect. The idea I’ve put into the world is a rich intellectual asset—my Hub. Now I get to design distinct experiences for different audiences.*

*I’m not copying; I’m composing. For LinkedIn, I’ll reframe this as a professional insight with a diagnostic lens. For Twitter, I’ll build a thread that creates curiosity and tension. For short-form video, I’ll start with a visual metaphor that stops the scroll. Each platform deserves its own narrative logic, and I know how to provide it.*

*The time I invest in preparing my Hub pays back tenfold. When I sit down to create, I’m not digging through transcripts; I’m assembling from pre-processed components. That’s not extra work—that’s leverage.*

*My voice isn’t something AI dilutes. My voice is the set of constraints I give the machine: the hooks I use, the rhythms I prefer, the phrases that are unmistakably mine. The AI drafts the structure; I inject the soul. That’s a partnership, not a surrender.*

*Today, I’ll focus on orchestrating one seamless audience journey. The rest—post count, vanity metrics—is just noise. I’m an Editorial Director. This is my craft.*
