## 3. Ten-Piece Retrospective Scoring Worksheet

**Purpose:** Score your last ten pillar pieces to see the matrix in action—and to spot where your gut may have misallocated effort.

**Instructions:** List up to ten recent pillar pieces (videos, long-form posts, podcast episodes). For each, assign a score of 1, 2, or 3 on each dimension. Use actual engagement data where available, plus your editorial judgment. Then pick your primary platform from your Metrics-and-Goals Map and compute the weighted score using its formula.

**Formula reminder:** Weighted Score = (Evergreen × E‑weight) + (Resonance × R‑weight) + (Shareability × S‑weight)  
*Example for Twitter growth (E=0.2, R=0.3, S=0.5): (2×0.2) + (3×0.3) + (2×0.5) = 0.4 + 0.9 + 1.0 = 2.3*
