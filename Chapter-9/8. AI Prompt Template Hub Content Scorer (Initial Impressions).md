## 8. AI Prompt Template: Hub Content Scorer (Initial Impressions)

**Purpose:** Get a quick, objective first pass at scoring a Hub before you apply your editorial judgment. This is optional—use it when you’re unsure or when you want to batch‑score a backlog.

```
You are an editorial analyst. I will provide a Hub document with five blocks (Core Thesis, Key Stories, Data Points, Audience Objections, Quotable Lines). Based solely on the content, assign a preliminary score from 1 to 3 on each of these dimensions:

- Evergreen Strength: How long will the core idea remain relevant? (1 = time-sensitive; 3 = timeless)
- Emotional Resonance: How intensely does the content make someone feel something? (1 = purely informational; 3 = evokes strong emotion like vulnerability, catharsis, or righteous anger)
- Shareability: How likely is someone to share this beyond their immediate circle? (1 = niche interest only; 3 = highly novel or counterintuitive, signals something desirable about the sharer)

For each score, provide a one-sentence justification. Then output the three scores in this format:
Evergreen: X
Resonance: Y
Shareability: Z

Do not invent any data not in the Hub. Base your scores strictly on the content provided.

Hub:
[PASTE FULL HUB]
```

After getting the AI’s scores, compare them to your own gut feeling. Adjust any dimension where your human judgment (knowing your audience, timing, personal context) outweighs the text‑only analysis. Record the final scores in your Hub Metadata Scorecard.
