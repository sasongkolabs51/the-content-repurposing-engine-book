## Tool 2: Pillar Content Scoring Sheet (Single Piece)

**Purpose:** To quickly evaluate any pillar piece on the three dimensions and compute its priority score for each platform. Use this every time you build a new Hub, and paste the scores into the Hub metadata.

**Instructions:** After populating the five blocks of a Hub, rate the content on each dimension using the 1–3 scale. Then apply the weights from your Metrics & Goals Map to get a final priority score per platform.

### The Three Dimensions (1–3 Scale)

| Dimension | 1 = Low | 2 = Medium | 3 = High |
|-----------|---------|------------|----------|
| **Evergreen Strength** | Time‑sensitive or news‑pegged; irrelevant in < 3 months. | Relevant for 6–12 months; minor updates needed. | Perennially relevant; could be republished years later with zero changes. |
| **Emotional Resonance** | Primarily informational, neutral tone. | Contains personal story or relatable struggle; some emotional texture. | Elicits strong emotion—vulnerability, righteous anger, hope, catharsis; drives personal comments and shares. |
| **Shareability** | Interesting to your niche only; unlikely to spread. | Broadly relevant; could appeal to adjacent audiences. | Highly novel or counterintuitive; sharing signals something desirable about the sharer (smart, in‑the‑know, empathetic). |
