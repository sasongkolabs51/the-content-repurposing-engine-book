## 9. Repurposing Decision Matrix at a Glance (One‑Pager)

**Print this and keep it near your sprint workspace.**

**The Three Dimensions:**
- **Evergreen:** 1 = timely, 3 = timeless
- **Resonance:** 1 = info only, 3 = strong emotion
- **Shareability:** 1 = niche, 3 = novel/social signal

**Default Weights (adjust to your goals):**
- **Growth (Twitter):** S=0.5, R=0.3, E=0.2
- **Leads/Authority (LinkedIn):** R=0.5, E=0.3, S=0.2
- **Retention (Newsletter):** R=0.4, E=0.4, S=0.2
- **Awareness (TikTok):** S=0.6, R=0.2, E=0.2

**Weighted Score = (E × E‑weight) + (R × R‑weight) + (S × S‑weight)**

**Intensity Guide:**
- Score ≥ 2.5 → Full sprint, all platforms
- 2.0–2.4 → Standard sprint, all platforms
- 1.5–1.9 → Light sprint, two platforms
- < 1.5 → Micro sprint, one platform

**Weekly Routine:**
1. Score any new Hubs immediately.
2. At sprint planning, filter candidate Hubs by primary platform score.
3. Pick the highest; adjust intensity based on score.
4. Schedule one Discovery Sprint per month for untested, high‑potential content.

**Monthly Routine:**
- Re‑score based on actual performance.
- Adjust weights if business goals changed.
- Identify and schedule one “sleeper” piece the matrix loves but you haven’t used.
