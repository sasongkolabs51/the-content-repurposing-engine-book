## TOOL 9: The Complex Scenario Worksheet

**Purpose:** For the nuanced decisions the matrix can't make alone. When strategic scores conflict with editorial judgment, emotional sensitivity, or brand considerations. This is where the director's eye overrides the spreadsheet.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes per scenario

---

### SCENARIO: _______________

**The piece:** _______________
**Matrix scores:** E:___ R:___ S:___ → Top platform: _______________ Score: ___ / 3.0
**Matrix recommendation:** _______________ *(e.g., "Full sprint on LinkedIn. High resonance = lead gen gold.")*

---

### The Complication

**Why I'm hesitating to follow the matrix:**

☐ The content is deeply personal / vulnerable. I'm not sure I want to re-expose it.
☐ The content is time-sensitive. The matrix says high Evergreen, but I think the context has shifted.
☐ The content performed well once. I'm worried about "beating a dead horse" if I repurpose again.
☐ The content is contrarian. I'm worried about backlash if I amplify it further.
☐ The content is great for one platform but feels wrong for the platform the matrix recommends.
☐ My business goals have shifted since I scored this piece.
☐ Other: _______________________________________________

---

### The Decision Framework

**Question 1: Is the matrix recommendation strategically sound?**

☐ Yes. The data supports it. My hesitation is emotional, not strategic.
☐ Partially. The strategy is right, but the execution needs adaptation.
☐ No. The matrix is wrong here because: _______________

---

**Question 2: If I follow the matrix, what adaptation do I need?**

*(Use Chapter 8 conditional logic to adjust the approach without abandoning the strategic choice.)*

| If the issue is… | The adaptation is… |
|---|---|
| Too vulnerable / raw | Use "distant reflection" tone. Frame as lesson learned, not open wound. Weight toward insight, not pain. |
| Already repurposed once | Change the hook pattern. Use a different story from the Hub. Target a different platform. Angle it differently. |
| Contrarian / risky | Soften the close. Invite debate, don't declare war. Use "I could be wrong" energy. |
| Wrong platform fit | Override the platform. Use the piece where it fits naturally, even if the score is slightly lower. |
| Time-sensitive | Check: is the CORE IDEA still relevant even if the specific example is dated? If yes, update the example. If no, archive. |
