## TOOL 10: The 30-Day Implementation Roadmap (Chapter 9)

**Purpose:** Build your matrix, score your content, and make your first data-driven sprint decision within four weeks.

---

### Week 1: Define Goals and Build the Rubric

- [ ] Complete the **Metrics-and-Goals Map** (Tool 1). Define goals, metrics, and weights for each platform.
- [ ] Read and internalize the **Three-Dimension Scoring Rubric** (Tool 2). Keep it accessible during scoring.
- [ ] Set up the **Matrix template** (Tool 3) in your preferred tool (spreadsheet, Notion database, paper).
- [ ] Add the **Hub Metadata Extension** (Tool 8) to your Hub template.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~40 minutes

### Week 2: Score Your Content

- [ ] Complete the **Ten-Piece Retrospective Score** (Tool 4). Score your last 10 pillars. Compute weighted scores. Rank them.
- [ ] Do the comparison: where did your intuition misallocate energy? Let that land.
- [ ] Score any new Hubs you build this week using the metadata extension.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~45 minutes

### Week 3: Make Your First Matrix-Driven Decision

- [ ] Use the **Sprint Prioritization Planner** (Tool 5) for this week's sprint. Let the matrix choose your sprint focus.
- [ ] Run the sprint on the highest-scoring piece. Note how it feels to let data decide.
- [ ] If there's a "No Data" piece you suspect is strong, run a **Discovery Sprint** (single platform, 15 min).
- [ ] Log the results in the Hub's Performance Notes.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~20 minutes planning + normal sprint time

### Week 4: Establish the Maintenance Habit

- [ ] Run your first **Monthly Matrix Review** (Tool 6). Update scores. Check for drift. Validate weights.
- [ ] Use the **Data Blind Spot Diagnostic** (Tool 7) if anything feels off.
- [ ] If you have a high-scoring piece you're avoiding, work through the **Complex Scenario Worksheet** (Tool 9).
- [ ] Plan next month's sprint priorities using the updated matrix.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~30 minutes
