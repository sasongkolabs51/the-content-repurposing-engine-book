## 2. Scoring Dimensions Reference Card

**Purpose:** A cheat sheet to keep the three dimensions top of mind and to ensure consistent scoring across pieces.

### Evergreen Strength
*How long will the core idea stay relevant?*

- **1** – Time-sensitive or news-pegged. (e.g., “How to file 2024 taxes,” a reaction to a platform algorithm change.)
- **2** – Relevant for 6–12 months. (e.g., a framework tied to current tool features, a seasonal productivity tip.)
- **3** – Perennially relevant. (e.g., “The psychology of procrastination,” a principle of human behavior, a storytelling technique.)

**Quick question:** “If someone found this piece two years from now, would it still feel fresh and useful?”

### Emotional Resonance
*How intensely does the content make people feel something?*

- **1** – Primarily informational, neutral tone. (e.g., a straightforward tutorial, a list of tools.)
- **2** – Contains some personal story or relatable struggle, but the emotional arc is light. (e.g., a case study with a mild personal admission.)
- **3** – Elicits strong emotion: vulnerability, righteous anger, hope, catharsis, or a sudden shift in perspective. (e.g., a personal failure story, a passionate call to action, a counterintuitive revelation that hits hard.)

**Quick question:** “Will this piece make someone pause, feel something in their gut, and want to tell a friend, ‘You need to read this’?”

### Shareability
*How likely is someone to share this beyond their immediate circle?*

- **1** – Interesting to your niche but unlikely to spread. (e.g., a deep-dive into a narrow technical topic.)
- **2** – Broadly relevant; could appeal to adjacent audiences. (e.g., a productivity tip that applies across professions.)
- **3** – Highly novel or counterintuitive; sharing it signals something desirable about the sharer (e.g., being in-the-know, empathetic, smart). (e.g., “I tracked every decision I made for thirty days. The results are horrifying.”)

**Quick question:** “Does sharing this post make someone look good?”
