## Tool 8: The Repurposing Decision Matrix Summary Card & Mental Script

### Summary Card: The Matrix in Five Lines

- **Define success per platform.** Growth? Leads? Retention? Pick one primary goal and the metric that tracks it.
- **Score every pillar on three dimensions.** Evergreen Strength, Emotional Resonance, Shareability (1–3 each). Honest, data‑informed.
- **Weight the dimensions to match the platform goal.** A shareable thread needs high Shareability; a lead‑gen carousel needs high Resonance.
- **Use the weighted score to prioritize your sprint.** Full sprint for 2.7+, mini‑sprint for 2.0–2.6, skip or Discovery Sprint below 2.0. Match effort to potential.
- **Audit monthly.** Re‑score with real data, adjust weights, catch blind spots. The matrix is alive.

### Mental Rehearsal Script

*I’m no longer repurposing based on what I remember feeling about a piece. I’m letting a simple, data‑informed framework guide my energy to the content that matters most for my goals.*

*Every pillar I create gets scored on three dimensions: how long it will last, how deeply it resonates, and how likely it is to travel. Those scores, weighted for each platform’s purpose, give me a priority number. That number tells me not just “should I repurpose?” but “how deep should I go?”*

*This week, I’ll look at my scored pillars, pick the highest‑return one for my primary goal, and run my sprint. For lower‑scoring pieces, I’ll do a lighter pass—or consciously skip them, without guilt. That’s not laziness; that’s strategic resource allocation.*

*If my matrix ever feels off—if a high‑scoring piece flops or a low‑scoring piece goes viral—I’ll investigate. I’ll adjust weights, add Discovery Sprints, and refine my scoring. The matrix gets smarter over time because I treat it as a hypothesis, not a verdict.*

*Today, my Content Transformer has a navigation system. I know where I’m going, and I’m investing every creative hour where it will compound. Let’s prioritize.*
