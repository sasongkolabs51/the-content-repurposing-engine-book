### Step 3: Set Your Dimension Weights

Based on your goal for each platform, how much should each scoring dimension matter?

**The three dimensions:**
- **Evergreen Strength (E):** How long will this content stay relevant?
- **Emotional Resonance (R):** How strongly does it make people *feel*?
- **Shareability (S):** How likely is someone to share it with a non-follower?

**Default weights (adjust based on your goals):**

| Platform | Goal | E Weight | R Weight | S Weight | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Twitter | Growth | 20% | 30% | **50%** | Growth = shares = new eyes. Shareability dominates. |
| LinkedIn | Leads / Authority | 30% | **50%** | 20% | Leads come from trust. Trust comes from resonance. |
| TikTok | Awareness | 20% | 20% | **60%** | Top-of-funnel. Virality = reach. Shareability dominates. |
| Newsletter | Retention / Depth | **40%** | **40%** | 20% | Depth + timelessness. Readers stay for substance. |
