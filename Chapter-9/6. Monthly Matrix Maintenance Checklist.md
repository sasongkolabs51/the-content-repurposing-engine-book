## 6. Monthly Matrix Maintenance Checklist

**Purpose:** Keep the matrix alive and accurate. A 15‑minute monthly ritual prevents rot.

- [ ] **Update scores with real data.** For each pillar piece you repurposed this month, adjust its Evergreen, Resonance, and Shareability scores based on actual performance. Did a piece you rated Shareability 2 go surprisingly viral? Bump it to 3.
- [ ] **Check for Evergreen drift.** Re‑score any pieces older than six months. Has the core idea aged? (e.g., a tech tutorial may have dropped from 3 to 1.)
- [ ] **Audit metric alignment.** Look at your highest‑scoring posts per platform. Are they driving the outcomes you actually care about? If high-Shareability posts never lead to conversions, adjust the weighting in your Metrics-and-Goals Map. Down‑weight Shareability, up‑weight Resonance.
- [ ] **Review the “no data” backlog.** Any Discovery Sprints that produced enough data to now slot the piece into the matrix? Score them and fold them into your candidate pool.
- [ ] **Spot the sleeper.** Is there a piece that the matrix consistently ranks highly, but you haven’t repurposed yet? Schedule it for next week.
- [ ] **Clean house.** Archive any pieces that have dropped below a score of 1.5 on all platforms. They’ve served their purpose.
