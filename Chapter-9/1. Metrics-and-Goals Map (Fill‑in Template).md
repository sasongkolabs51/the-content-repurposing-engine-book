## 1. Metrics-and-Goals Map (Fill‑in Template)

**Purpose:** Clarify, for each platform, what “success” means for your business. This anchors the weighting in the decision matrix.

**Instructions:** List every platform where you maintain an active presence. For each, define a single primary goal (the one that matters most to your business right now) and the metric you’ll track to gauge progress. Then assign a rough weighting to the three scoring dimensions—Evergreen Strength, Emotional Resonance, and Shareability—based on that goal. The sum of the three weights should equal 1.0 (or 100%).

| Platform | Primary Goal | Key Metric(s) | Evergreen Weight | Resonance Weight | Shareability Weight |
|----------|--------------|---------------|------------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Twitter/X | e.g., audience growth, top-of-funnel reach | Impressions, retweets, new follower rate | 0.2 | 0.3 | 0.5 |
| LinkedIn | e.g., lead generation, professional authority | Comment depth, DM inquiries, click-through to offer | 0.3 | 0.5 | 0.2 |
| Newsletter | e.g., retention, conversion of warm leads | Open rate, reply rate, link click rate | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.2 |
| TikTok/Reels | e.g., brand awareness, top-of-funnel discovery | Views, new follower rate, share rate | 0.2 | 0.2 | 0.6 |
| (Other) |  |  |  |  |  |

**Review cadence:** Revisit this map quarterly, or whenever your business priorities shift. If you’re launching a course soon, you might temporarily weight LinkedIn more heavily for lead generation.
