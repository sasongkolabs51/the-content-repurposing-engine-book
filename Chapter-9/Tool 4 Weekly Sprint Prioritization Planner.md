### Step 3: Select Content Using Matrix

- **If I can run one full sprint:** Pick the highest‑scoring piece for my primary platform. If it also scores well on secondary platforms, use it for all. If not, consider a second piece for the secondary platform.
- **If I have limited time (mini‑sprint):** Pick the single highest‑scoring piece and generate only the primary platform asset(s). Skip or defer lower platforms.
- **If a new pillar just published and lacks data:** Use the “Discovery Sprint” rule—run a single‑platform sprint (Twitter or LinkedIn) to get initial engagement signals, then score properly next week.
