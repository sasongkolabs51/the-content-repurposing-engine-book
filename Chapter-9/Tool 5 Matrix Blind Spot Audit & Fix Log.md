## Tool 5: Matrix Blind Spot Audit & Fix Log

**Purpose:** To catch and correct the three common data‑interpretation failures—recency bias, metric myopia, and the “no data” trap—before they skew your matrix over time.

**Instructions:** Use this log during your monthly matrix review (Tool 6) and any time a repurposed post performs in a way that surprises you.

**Recurring Fixes to Consider:**
- If high‑shareability posts rarely convert, reduce Shareability weight and increase Resonance weight in your Metrics & Goals Map.
- If a piece surprised you with high engagement after a “Discovery Sprint,” create a permanent rule: “Any new pillar with a subjective Resonance score of 3 gets an automatic Discovery Sprint on Twitter within one week.”
- To reduce recency bias, re‑score pieces at least two weeks after publication, using actual comments/replies as data.
