> *This is the matrix's immediate value. Not that it tells you something you didn't suspect. But that it puts a NUMBER on the suspicion. "I think I've been over-investing in tutorials" becomes "I spent 6 hours repurposing a piece that scored 1.7, and 45 minutes on a piece that scored 2.9." The number makes the pattern undeniable. And undeniable patterns get fixed.*
