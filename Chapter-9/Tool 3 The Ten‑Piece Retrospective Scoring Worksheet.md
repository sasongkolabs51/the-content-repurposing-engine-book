## Tool 3: The Ten‑Piece Retrospective Scoring Worksheet

**Purpose:** To back‑score your recent content and see, in one view, where you’ve been investing repurposing energy versus where the matrix says you should have been. This is the “aha moment” exercise from the chapter.

**Instructions:** List your last ten pillar pieces (videos, podcasts, blog posts). For each, assign Evergreen, Resonance, and Shareability scores using the scale above. Then compute the weighted score for your *primary* platform only (the one you care most about right now). Sort by weighted score descending.

**Post‑Scoring Reflection:**
- Look at the top three pieces by weighted score. Did you give them the repurposing attention they deserved? If not, why not? (e.g., “I didn’t think it was ‘evergreen enough’,” “I was too busy that week.”)
- Look at the pieces you *did* repurpose fully. Where do they rank? Any surprises?
- One piece I should have repurposed more aggressively: _______________

This worksheet often reveals that a “quiet” piece actually has higher potential than the flashy one you spent days on. That’s the matrix’s value in action.
