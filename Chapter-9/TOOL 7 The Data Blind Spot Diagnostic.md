## TOOL 7: The Data Blind Spot Diagnostic

**Purpose:** When your matrix feels "off"—when the rankings don't match reality, when you keep ignoring its recommendations, when the data seems to lie—use this to find the specific cognitive bias at play.

**Time needed:** 5 minutes per diagnosis

---

### The Symptom: _______________

*(e.g., "The matrix says my burnout essay is my highest-scoring piece, but I keep choosing to repurpose the tactical tutorials instead." or "My high-shareability posts get retweets but zero course sales." or "I scored a new piece low because it has no data, and now it's buried.")*

---

#### Blind Spot 1: Recency Bias

**The symptom:** I'm over-scoring recent pieces on Resonance because I remember how they felt to publish. Or I'm under-scoring older pieces because I've forgotten their impact.

**Diagnostic questions:**

☐ Did I score this piece based on *memory* or *data*?
☐ If I look at the actual comments/shares/DMs from when it was published, does the score hold?
☐ Am I giving a "recency bonus" to something published in the last 2 weeks?

**The fix:**

☐ Re-score the piece using ONLY the original engagement data (comments, shares, replies). Ignore your memory.
☐ Wait 2 weeks after publishing before scoring a new piece. Let the data settle.
☐ For older pieces: pull the original analytics. Score based on those numbers, not your current feeling.

#### Blind Spot 2: Metric Myopia

**The symptom:** I'm over-indexing on one dimension because it's easy to measure. Usually Shareability (retweets are public and immediate) at the expense of Resonance (comment depth is harder to quantify) or Evergreen (requires long-term tracking).

**Diagnostic questions:**

☐ Am I weighting Shareability too high because retweet counts feel like "proof"?
☐ Am I ignoring Resonance because "people said nice things" feels soft?
☐ Am I discounting Evergreen because I can't measure "will this matter in 2 years"?
☐ When I look at actual BUSINESS outcomes (sales, leads, DMs), which dimension correlates most strongly?

**The fix:**

☐ Pull a quarterly report connecting posts to business outcomes. Which dimension actually drives revenue?
☐ Adjust weights to reflect what actually converts, not what's easiest to count.
☐ Add a "comment depth" metric: count substantive replies (3+ sentences) vs. "Great post!" replies. This quantifies Resonance.

#### Blind Spot 3: The "No Data" Trap

**The symptom:** A new piece has zero engagement data, so I score it tentatively (usually low), and it sinks to the bottom of the matrix. It never gets a chance to prove itself. The matrix becomes a closed loop that only reinforces past winners.

**Diagnostic questions:**

☐ Did I score this piece based on its *inherent qualities* (the rubric), or based on *absence of data*?
☐ Am I conflating "I don't know how this will perform" with "this will perform poorly"?
☐ Is this piece similar to a past piece that overperformed expectations?

**The fix:**

☐ Score the piece on its inherent qualities using the rubric. Ignore the lack of data.
☐ Run a **Discovery Sprint**: one platform, one asset, 15 minutes. Generate initial signals.
☐ After one week, re-score with real data. Slot it into the matrix properly.
☐ Add a rule: "No piece stays unscored for more than 2 weeks. If it has no data, score it on potential and run a Discovery Sprint."

#### Blind Spot 4: The Vulnerability Avoidance

**The symptom:** The matrix consistently ranks personal, vulnerable content at the top (high Resonance, high Shareability). But I keep choosing to repurpose the "safe" tactical content instead. The matrix says one thing. My fear says another.

**Diagnostic questions:**

☐ Is there a high-scoring piece I'm avoiding because it feels "too personal"?
☐ Am I worried about being seen as "exploiting my pain" for engagement?
☐ Am I worried about over-exposing a story I've already told once?
☐ Is the avoidance actually about the audience, or about my own discomfort?

**The fix:**

☐ Acknowledge the fear. It's valid. But the data says the audience WANTS this content.
☐ Use the **Complex Scenario approach** (Tool 9): adapt the tone. "Distant reflection" instead of "raw wound." Lesson learned, not pain re-performed.
☐ You don't have to repurpose it on EVERY platform. Choose the one where it fits best (usually LinkedIn or Newsletter for vulnerable content).
☗ You don't have to repurpose it EVERY week. Once per quarter is enough for a deeply personal piece.
☐ Ask: "If a reader told me this story helped them, would I still feel like I was exploiting it?" (Probably not.)
