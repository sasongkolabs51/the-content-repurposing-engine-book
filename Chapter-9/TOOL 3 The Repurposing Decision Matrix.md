### How to Compute the Weighted Score

**Example:** If your Twitter weights are E:20%, R:30%, S:50%, and a piece scores E=2, R=3, S=3:

Twitter Score = (2 × 0.20) + (3 × 0.30) + (3 × 0.50) = 0.4 + 0.9 + 1.5 = **2.8**

### The Intensity Decision

Use the score to decide HOW MUCH repurposing each piece deserves:

| Score Range | Intensity | What This Means |
|---|---|---|
| **2.5 – 3.0** | Full Sprint | Every platform. Every asset. Full editorial polish. Personal layer on every piece. This is your A-game content. |
| **2.0 – 2.4** | Standard Sprint | 3 platforms. Standard prompts. Light polish. Solid but not heroic effort. |
| **1.5 – 1.9** | Light Sprint | 1–2 platforms only. Quick thread or newsletter mention. Minimal editing. |
| **1.0 – 1.4** | Skip / Archive | Not worth repurposing effort right now. Revisit in 6 months if Evergreen score allows. |
