## Tool 6: Monthly Matrix Maintenance Ritual (Checklist)

**Purpose:** To keep the matrix alive, accurate, and fully integrated with your sprint workflow. This 15‑minute monthly review prevents the matrix from becoming a static relic.

**Instructions:** Schedule a recurring 15‑minute calendar block (e.g., first Monday of the month). Run through the checklist. Keep a log of changes.

### Monthly Checklist

- [ ] **Update Scores Based on New Data:** For each pillar piece repurposed in the last month, review performance. Did engagement confirm or challenge your original scores? Update Evergreen, Resonance, and Shareability ratings accordingly. Use actual comments, shares, click‑throughs, not memory.
- [ ] **Re‑score Older Pieces for Evergreen Drift:** Any piece older than 3–6 months may have shifted in Evergreen Strength. If a piece has lost relevance (e.g., a tool‑specific tutorial now outdated), downgrade it. If it proved genuinely timeless, keep it at 3.
- [ ] **Review Platform Goals & Weights:** Have your business goals shifted? (e.g., launching a course soon may shift LinkedIn weight toward Resonance/Conversion.) Adjust the Metrics & Goals Map if needed, and recompute any active pillar scores.
- [ ] **Identify and Schedule a “Missed Gem”:** From your scored list, pick one piece that ranks highly but hasn’t been repurposed yet (or was under‑repurposed). Add it to the upcoming week’s sprint plan.
- [ ] **Log Any Blind Spots Detected:** Use Tool 5 to record any surprises from the past month and apply fixes.
- [ ] **Trim or Archive:** If any pillar piece’s weighted score has fallen below 1.5 for all platforms, consider archiving it from active repurposing consideration. It’s served its purpose.
