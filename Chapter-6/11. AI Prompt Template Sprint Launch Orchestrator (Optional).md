## 11. AI Prompt Template: Sprint Launch Orchestrator (Optional)

If your AI tool supports long, multi-part outputs, you can use a single prompt to generate all four platform drafts at once. This is an advanced efficiency boost; test it against your parallel-tab method and use whichever feels faster.

```
You are my multi-platform editorial assistant. I will provide a Hub with five blocks. Your task is to generate FOUR distinct drafts, one for each platform, using the assigned voice constraints, platform grammar, and specific Hub blocks listed below.

[PASTE YOUR CONDENSED VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK HERE]

=== PLATFORM 1: TWITTER THREAD ===
Structural rules: [paste your Twitter thread rules from Chapter 5 prompt]
Use these Hub blocks: Core Thesis, Quotable Lines, Audience Objections.

=== PLATFORM 2: LINKEDIN CAROUSEL ===
Structural rules: [paste your LinkedIn carousel rules]
Use these Hub blocks: Core Thesis, Key Stories (Story #1), Data Points (the first stat), Audience Objections (first objection + response).

=== PLATFORM 3: TIKTOK / REELS SCRIPT ===
Structural rules: [paste your TikTok script rules]
Use these Hub blocks: Core Thesis (condensed to 1 sentence), one Quotable Line, one Key Story (max 3 sentences).

=== PLATFORM 4: NEWSLETTER EXPANSION ===
Structural rules: [paste your newsletter expansion rules]
Use these Hub blocks: Core Thesis, Key Stories (Story #2), Data Points (all), Audience Objections (all).

Output each draft under a clear heading: ## Twitter Thread, ## LinkedIn Carousel, ## TikTok Script, ## Newsletter. Do not mix formats. Do not invent data or stories not in the Hub.

Hub:
[PASTE YOUR FULL HUB HERE]
```

Test this prompt once. If it produces all four assets cleanly, it can replace the multi-tab burst, saving you copy-paste time. If any platform’s output is weak, keep that platform in its own tab and use the orchestrator prompt for the rest.
