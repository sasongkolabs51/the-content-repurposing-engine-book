## TOOL 8: The Microsprint Template

**Purpose:** The 15-minute emergency version. For when you're short on time, low on energy, or rebuilding after a sprint drought. One platform. One asset. Fifteen minutes. The goal is not volume—it's re-establishing the neural pathway of *sprint → output → relief.*

**Time needed:** 15 minutes. Hard stop.

---

### MICROSPRINT — [Date]

**Platform:** ☐ Twitter ☐ LinkedIn ☐ TikTok ☐ Newsletter
**Hub:** _______________
**Timer:** 15 minutes. Set it now.

---

#### Phase 1: Fire (2 minutes)

- Open the prompt for this platform.
- Paste the relevant Hub blocks.
- Hit generate.

#### Phase 2: Triage (5 minutes)

- Read the draft.
- Quick check: Hook right? Voice right? Structure right?
- Decision: ☐ Approve ☐ Lightly Edit (one fix) ☐ Flag (note it, move on)

#### Phase 3: Polish (5 minutes)

- If Approved: quick read for typos. Add your one personal line. Done.
- If Lightly Edit: make the ONE fix. Add your personal line. Done.
- If Flagged: skip polish. Note the failure. You'll regenerate later.

#### Phase 4: Queue (3 minutes)

- Copy into scheduling tool.
- Format for platform.
- Done.

> *The Microsprint is not a consolation prize. It's a re-entry vehicle. Every time you run one, you're telling your brain: "The system is still here. It still works. I just needed a smaller door." Next week, you walk through the full door.*
