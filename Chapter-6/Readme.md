# Chapter 6 Companion Tools  
**The Sixty-Minute Multi-Platform Sprint**  
*Checklists, Decision Frameworks, and Trackers to Run Parallel Generations, Defeat Perfectionism, and Make Repurposing a Single Focused Session*

The tools that follow are designed to turn the sprint from a concept into a repeatable weekly event. They include the preflight checklist that eliminates setup friction, a parallel-generation mapping sheet so you never wonder which Hub block goes where, an editorial triage framework that keeps you out of the perfectionism weeds, and recovery protocols for when life inevitably interrupts the rhythm. Work through them in order and keep them within arm’s reach of your sprint workspace.
