## 10. Sprint Day Identity Anchor

Read aloud before you start the timer. Write your favorite line on a sticky note.

> I am an editorial director, and this sprint is my production floor.  
> My Hub is the raw material. My prompts are the machinery.  
> In the next sixty minutes, I will triage, not perfect.  
> I will fire every prompt in parallel, because my AI works for me, not the other way around.  
> A draft that’s 85% me is a win. I’ll add the final 15% with a light edit, not a rewrite.  
> When the timer rings, I will have four assets ready to schedule.  
> And if a draft isn’t there yet? I’ll flag it and move on—because done beats perfect, and consistency beats heroics.  
> Today, I turn one pillar into a presence everywhere. This is my hour.
