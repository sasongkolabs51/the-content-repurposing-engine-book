## TOOL 3: The Triage Scorecard

**Purpose:** The rapid assessment sheet you fill in as each draft comes back. Forces the Approve / Lightly Edit / Flag decision before you get sucked into line-by-line editing.

**Time needed:** 2–3 minutes per asset to fill in. (The actual editing happens after the decision.)
