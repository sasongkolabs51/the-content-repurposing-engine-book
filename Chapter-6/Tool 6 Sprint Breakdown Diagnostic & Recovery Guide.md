## Tool 6: Sprint Breakdown Diagnostic & Recovery Guide

**Purpose:** A quick‑reference for when the sprint goes off the rails—so you diagnose instead of despairing. Use the table to identify the failure mode and take the suggested fix.

| Failure Mode | Symptoms | Immediate Fix (this sprint) | Permanent Fix (next sprint) |
|--------------|----------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|
| **Prompt Drift** | 2+ outputs are significantly below tested quality. Voice is off, or format rules are ignored. | Note the specific drift. If it’s a single prompt, quickly adjust the template (add a banned phrase, tighten the hook rule) and regenerate. If multiple, flag the assets and regenerate after the sprint. | Before next sprint, run a quick single‑output test with the adjusted prompts against a different Hub to confirm robustness. |
| **The Overshoot** | Timer says 0:00 but you’ve only triaged 2 assets. Perfectionism snuck in. | HARD STOP. If the remaining assets are not yet reviewed, quickly triage them now with a 3‑minute limit each—Approve, Lightly Edit, or Flag—and move on. You can polish outside the sprint. | Next sprint, set a sub‑timer for each asset (5–8 minutes) and switch immediately when it beeps. Use the “Lightly Edit” category more aggressively; you can always polish later. |
| **The Abandoned Sprint** | You skipped the sprint for 2+ weeks. The thought of a full 60‑minute session feels heavy. | Do a **micro‑sprint** within 24 hours: pick ONE platform (e.g., Twitter), run the prompt, triage in 15 minutes. Just ship that one asset. The goal is to break inertia. | Schedule the next full sprint for the following week but treat the micro‑sprint as a bridge. Also, lower your sprint scope temporarily—process only 2 platforms for the first returning week. |
| **The Distracted Sprint** | Notifications pulled you out, or you started scrolling mid‑sprint. | Note what grabbed you. Finish the sprint but with a note to remove that specific distraction next time (e.g., “keep phone in another room”). | Update your preflight checklist: add that specific distraction as an item to neutralize. |
| **The Hub Gap** | You realize mid‑sprint that a Key Story is too vague, or data is missing. | Flag the asset. Note in the Hub what’s missing and add a placeholder. After the sprint, fill the gap and regenerate. | Before the next sprint, add a “Hub Readiness” check to your preflight (already in Tool 1) — if a block feels thin, spend 5 minutes enriching it before you start the timer. |

**Recovery mantra:** “A missed sprint is a data point, not a failure. I can always run a micro‑sprint and restart next week.”
