## 7. Sprint Failure Diagnostic Card

When a sprint goes sideways, match the symptom to the fix. Don’t abandon the system—adjust it.

| Symptom | Diagnosis | Immediate Fix for Next Sprint |
|---------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| 2+ outputs flagged for Rewrite; voice or format off | **Prompt Drift:** The Hub topic is different enough from test content that prompts need recalibration. | Post-sprint: Spend 10 minutes tightening prompts. Add one specific negative example to each, or adjust Voice Constraints for this content type. Regenerate flagged assets. |
| Timer expired but I only triaged 1-2 assets | **The Overshoot:** I fell into perfectionist editing. | Next sprint: Set a 10-minute sub-timer per asset. At the buzzer, move on no matter what. If an asset isn’t ready, flag it—don’t let it steal time from the others. |
| I skipped the sprint this week (or the last two) | **The Abandoned Sprint:** Life happened; the habit broke. | Today: Run a microsprint (see Section 9). 15 minutes, one platform. Rebuild momentum. Then schedule next full sprint for within 3 days. |
| All drafts returned but none feel ready to post | **Triage standard too high:** I’m expecting 100% voice match from first draft. | Re-read the Triage Decision Card. Remind yourself: “Lightly Edit” is success. Most AI drafts need one structural fix, not a rewrite. Lower the bar for “Approve.” |
| I couldn’t find the right prompt or Hub blocks | **Preflight skipped or incomplete.** | Build a permanent Sprint Setup Sheet (Section 2) during preflight so decisions are already made. Never search during the sprint. |
