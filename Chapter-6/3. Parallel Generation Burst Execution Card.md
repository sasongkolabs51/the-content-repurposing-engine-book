## 3. Parallel Generation Burst Execution Card

During the first five minutes of the sprint, follow this sequence. Speed is the goal; you can refine later.

1. **Open your timer.** Start it the moment you begin.
2. **Platform 1 – Twitter:** Navigate to your pre-loaded prompt tab. Paste the assigned Hub blocks at the bottom. Hit submit. Move immediately to the next tab—don’t wait for a response.
3. **Platform 2 – LinkedIn:** Paste assigned Hub blocks. Submit. Move on.
4. **Platform 3 – TikTok/Reels:** Paste assigned Hub blocks. Submit. Move on.
5. **Platform 4 – Newsletter:** Paste assigned Hub blocks. Submit.
6. **Pause.** All requests are now processing. Take a breath. Stretch for 30 seconds. When drafts start returning, you’ll move into triage.

**Timebox goal:** All prompts submitted within 5 minutes of the sprint start.
