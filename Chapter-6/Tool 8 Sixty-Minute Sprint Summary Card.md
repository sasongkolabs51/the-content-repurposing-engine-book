## Tool 8: Sixty-Minute Sprint Summary Card

**The Sprint in Five Lines**

- **Parallel, not sequential.** Feed your Hub into all prompts at once. Review, don’t create.
- **Preflight in two minutes.** Hub ready, prompts loaded, tabs open, timer set. No friction.
- **Triage, don’t perfect.** Approve (≤5 min), Lightly Edit (≤10 min), Flag (≤2 min). Deep polish happens outside the sprint.
- **Protect the rhythm.** Block the sprint on your calendar as a recurring, non‑negotiable event. Pillar‑coupled or weekly batch—pick one and stick.
- **Breakdowns are data.** Prompt drift? Tighten the rule. Overshoot? Shorten the triage timer. Abandoned sprint? Run a micro‑sprint and restart.
