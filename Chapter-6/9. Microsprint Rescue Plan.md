## 9. Microsprint Rescue Plan

When you’ve missed one or more sprints and the thought of a full 60-minute session feels heavy, use this 15-minute version to restart the engine.

**Instructions:** Pick one platform—the one you’re most likely to actually post on today. Open its prompt template, paste the relevant Hub blocks, and generate. Triage the output (Approve or Lightly Edit only). Schedule it. Done.

**Microsprint tracker:**
- [ ] Date: __________________ Platform: __________________ Time taken: _____ minutes
- [ ] Date: __________________ Platform: __________________ Time taken: _____ minutes

Once you’ve done two microsprints in a week, you’re ready to book a full sprint again. The neural pathway is rebuilt.
