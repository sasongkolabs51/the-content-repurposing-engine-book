## Tool 5: Weekly Sprint Rhythm Planner

**Purpose:** To move the sprint from “I’ll do it when I can” to a non‑negotiable calendar block. This planner helps you choose the right rhythm and protect the time.

### Step 1: Choose Your Rhythm

**Pillar‑Coupled Sprint** (best if you publish 1 pillar/week)  
I publish my pillar content on: _________________ (day) at approximately: _________________ (time).  
My sprint will be on the same day, immediately after publishing, or the next morning.  
**Sprint time block:** _________________

**Weekly Batch Sprint** (best if you produce 2+ pillar pieces or prefer batching)  
I’ll block one morning per week as my Content Transformer session.  
**Sprint day & time:** _________________  
**Number of Hubs I’ll process per batch sprint:** _________________

### Step 2: Block It on Your Calendar Now

Go to your calendar and create a recurring event:

- **Event name:** “60‑Minute Multi‑Platform Sprint” (or whatever name motivates you)
- **Duration:** 60 minutes (or 90 if you’re batching multiple Hubs)
- **Repeats:** Weekly
- **Color:** Something bold that signals “creative production”
- **Notifications:** Set a reminder 10 minutes before, so you can complete the preflight checklist without rushing.
