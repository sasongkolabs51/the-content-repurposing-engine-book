## 1. Sprint Preflight Checklist

Run through this list in under three minutes before every sprint. No sprint begins until every box is ticked.

**The Hub**
- [ ] Hub is fully populated (all five blocks complete).
- [ ] Headings match my prompt labels (Core Thesis, Key Stories, etc.).
- [ ] Stories are written as full narrative paragraphs (not just “story about the CEO”).
- [ ] Data points are on separate lines with attribution.
- [ ] Quotable lines are extracted and ready for hooks.

**The Prompt Library**
- [ ] My condensed Voice Constraints block is up to date and copyable.
- [ ] Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok (or Reels), and Newsletter prompt templates are filled in with current Structural Rules and Reference Examples.
- [ ] Each template has a “Hub: [paste blocks]” placeholder at the bottom, ready to receive today’s content.

**The Sprint Environment**
- [ ] I’ve opened my AI tool in separate tabs/windows for each platform (one per prompt template, pre-loaded).
- [ ] My timer is set and visible (phone, browser, or physical timer) for 60 minutes.
- [ ] All unrelated tabs, apps, and notifications are closed or silenced.
- [ ] My scheduling tool or a blank notes document is open for pasting finished assets.

**Mental State**
- [ ] I’ve read the Triage Decision Card (Section 4) and commit to “triage, not perfect.”
- [ ] I’ve set a personal intention for this sprint: e.g., “I will leave with four assets ready to schedule, not four masterpieces.”
