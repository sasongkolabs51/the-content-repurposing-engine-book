## Tool 3: The Editorial Triage Decision Framework

**Purpose:** To stop you from burning thirty minutes on a single draft. This framework gives you a clear, three‑category sorting system and a timebox for each asset. During the sprint, you are a *triage doctor*, not a surgeon.

### The Three Categories

| Category | Criteria | Action | Time Limit |
|----------|----------|--------|------------|
| **Approve** | The draft is 90%+ ready. Voice is on point, structure matches platform rules. Only minor typos or a single word that needs swapping. | Quick read‑through. Fix any glaring typos. Move to the next asset. | **≤ 5 minutes** |
| **Lightly Edit** | The draft is 75–90% there. Hook is solid but one structural element is weak (e.g., missing curiosity gap in tweet 3), or one story needs a more personal touch. | Sharpen the hook, fix the structural issue, or add a personal anecdote. Do not polish every sentence. | **≤ 10 minutes** |
| **Flag for Rewrite** | Below 75%. Voice is off, format is wrong, core thesis is missing or garbled. | Do NOT rewrite now. Note the specific failure (e.g., “hook too generic,” “tweets are multi‑paragraph”) in the Sprint Tracker. Move to the next asset. After the sprint, adjust the prompt and regenerate—or rewrite that single asset outside the timed session. | **≤ 2 minutes to note and move on** |
