## TOOL 6: The Weekly Rhythm Planner

**Purpose:** Choose your sprint rhythm and lock it into your calendar. The sprint only works if it's a non-negotiable appointment, not a "I'll get to it when I have time" aspiration.

**Time needed:** 10 minutes to plan. 1 minute per week to maintain.

### Step 3: The Weekly Prep Ritual (5 minutes, the night before)

Every [day before sprint], I will:

- [ ] Confirm the Hub for tomorrow's sprint is built and AI-ready
- [ ] Fill in the **Parallel Generation Burst Worksheet** (Tool 4) with specific Hub block selections
- [ ] Verify prompt templates are current (no stale Voice Fingerprint or Playbook rules)
- [ ] Set out any physical materials (printed checklist, timer, coffee)
- [ ] Clear my calendar for the sprint block
