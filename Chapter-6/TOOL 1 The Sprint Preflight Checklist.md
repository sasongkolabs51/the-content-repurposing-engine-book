## TOOL 1: The Sprint Preflight Checklist

**Purpose:** Eliminate all setup friction before the timer starts. A sprint that begins with "wait, where's my Hub?" is already broken. This checklist gets you from cold start to "ready to fire" in under two minutes.

**Time needed:** 90 seconds to 2 minutes. Every sprint. No exceptions.

---

### SPRINT PREFLIGHT — [Date] — Hub: [Title]

| # | Check | Done? |
|---|---|---|

| 2 | **Prompt library is open.** All templates up to date with current Voice Fingerprint and Platform Playbook rules. | ☐ |
| 3 | **AI tool tabs are open.** One tab per platform. Prompt template pasted into each. Hub blocks identified and ready to insert. | ☐ |
| 4 | **Timer is set.** 60 minutes. Visible. Not on your phone (you'll pick it up and scroll). Use a desktop timer, a browser extension, or a physical kitchen timer. | ☐ |
| 5 | **Distractions eliminated.** Phone on silent / in another room. Email closed. Slack / DMs muted. "Do Not Disturb" on. | ☐ |
| 6 | **Sprint goal stated.** "In 60 minutes, I will produce: [list platforms]." Written down. Visible. | ☐ |

### The 30-Second Mental Prep

Before you hit start on the timer, read this aloud (or in your head):

> *"I am an editorial director running a production session. My job in the next sixty minutes is to generate, triage, and approve—not to perfect. Done beats perfect. Four good assets beat one flawless asset and three abandoned ones. The timer is my ally. I will not negotiate with it."*
