## 4. Triage Decision Card

Use this card as you receive each draft. Spend no more than **8–10 minutes per asset** on triage. Do not deep-edit now.

| Category | Criteria | Action |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **APPROVE** | 90%+ voice accurate, format correct, hook lands. I’d be comfortable posting with a quick read. | Spend ≤5 minutes: read for glaring errors or voice breaks, make one or two tiny tweaks if needed, then **move to the Approved folder**. |
| **LIGHTLY EDIT** | 75–90% there. Structure is solid but hook needs sharpening, one story missing, or a few off-brand words. | Spend ≤10 minutes: fix the one main structural issue (rewrite hook, swap a sentence, add a personal line). Do not polish every word. Then approve and move on. |
| **FLAG FOR REWRITE** | Below 75%. Voice is generic, format is broken, or core thesis is missed. | Do not rewrite now. Write a brief note: “Hook too generic—needs curiosity gap.” or “Format bleed—reads like a blog.” Move the draft to a “Rewrite” folder. After the sprint, you’ll regenerate with a fixed prompt or rewrite manually. |

**Triage rule I will repeat to myself:** “I am a triage doctor. I stop the bleeding and move to the next patient. Surgery happens later.”
