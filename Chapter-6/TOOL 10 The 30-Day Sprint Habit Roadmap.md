## TOOL 10: The 30-Day Sprint Habit Roadmap

**Purpose:** Make the sprint automatic. Not a heroic effort you summon. A rhythm you fall into. The first four weeks are about building the neural pathway. After that, it's just how you work.

---

### Week 1: Run Your First Sprint (Imperfectly)

- [ ] Choose your rhythm (Tool 6). Lock it into your calendar.
- [ ] Do the **Preflight Checklist** (Tool 1). Set up your environment.
- [ ] Run your **first full sprint.** Use the Phase Tracker (Tool 2). Fill in the Triage Scorecard (Tool 3).
- [ ] Expect it to take 70–80 minutes. That's fine. You're learning the rhythm.
- [ ] Fill in the **Sprint Session Log** (Tool 5). Note what slowed you down.
- [ ] Run the **Post-Sprint Refinement Protocol** (Tool 9) for any flagged assets.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~90 minutes total (sprint + refinement + logging)

### Week 2: Tighten the Triage

- [ ] Run your second sprint. Focus on ONE improvement: **stick to the 8-minute-per-asset triage limit.** Set a subtimer.
- [ ] Use the **Parallel Generation Burst Worksheet** (Tool 4). Pre-fill it the night before.
- [ ] Compare your Session Log to Week 1. Where did you save time? Where did you lose it?
- [ ] If you overshot, use the **Sprint Failure Diagnostic** (Tool 7). Apply the fix.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~75 minutes total

### Week 3: Build Consistency

- [ ] Run your third sprint. You should be hitting 60 minutes (or close).
- [ ] Run a **Microsprint** (Tool 8) mid-week for a secondary piece of content. Prove the system works at small scale too.
- [ ] Update your prompts based on the last two sprints' iteration notes.
- [ ] Do the **Weekly Rhythm Planner** check-in (Tool 6). Is the day/time still working? Adjust if needed.
- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~70 minutes total

### Week 4: Lock In the Habit

- [ ] Run your fourth sprint. Aim for under 60 minutes.
- [ ] Review your **Cumulative Progress table** (Tool 5). Look at the trend line.
- [ ] Run the **Post-Sprint Refinement Protocol** (Tool 9). Confirm all assets are queued.
- [ ] Do a **monthly prompt review** (Chapter 5, Tool 7). Update versions.
- [ ] Write a one-sentence "Sprint Manifesto" and tape it to your monitor:

> "_______________________________________________________________"

*(Examples: "Four assets in sixty minutes. Done beats perfect. The timer is my ally." / "I am an editorial director. I triage, I don't perfect. I fire, I don't fuss.")*

- [ ] **Time commitment:** ~65 minutes total

---

### The 30-Day Proof

At the end of Day 30:

1. Did you run at least 3 full sprints this month?

   ☐ Yes (4+) ☐ Yes (3) ☐ Only 2 ☐ Only 1 ☐ Zero

2. Is your average sprint time under 65 minutes?

   ☐ Under 60 ☐ 60–65 ☐ 65–75 ☐ Still over 75

3. Are you producing 3–4 platform assets per sprint (not just 1–2)?

   ☐ Consistently 4 ☐ Usually 3–4 ☐ Usually 2–3 ☐ Still just 1

4. Has the "dread" of repurposing decreased?

   ☐ Dramatically ☐ Noticeably ☐ Slightly ☐ Not really

5. Are you adding your personal layer to every asset (not just publishing AI output raw)?

   ☐ Always ☐ Usually ☐ Sometimes ☐ Rarely

6. Could you run a sprint tomorrow without re-reading the chapter or the toolkit?

   ☐ Yes, it's muscle memory now ☐ Mostly ☐ I'd need to review ☐ Not yet

> *If you hit 4+ positive answers, the sprint is installed. It's no longer a technique you're "trying." It's how you work. The Three-Hour Repurposing Trap is a memory. You produce more, in less time, with less dread, and the quality is higher because your creative energy goes into editorial direction instead of manual grinding. Chapter 7 is where you connect this engine to your scheduling and distribution tools so the assets flow from sprint to publication without a dozen manual handoffs.*
