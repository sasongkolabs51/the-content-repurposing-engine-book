## 6. Weekly Sprint Rhythm Planner

Commit to a sprint rhythm that fits your content schedule and energy. Block these times in your calendar now.

### Option A: Pillar-Coupled Sprint
*Best for creators who publish one pillar piece per week and want same-day cross-platform presence.*

- My pillar content publishes on: __________________ (e.g., Tuesday morning)
- Sprint time: __________________ (e.g., Tuesday 10:00–11:00 a.m., immediately after publish)
- Hub build time: __________________ (e.g., Monday afternoon, after recording)

**Calendar entry title:** “60-Min Sprint – [Pillar Name]”  
**Protected:** Yes. Treat like a client call.

### Option B: Weekly Batch Sprint
*Best for creators with multiple pillar pieces or who prefer creation days separate from distribution days.*

- Sprint day: __________________ (e.g., Thursday)
- Sprint time: __________________ (e.g., 9:00–10:30 a.m. for 90 minutes)
- Hubs to process this week: __________________ (list 2-3)

**Calendar entry title:** “Weekly Content Transformer Sprint”  
**Protected:** Yes.

**Pledge:** I will not skip my sprint block for non-emergency tasks. If I must miss a week, I’ll run a microsprint (Section 9) within 48 hours.
