## TOOL 5: The Amplification Intensity Decision Tree

**Purpose:** Not every pillar deserves a Five-Day Arc. Not every high-scoring piece needs a 1,200-word essay. This decision tree tells you which amplification level matches the content's potential and your available time.

**Time needed:** 2 minutes per decision.

---

### THE PIECE: _______________

**Matrix scores:** E:___ R:___ S:___ → Weighted score (primary platform): ___ / 3.0

---

### The Decision Tree

**Q1: Is the weighted score ≥ 2.5?**

☐ No → **Standard Sprint or Light Sprint.** (Chapter 6 protocol. No amplification needed.)
☐ Yes → Proceed to Q2.

---

**Q2: Does the content have high Emotional Resonance (R = 3)?**

☐ No → **Standard Sprint + One Long-Form Expansion.**
*(Run the normal sprint. Add ONE newsletter essay or long-form LinkedIn post using the Three-Link Chain. No serialization.)*

☐ Yes → Proceed to Q3.

---

**Q3: Does the content have high Evergreen Strength (E ≥ 2) AND high Shareability (S ≥ 2)?**

☐ No → **Standard Sprint + Long-Form Expansion + One Follow-Up Post.**
*(The content resonates deeply but may not sustain a full five-day arc. Expand it into a rich essay, then publish one follow-up post a week later addressing the top objection.)*

☐ Yes → Proceed to Q4.

---

**Q4: Do I have 90+ minutes this week for an extended amplification session?**

☐ No → **Standard Sprint + Long-Form Expansion.** Save the Five-Day Arc for a week when you have the time.
☐ Yes → **FULL FIVE-DAY ARC.** This piece deserves the full treatment.
