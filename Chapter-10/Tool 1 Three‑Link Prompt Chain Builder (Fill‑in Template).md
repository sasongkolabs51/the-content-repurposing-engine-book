## Tool 1: Three‑Link Prompt Chain Builder (Fill‑in Template)

**Purpose:** To produce a rich, long-form asset (like a 1,200‑word newsletter) by breaking generation into three linked prompts that you steer at each stage—preventing meandering and voice drift.

**Instructions:** Use this template with a Hub that you’ve scored highly for resonance and evergreen strength. Follow the three links in order. At each link, paste the specified Hub blocks, run the AI, and then copy the output as input for the next link. The whole process should take under 30 minutes of AI generation time, plus your micro-edits.

### Link 3: Editorial Deepening (“Yes, And”)

Now you’ll add your personal texture. Read the draft and identify 2–3 paragraphs where a real story, a contrarian thought, or a vulnerable admission would make the piece uniquely yours. Write your raw addition (a sentence or short paragraph) for each. Then use the following prompt for each paragraph.

**Prompt Template (run for each selected paragraph):**

*“Here is an AI‑written paragraph: [paste the paragraph].*

*And here is my personal thought that I want to weave in: [paste your raw addition].*

*Rewrite the paragraph to seamlessly integrate my thought. Maintain the original voice constraints. If my thought challenges the paragraph’s idea, adjust the argument to hold the tension—do not smooth it over.”*
