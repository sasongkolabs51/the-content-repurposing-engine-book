## 5. AI Prompt Templates: Amplification Chains

These are ready‑to‑fill prompts for common amplification tasks. Use them in conjunction with the Planner and Audit.

### 5.1 Long‑Form Newsletter Essay Prompt (Integrated Three‑Link as a Single Prompt)

If you prefer a single large prompt rather than a staged chain, use this. It compresses the three links but includes internal steps for the AI.

```
You are my newsletter co‑writer. Your task is to produce a complete 1,000‑word essay from my Hub, but you will do it in three internal stages. Do NOT show me the intermediate stages—just the final essay—but follow this process internally:

[PASTE YOUR VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK]

STAGE 1 (internal): Create a narrative outline with a personal opening, a deepened thesis section with data and a second story, an objection‑handling section, and a reflective closing.

STAGE 2 (internal): Expand the outline into a full draft, applying my voice constraints exactly. Where the outline calls for a personal anecdote, invent a placeholder that fits my tone.

STAGE 3 (internal): Read your draft and ask: “Where could a contrarian opinion or an unexpected insight add depth?” Add at least one such moment. Then polish for flow.

OUTPUT: The subject line (prefixed “Subject: ”) followed by the final essay. No outline, no notes—just the finished piece.

Hub:
[PASTE YOUR FULL HUB]
```

Note: The single‑prompt approach works well for many, but if you want more control over each stage, use the separate Link prompts in Section 1.

### 5.2 Serialization Day Prompt Template (Fill‑in for Any Day)

Use this as a base prompt for any day in the Five‑Day Arc. Fill in the bracketed specifics from your Planner.

```
You are my editorial assistant. Write in my exact voice for [DAY X] of a 5‑day content series.

[PASTE YOUR VOICE CONSTRAINTS BLOCK]

TODAY’S ROLE: [e.g., The Story – personal narrative that grounds the insight]
HOOK ARCHITECTURE TO USE: [e.g., Vulnerability Ledger]
CONTENT TYPE TO FORCE: [Analytical / Personal / Contrarian] – override the auto‑detection to match the day’s role.

STRUCTURAL RULES FOR THIS PIECE:
- Platform: [e.g., LinkedIn Carousel]
- [Insert format rules: slide count, length, etc.]
- The emotional register must be: [e.g., vulnerable and warm, but not self‑indulgent]
- End with: [e.g., a cliffhanger that teases Day 3’s framework]

HUB BLOCKS TO USE: [List specific blocks]

REFERENCE EXAMPLE: [Paste a similar post of yours that matches the intended tone and platform]

Hub:
[PASTE RELEVANT BLOCKS]
```

Generate each day’s piece using this template, adjusting the role, hook, and emotional register each time.

### 5.3 Voice Deepening “Yes, And” Prompt (Reusable)

Use this whenever you have an AI paragraph and a personal addition.

```
Here is a paragraph from an AI draft:
[PASTE PARAGRAPH]

Here is my additional thought I want to integrate without breaking voice or flow:
[PASTE YOUR PERSONAL SENTENCE]

Rewrite the paragraph to incorporate my thought seamlessly. If my thought contradicts the original point, preserve the tension—don’t erase it. Maintain all original voice constraints.
```
