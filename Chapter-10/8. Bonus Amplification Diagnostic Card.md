## 8. Bonus: Amplification Diagnostic Card

When a long‑form or serialized piece feels “off,” use this quick reference.

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Essay feels like a blog post stretched out, not a deeper argument | Link 1 outline was too high‑level; AI didn’t have enough structure | Next time, demand more detail in the outline: specify the argument progression, not just section labels |
| Day 2 (Story) and Day 3 (Framework) sound identical in tone | Conditional logic not overridden; AI applied same tone to both | Use the Serialization Day Prompt Template and explicitly force “Personal” for Day 2, “Analytical” for Day 3 |
| Voice becomes formal and stiff in the second half of a long essay | AI formality drift in longer outputs | Add a “Long‑Form Rule” to your Voice Constraints: “In outputs > 500 words, increase contractions and direct address by 20%.” |
| Series feels repetitive by Day 4 | AI is reusing the same quotes and stories across days | In each day’s prompt, specify exactly which Hub blocks to use, and forbid reuse of the same story unless it’s explicitly a callback. |
| “Yes, and” additions feel pasted in, not integrated | The integration prompt wasn’t strong enough | Use the Voice Deepening Prompt (5.3) and explicitly ask the AI to “rewrite to incorporate my thought seamlessly” rather than just appending it. |
