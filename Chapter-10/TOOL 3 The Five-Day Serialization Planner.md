## TOOL 3: The Five-Day Serialization Planner

**Purpose:** Design the full narrative arc for a serialized content experience. One pillar. Five days. Five distinct emotional functions. Five platforms/formats. One cumulative journey that turns casual readers into devoted subscribers.

**Time needed:** 20 minutes to plan. Then 60–90 minutes to generate all five pieces (can be batched in one extended sprint or spread across the week).

---

### The Arc Coherence Check

Before generating, verify:

☐ Each day has a DISTINCT emotional function (not the same note five times)
☐ The hook patterns VARY across the five days (not the same pattern repeated)
☐ The content types shift appropriately (Personal → Analytical → Contrarian → Personal)
☐ Day 1 does NOT solve anything (it's a teaser, not a summary)
☐ Day 5 does NOT repeat Day 1 (it's a reflection, not a restatement)
☐ The arc builds CUMULATIVELY (each day adds something the previous didn't)
☐ A reader who only sees Day 3 still gets value (each piece stands alone)
☐ A reader who sees all five feels they went on a JOURNEY

**The one-sentence description of this arc's journey:**

"From _______________ to _______________."

*(Example: "From 'I'm broken because I can't decide' to 'I'm not broken—I'm just spending my decision budget on the wrong things.'")*
