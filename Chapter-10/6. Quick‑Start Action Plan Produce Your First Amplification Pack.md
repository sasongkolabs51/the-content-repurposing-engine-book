## 6. Quick‑Start Action Plan: Produce Your First Amplification Pack

Set aside a 2‑hour block to create a full editorial amplification from one high‑scoring pillar.

- [ ] **0–5 min:** Select a pillar that your Decision Matrix scored above 2.5 for your primary platform. Open its Hub.
- [ ] **5–20 min:** Complete the Five‑Day Content Arc Planner (Section 3) for that pillar. Decide on platforms for each day and the specific hook for each.
- [ ] **20–25 min:** Choose your amplification format. For this first run, pick ONE of the following: (a) 1,000‑word newsletter essay using the Three‑Link Chain (Section 1), OR (b) Days 1, 3, and 5 of the serialized arc using the Serialization Day Prompt Template (Section 5.2).
- [ ] **25–80 min:** Generate the pieces. If doing the newsletter essay, work through Links 1, 2, and 3. If doing the arc, generate the three selected days. Use the “Yes, And” technique on at least two paragraphs.
- [ ] **80–95 min:** Run the Voice Drift Audit (Section 4) on the completed pieces. Fix any drift by adjusting the relevant prompt and regenerating.
- [ ] **95–110 min:** Do a final read‑through for typos and flow. Paste the pieces into your Hub’s Spoke sections and schedule them via your unified workflow.
- [ ] **110–120 min:** Reflect. Write one sentence: “The biggest thing I learned about scaling my voice without losing it was __________.”
