## Tool 7: Voice Amplification Summary Card & Mental Rehearsal Script

### Summary Card: Scale Without Losing Soul

- **Chain prompts, don’t cram them.** Outline → Draft → Deepen. Insert editorial judgment at each link.
- **“Yes, and” the AI.** Accept its draft as an offer, add your unique perspective, and let the AI integrate it. You’re not correcting; you’re cocreating.
- **Serialize with an arc.** Hook → Story → Framework → Objection → Reflection. Each day earns the next.
- **Audit for voice drift.** Check hook openings, lexical fingerprints, and emotional register across all pieces in a series. Fix drifts by tightening prompts, not by rewriting.
- **Scale is the test of authenticity.** If your voice stays consistent from a single tweet to a five‑day arc, you’ve proven that AI amplifies who you are, not dilutes it.

### Mental Rehearsal Script

*I’m no longer repurposing; I’m orchestrating an editorial experience. One powerful idea can become a weeklong journey for my audience—and my voice will guide them through every step.*

*With prompt chaining, I don’t ask the AI to write a long piece from nothing. I build it layer by layer: an outline, then a draft, then my personal additions woven in at exactly the right places. The result isn’t longer—it’s deeper. And it’s still me.*

*When the AI gives me a draft, I don’t put on my critic hat. I put on my collaborator hat. I find the most interesting kernel, I add my lived experience, and we rewrite it together. That’s “yes, and.” It turns editing from a chore into a creative conversation.*

*I have a template for a five‑day arc: Hook, Story, Framework, Objection, Reflection. I can batch‑generate the entire arc, then run a quick voice drift audit to make sure the AI didn’t slip. If Day 5 feels slightly formal, I tighten the prompt and regenerate. I don’t settle.*

*Scaling doesn’t mean sounding generic. It means proving that my voice is so well‑defined, so well‑encoded, that it can fill a newsletter, a slide deck, and a week of posts without faltering. I am not a content factory. I am an editorial architect, and I build experiences that feel personal at any volume.*

*Let’s amplify.*
