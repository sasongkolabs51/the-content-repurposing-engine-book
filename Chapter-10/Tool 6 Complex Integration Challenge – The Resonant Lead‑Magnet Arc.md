## Tool 6: Complex Integration Challenge – The Resonant Lead‑Magnet Arc

**Purpose:** To practice the highest‑level skill from this chapter: adapting a deeply personal pillar into a five‑day series that also serves subtle business goals—without breaking trust. This is the integration of editorial depth, voice amplification, and strategy.

**Scenario:** Your pillar is a personal story of failure that led to a professional breakthrough (e.g., a project that bombed, which taught you a system you now coach others on). The matrix scores it: Evergreen 3, Resonance 3, Shareability 2. You want to amplify it into a five‑day arc, and you also want the series to function as a soft lead magnet for your coaching program. You must never sound like you’re selling from a place of pain.

### Step 4: Pre‑Publish Trust Check

Before scheduling, ask yourself:

- If a reader encounters Day 3 first (without seeing Days 1–2), would the CTA still feel earned? If not, adjust.
- Would I feel comfortable sending this to a close friend who’s struggling with the same topic? If it feels manipulative even a little, revise the CTA language.
- Does the overall arc give more than it asks? Count the ratio: _____ pieces of pure value to _____ subtle CTAs. A 4:1 or 5:1 ratio is safe.
