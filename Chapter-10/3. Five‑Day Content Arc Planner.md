## 3. Five‑Day Content Arc Planner

**Purpose:** Transform a single pillar into a serialized editorial experience that builds cumulative engagement across a full week.

**Instructions:** Choose a pillar that your Decision Matrix scored highly on Resonance and Shareability. Fill in this planner before generating any content.

**Pillar title:** __________________  
**Arc start date:** __________________

| Day | Platform(s) | Role in Arc | Hook Architecture (from Ch. 8) | Hub Blocks to Use | Special Notes |
|-----|-------------|-------------|--------------------------------|-------------------|---------------|
| 1 | Twitter or LinkedIn (short post) | The Hook – introduce the problem; create a curiosity gap | Bold Reframe or Specificity Bomb | Core Thesis, Quotable Line | End with a cliffhanger: “Tomorrow I’ll share the exact moment it all shifted.” |
| 2 | Newsletter or LinkedIn Carousel | The Story – personal narrative that grounds the insight | Vulnerability Ledger or Confession Opener | Key Stories (Story #1), Audience Objections (implied) | This is the vulnerability beat. Use a real personal detail; AI can draft structure, but you “yes, and” the emotional truth. |
| 3 | Twitter Thread or LinkedIn Carousel | The Framework – deliver the instructional core | Insight‑First Headline | Core Thesis, Data Points, Key Stories (Story #2 if applicable) | Make the steps feel earned after Day 2’s emotional setup. |
| 4 | Twitter or LinkedIn Text Post | The Objection – address the strongest pushback | Contrarian Cliffhanger or Diagnostic Question | Audience Objections block | Show intellectual honesty. Begin with “But wait—” or “The most common pushback…” |
| 5 | Newsletter or Long‑Form LinkedIn Post | The Reflection – look back, share what’s changed, invite conversation | Curiosity Gap or Personal Time‑Stamp (subject line) | Whole Hub, but add new reflection on the idea’s journey since you first published it | Use a reflective, open‑ended tone. Ask a direct question to encourage replies. |

**Post‑plan prompt generation tip:** Use the magnetic prompt templates from Chapter 8, but for each day, explicitly override the content type detection to match the day’s role. For example, on Day 2, force “Personal” mode regardless of the Hub’s overall tone, to ensure the emotional arc is fully present.

**Sprint adaptation:** You can batch‑generate all five days in a single extended sprint. Load the Hub, and for each day, fire a separate prompt with the specific constraints from this planner. Triage and lightly edit all five in one session. Use the unified workflow to schedule them across the week.
