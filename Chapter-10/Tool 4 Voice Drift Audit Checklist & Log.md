## Tool 4: Voice Drift Audit Checklist & Log

**Purpose:** To ensure that across multiple pieces in an amplification series, your voice remains consistent—catching the subtle drift toward formality or genericism before you hit publish.

**Instructions:** After generating all pieces for a series (or a batch sprint), pull them into a single document. Read them in sequence. For each piece, check the three markers below. If a piece triggers a “Yes” on any drift indicator, log it and apply a targeted prompt fix before regenerating that piece.

### Voice Drift Indicators

| Marker | What to Check | Drift Indicator (If Yes, Drift Detected) |
|--------|---------------|-----------------------------------------|
| **Hook Architecture** | Does the opening follow the specified hook pattern exactly? | Opening is a rhetorical question, a universal statement (“We’ve all been there…”), or a generic stat. |
| **Lexical Consistency** | Are my signature phrases present? Are contractions used? Any banned corporate words? | Signature phrase missing; formality increased (e.g., “do not” instead of “don’t”); a banned word like “leverage” appeared. |
| **Emotional Register** | Does the tone match the content type? Is the warmth/vulnerability/contrarian edge where it should be? | The reflective piece sounds like an instruction manual; the contrarian piece is too polite; the story piece lacks concrete emotional details. |

**Pattern Insights:**  
If the same drift appears across multiple pieces, you have a systemic prompt gap. Create a “Long‑Form Voice Reinforcement” rule to add to your templates. For example:

*“In any output longer than 300 words, increase contractions by 30% and ensure at least one self‑deprecating comment or personal aside appears.”*

Add that rule to your Voice Fingerprint block or directly into the prompt template for newsletter/long‑form.
