## Tool 3: The Five‑Day Serialization Arc Planner

**Purpose:** To map a single high‑scoring pillar into a weeklong editorial series that builds narrative momentum and keeps your audience returning. Fill in this planner before generating any content, then use it to batch-produce the entire arc in one extended sprint.

**Instructions:** Choose a pillar that scores high on resonance and evergreen. For each day, define the platform, content type, hook strategy, Hub blocks to draw from, and the emotional job that day’s piece should perform.

### Day‑Specific Prompt Snippets

Use these starter instructions inside your full prompt templates.

**Day 1 Hook Tweet (Twitter):**  
“Generate a single hook tweet (under 140 chars) that introduces the core problem from the Hub’s Core Thesis. Use the Contrarian Cliffhanger pattern. Do NOT offer the solution. End with an open loop.”

**Day 2 Story Carousel (LinkedIn):**  
“Using Key Story #1, create a 7‑slide carousel that tells the personal story as a narrative arc (struggle → insight → turning point). Slide 1 title: a single line that captures the emotional kernel. Keep tone vulnerable. No frameworks, no bullet points.”

**Day 3 Framework Thread (Twitter):**  
“Take the Core Thesis and Data Points. Generate a 7‑tweet thread that teaches the framework in numbered steps. Each tweet delivers one step + a mini‑example. Use the Insight‑First Headline hook. End tweet 7 with a reflective question.”

**Day 4 Objection Post (LinkedIn):**  
“Use the Audience Objection block. Write a LinkedIn text post that begins: ‘The most common pushback I get on [idea] is…’ Then present the objection fairly, followed by my response. Tone: confident but not combative. End by asking readers if they’ve experienced this tension.”

**Day 5 Reflection Newsletter:**  
“Using the Core Thesis and Key Story #2, write a 600‑word newsletter that reflects on the journey of the idea. Open with a quiet moment of realization or a line that shows how my thinking has evolved. End with: ‘I’d love to hear—what’s a belief you once held that you’ve since re‑examined? Hit reply.’”
